Forbidden Words in Live Streams

1. Words related to "most," such as 最 (most), 最佳 (best), 最具 (most suitable), 最爱 (most loved), 最赚 (most profitable), 最优 (best), 最优秀 (most excellent), etc.;

2. Words related to "first," such as 中国第一 (China's number one), 全网第一 (number one across the web), 销量第一 (number one in sales), NO.1, TOP.1, etc.;

3. Words indicating authority, such as 国家XXX领导人/机关推荐 (national XXX leader / recommended by an agency), 质量免检 (quality exempt from inspection), 老字号 (well-known brand), 中国驰名商标 (China's famous trademark), 特供 (special supply), 专供 (exclusive supply), etc.;

4. Words related to "first," "home," or "country," such as 首个 (first), 首选 (preferred), 全国首家 (first in the country), 全网首发 (first on the web), 独家 (exclusive), 全国销量冠军 (national sales champion), etc.;

5. Words containing "level" or "extreme," such as 国家级 (national level, except when issued by relevant units), 全球级 (global level), 顶级 (top level), etc.;

6. Words related to brand status, such as 王牌 (top brand), 领袖品牌 (leading brand), 世界领先 (world-leading), 遥遥领先 (way ahead), etc.;

7. Words indicating absolute or extreme claims that cannot be verified, such as 绝无仅有 (unique), 史无前例 (unprecedented), 万能 (all-powerful), 100%, etc.;

8. Expressions that涉嫌欺诈消费者 (suspect to deceive consumers), such as 点击领奖 (click to claim prize), 恭喜获奖 (congratulations on winning), 全民免单 (everyone gets a freebie), 点击有惊喜 (click for a surprise), etc.;

9. Words related to false content, such as 史无前例 (unprecedented), 前无古人 (no one before), 永久 (permanent), 万能 (all-powerful), 祖传 (passed down through generations), 特效 (special effect), 无敌 (invincible), etc.;

10. Discussions about 刷单 (brushing orders), certification of 黄v (verified gold), 白v (verified white), 灰v (verified gray);

11. Exaggerated claims about effects, such as 超级美白 (super whitening), 超级补水 (super hydration), 非常抗皱 (very anti-wrinkle);

12. References to religious beliefs, such as 天主 (God), 耶稣 (Jesus), 神 (god), 上帝 (God), and the appearance of related language or physical objects;

13. Discussions about national laws, regulations, or politically sensitive topics related to the U.S., Japan, Hong Kong, and Taiwan;

14. Organizing, promoting, or inducing users to join传销 (pyramid scheme) or organizations suspected of传销;

15. Long discussions about 导流 (traffic diversion), 引流 (traffic引流), or third-party platforms like 抖音 (Douyin) and 快手 (Kuaishou);

16. Inappropriate display of national authority emblems or badges, such as 国旗 (national flag), 国徽 (national emblem), 警徽 (police badge), etc.;

17. Infringing on legal rights, such as showing someone's portrait, brand logos, or infringing on reputation;

18. Infringing on others' privacy, such as leaking偷拍偷录 (stolen recordings), publishing others' names, contact information, or chat records;

19. Broadcasting or leaking others' intellectual property content or commercial secrets.

20. Content related to investment and financing, such as stock recommendations, online lending, promotion of financial products, paid securities or futures consulting;

21. Violent and terrorist imagery, such as animal abuse, killing animals, beating or violent threats against others;

22. Fake eating, eating with vomiting, promoting overeating, binge eating, or excessive consumption;

23. Showing the use of (simulated) knives, guns, or performing dangerous actions;

24. Publishing news or political information without proper资质 review;

25. Publishing fundraising or charitable content without proper资质 review;

26. Religious or superstitious content;

27. Promoting medical treatments, drugs, medical devices, pesticides, veterinary drugs, health foods, tobacco, or adult products without proper资质 review;

28. Explaining or showcasing online games without the required cultural administration content review approval number or filing number;

29. Promoting third-party platform services without permission, such as QR codes or external links from other platforms;

30. Guiding offline transactions, redirecting to transactions on other platforms or through bank cards;
