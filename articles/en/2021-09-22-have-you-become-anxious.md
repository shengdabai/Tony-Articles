# Have You Become Anxious?

> Published: 2021-09-22 · [中文](../zh/2021-09-22-%E4%BD%A0%E7%84%A6%E8%99%91%E4%BA%86%E5%90%97.md) | [English](../en/2021-09-22-have-you-become-anxious.md)

---

Anxiety is the most common state for modern people. It's aagitated emotion caused by excessive worry about the safety of loved ones or oneself, or about one's future and fate. Two key points to focus on: it's related to oneself, and it's an emotion caused by worry.

When did the word "anxiety" become our everyday state? With the accelerated pace of modern society, we seem to have to do a lot of things, take on multiple roles, and shoulder many responsibilities — which is normal. Once something becomes too much, it naturally leads to corresponding emotions, and anxiety follows. Yet most people feel helpless in the face of it.

Most people think anxiety is the right response. After all, we often use the phrase "pressure creates motivation" to deal with everything. But the question is, is this really accepting anxiety, or are we just passively waiting?

From my perspective, anxiety boils down to one character: greed! It's because of greed!

I want to stay healthy, I want to read widely, I want to walk every piece of land in my motherland, and I also want to...

Looking at what happens every day, I want to finish reading this book. Suddenly I remember someone is live-streaming, I watch the live stream, a friend sends me a private message, then I go do something else...

I feel like I've done a lot, but my attention is scattered. Yet most of the time, we only think we've spent some time, and never mention the attention we've lost — which is a big harm.

We've identified the cause of anxiety. What should we do next? Also one character: throw it away!

Delete unnecessary apps and so-called resources, cancel ineffective social connections. Once you have the idea to do something else, first check your daily plan, give yourself some space, don't push yourself too hard.

Recently, I've been adjusting my writing and video content. My work is still being refined. I want to focus on conveying my perceptions. Maybe there will be a piece tomorrow, or a week from now, or even a month — it's hard to say. But what I can do is not be anxious and take it slowly.

Recently, I've seen many friends growing, their works getting better and better. I'm happy for them, but I also feel a bit anxious myself. Thinking about it, it's still my own ability that's lacking. I should keep refining my work and carefully cultivate it.

Finally, I'll share a picture with you, hoping it's helpful. Good night 😊
