Note 90

001 The Best State Is Obsession

The best state for creating a product is when the founder is fully passionate about it. When Shi Yuzhu was making *Zhengtu*, his state was being completely immersed in the game. He would often joke that he was always exercising and riding horses in the game.

002 Careful Selection

Brain White Gold was selected from over 30 health products. The key was its effectiveness, especially in improving sleep and digestive issues, which perfectly met the needs of the elderly.

003 Trial and Iteration

If you have new ideas, you can first test them in a small market. When Brain White Gold was first launched, the team abandoned traditional media for promotion and instead focused on soft articles as a breakthrough, building reputation through word-of-mouth. Users trust people they know.

004 Make It Available

Once a product has a good reputation, it's still not enough—it must be accessible to consumers. The Brain White Gold team created a marketing manual to standardize the process.

005 Advocate McDonald's

Shi Yuzhu advocates McDonald's approach: the headquarters handles R&D, sets standards and processes, and the branches just follow procedures. The headquarters must personally conduct market research, evaluate results, and then create a standard before rolling it out nationwide.

006 Humanity Over Wolf Spirit

Reward appropriately. When Shi Yuzhu emphasized wolf spirit, he specifically mentioned giving a 50% raise to the researchers on the front lines. Before a boss talks about wolf spirit, they should first show humanity.

007 Break Before You Build

There are rumors that Shi Yuzhu implemented a "recruitment" sales strategy. He found a group of people to play *Legend* every day. Their task was simple: find other players, chat, and say, *Zhengtu* is really fun, go play it.

008 Cut When Necessary

Shi Yuzhu once worked on over twenty projects at the same time, trying to keep them all, but none survived. When you realize a project or product is going the wrong way and the team can't keep up, and if the next stage has no hope, don't care about the loss—cut it quickly. Cut when necessary.

009 Control Risk

Go slower, but never let the cash flow break. Before the collapse of Jiaren Mansion, Shi Yuzhu's company had extremely high debt. Later, he learned from the experience, and both Brain White Gold and *Zhengtu* had no debt.

010 Obsess Over Improvement

You can never change others; you can only change yourself. You change others by changing yourself.
