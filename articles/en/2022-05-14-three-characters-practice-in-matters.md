Note 42

Practice in Matters #Plain Talk# #Little Secret#

01 A user asked Li Ma Song teacher how to systematically learn marketing knowledge. Li Ma Song teacher said that there's no need to systematically learn marketing; what you need is to improve your business capabilities seriously.

02 This gave me a lot of inspiration. Some users often ask me how to systematically learn about WeChat Channels. Do they need to sign up for courses, or should they read certain books? Actually, practical experience is more valuable.

03 Marketing is about making sales unnecessary, optimizing through every detail. WeChat Channels is a huge billboard; your core isn't the WeChat Channels itself, but what you present through it?

04 For example, with the business I currently collaborate on, when I consult with users, I pay attention to whether they have solid foundations and how well their original business is performing. If they lack sufficient content, I suggest they first focus on building that base.

05 Throughout the learning process, what I care about is "practice." Li Xiaolai teacher has a saying I absolutely agree with: there's no content you can't learn, as long as you want to learn. And "practice" means a lot of repetition, constant refinement—this process is even more crucial.

06 My role is that of a coach and consultant, with the core goal of solving problems. I use my practical experience and capabilities to help users perform better. WeChat Channels is just a format; you can still do well without it.

07 First, put aside the idea of learning something. Let's take the three characters Wang Yangming gave to the world: "Practice in Matters." Let's all strive together!

I've finished writing. Thank you for being someone who can accomplish things!
