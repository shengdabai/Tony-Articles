Note 13

01 Carnegie Training is a professional and systematic learning program; you can look into it. The textbooks used are not unfamiliar to you: *The Weakness of Human Nature* and *The Strength of Human Nature* (translation may not be accurate).

02 With the guidance of a professional teacher from Dedao, these two books—one focusing on interpersonal relationships and the other on managing personal stress and emotions—can help you become a better individual.

03 At first, I was curious as to why companies would arrange for large-scale Carnegie Training. Isn’t it more suitable for individuals? In fact, as long as there are people, regardless of the organizational form, it applies.

04 When you learn and can appropriately practice and apply it, you and those around you will change. I call this the "money-attracting individual."

05 Business is an exchange process. When you become a better version of yourself, in other words, you become more marketable.

06 For example, Mr. Buffett highly recommends Carnegie Training. Through systematic training, you will undergo some degree of transformation.

07 If you can't participate in professional training for now, start reading these two books and practice them repeatedly. Listen to the advice from those around you, and you'll be surprised at what you discover.
