Common monetization methods for WeChat Channels:

1. Skill-based monetization - Paid communities, knowledge-based payments
2. Advertising monetization
3. Personal IP monetization - Paid communities, knowledge-based payments
4. Product promotion, brand live streaming
5. Shopping guide monetization
6. Live streaming tip monetization

In the context of my field, English education, the monetization path is relatively clear.

English education account:

1. Video sharing: English short phrases, vocabulary memorization, addressing pain points in English learning, leaving a WeChat Official Account link in the video description, guiding users to scan and contact customer service via WeChat or add customer service via WeChat Channels private message. Customer service provides one-on-one service, free resource packages, trial classes, and then guides users to sign up for courses. The courses are recorded, and a class group is established for post-class support. This is an online education model.

2. Live streaming for traffic acquisition: The WeChat Channels influencer teaches live, team teachers teach, or assistants or customer service staff answer questions live. Usually, the live stream is themed around free English classes, with interaction during the class and live calls to correct pronunciation. The focus is on spoken English issues and long-term learning difficulties such as grammar and vocabulary. Other language learning skills are rarely covered. The goal is to guide users to purchase courses directly in the live stream. If they have questions, they can contact customer service and add WeChat to enter the owned audience, and later convert through one-on-one communication orWeChat Moments.

Case analysis: Da Gao English

1. Company background: Good Marks is an education technology company based on short videos, currently leading in the English category on Kuaishou and Xiaohongshu. Since its establishment, it has been committed to promoting educational resource equity, allowing children in fourth- and fifth-tier cities to access quality education. The co-founders include experienced overseas return English teachers, seasoned entrepreneurs, and a product manager once favored by Xu Xiaoping.

2. Related data:

- Douyin: 3.093 million followers, 12.447 million likes, 1,329 posts
- Kuaishou: 3.273 million followers, 2,097 posts
- Xiaohongshu: 1.495 million followers, 5.532 million likes and saves
- WeChat Channels: Over 1 million followers, average likes per post 4,021.6, 13 posts with over 100,000 likes

3. Viral content:

"Graduated from Nanjing University with a bachelor's degree, I've been live-streaming free English lessons every day. I hope my efforts can help more ordinary families and children avoid unnecessary detours on the path of learning English." The main content is four sentences: graduated with honors from Nanjing University, master's degree from the University of New South Wales in Sydney (background advantage), 8 years of experience in English education (professional ability), and currently over 8 million people are following me to learn English (empathy). Free live streams allow ordinary families' children to access quality educational resources (target audience). The content tells users, "I am a renowned teacher," "I have experience and capability," and "I want to help you for free." The slogan appeals to the public's desire for a good deal, especially users with limited purchasing power who are looking for cost-effective solutions. It's easy to be swayed. The video format is the influencer's self-disclosure, making it more relatable and approachable.

Other posts with over 100,000 likes are similar in content. Upon observation, it's clear that most of these posts are not related to academic knowledge. This highlights the diversity of content creation on WeChat Channels, even including emotional appeals. Although it's a form of hype, it's evident that the creator is genuinely trying to engage the audience and achieving the desired effect. This type of video format can be referenced, but it's recommended to approach it with a critical mindset. Overdoing it can backfire and damage trust with followers.

4. Conversion process

The comment section under the video links users to how to listen to free live streams and trial classes. Clicking the link takes them to the official WeChat account GoodMarks, where they scan to add the company's WeChat. They then receive a free trial class, followed by the paid course.

Live streams occur at 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM daily. The content is simple and easy to understand, with the theme indicating the target audience of 4 to 70 years old. Live stream recordings require adding the personal WeChat. The small shop sells a 365 yuan premium course book. Monetization is achieved through the small shop, and learning gift boxes from various institutions are also sold for distribution monetization.

The link page isn't just about course descriptions and content; it starts with student success case studies, then features the self-disclosure video of the teacher, followed by parent reviews, and ends with a WeChat ID. This step-by-step approach enhances user willingness and trust, and the structure is reusable. The live stream times of 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM can reach most users, but since the live stream teacher isn't the influencer, it may lead to user attrition.
