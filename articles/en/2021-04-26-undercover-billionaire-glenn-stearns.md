**The 8-part documentary tells the story of this entrepreneurial journey. In the end, Glenn created a business worth $750,000.**

Background Introduction

The protagonist, Glenn, is an American by birth and a controversial figure. According to his own account, he was born into a low-income family, his parents were alcoholics, and the family income was not high. Glenn was diagnosed with dyslexia at a young age and became a father at 14. Fortunately, Glenn did not succumb to his background or his inherent disadvantages. He worked hard and eventually went to college, becoming the first college graduate in his family. At the age of 25, Glenn founded a company that provided mortgage services, thus beginning his path of life transformation.

Entrepreneurship always unfolds in a specific environment. The most important element in entrepreneurship is the market and the customer. All entrepreneurship and business must be rooted in the market and customers, so it is impossible to discuss entrepreneurship without considering the market context. Erie, the city where Glenn started his journey, is a small coastal city in Pennsylvania. How small is this city? Its total population is about 100,000. If you watch this documentary, you will find that this city has very typical remnants of industrialization. In today's information age, this city is somewhat outdated and has fallen behind the times. You can feel that the people there are not particularly wealthy. After 90 days of hiding his identity in Erie, when Glenn revealed his real identity to his team and rewarded them with money, he gave his team up to $25,000. The two people who received $25,000 were so moved they were close to tears, as if they had never seen so much money in their lives. In reality, $25,000 is about 180,000 yuan in RMB, which is not that much. Therefore, the main theme of this small city is to revive. Understanding this is particularly important. Later, when Glenn started his entrepreneurial project, he did not just think of what direction to take. Glenn carefully observed and analyzed the local environment and chose the correct entrepreneurial direction. This decision was not made on a whim, even though it was not explicitly stated in the dialogue or in the logical flow. However, it was crucial in Glenn's entrepreneurship, and we will discuss it in detail later.
