Note 84

DR Diamond Ring - A Ring for One Person Only

001 The Power of Social Proof

Diamonds are forever, one diamond is passed down through generations. No matter what the intrinsic value is, social proof amplifies its value, and it has become a symbol of love.

002 Playing with New Heights

DR Diamond Ring aims to become a global leader in the culture of true love, encouraging users to buy diamond rings and imbuing the concept of true love through a series of innovative approaches.

003 Setting the Label First

Purchasing a DR Diamond Ring means the man is committing to buy this unique diamond ring with his ID, representing a lifelong promise of love, loving only one person for a lifetime. This label is tied to the purchasing behavior.

004 Letting Best Friends Be the Ambassadors

Neither the buyer nor the recipient of the diamond ring is the best ambassador; it is the woman’s best friend who truly understands the meaning behind DR, and thus can spread the brand more effectively.

005 The Arrogant Agreement

When purchasing a diamond ring, you must sign a "True Love Agreement," which includes phrases like "I will love you only one person for a lifetime" and "I will only give this ring to one person." Both the promise-maker and the promise-receiver must sign, along with a true love code and the signing date. This serves as proof that the promise of true love remains unchanged for a lifetime.

006 Creating Scarcity Through Ceremony

Scarcity is a powerful persuasion tool. DR has its own unique purchasing process, rich in ceremony, which allows users to deeply feel the sense of scarcity and enhances their admiration for the brand.

0.07 Capitalizing on Loss Aversion

Once a user has experienced this unique ring and it has been imbued with personal meaning, the psychological effect of loss aversion makes it extremely difficult for them to resist purchasing it, leaving them with no reason to say no.

008 Controversial Yet Unique

Users can only buy one diamond ring with their personal information, which has led to various criticisms and opposing arguments. However, DR Diamond Ring continues to maintain its original characteristics and persists in its marketing strategy and brand positioning.

009 Prominent Brand Identity

Occupying users' mental space, DR's brand logo and advertisements are easily seen in various public places, continuously spreading and shaping its value.

010 Online Viral Spread

DR's online promotion is also quite successful. The promotion on new media platforms like Xiaohongshu has led to a continuous rise in brand premium and increasing profits.
