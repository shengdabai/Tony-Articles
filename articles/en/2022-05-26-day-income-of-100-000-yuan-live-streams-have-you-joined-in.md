Note No. 50

Day-income of 100,000 yuan, do you feel envious? #LiveStreaming #CaseStudy#

01 A certain live streamer broke through the 100,000 yuan income threshold in a single day, and I just want to ask, is this sustainable?

02 You might think I'm picking on them, or maybe you'd like to challenge me by asking, have you done this yourself? I don't deny their strength, but I hope you have an objective understanding of it.

03 Live streaming is a form of presentation. If this were a large-scale sales event, the same results could be achieved. What matters is what was done before the live stream.

04 A live stream, from planning to execution, every detail and various possibilities need to be anticipated. For example, take a project I managed—externally, it looked like a million-yuan project, but the entire project lasted for over a fortnight. The live stream was the means by which we made our name and marked a milestone.

05 For us, sustainable performance is more valuable. For instance, can you achieve a daily income of 100 yuan? As a new live streamer, it's definitely possible. Gradually moving toward daily income of thousands and monthly income, taking it step by step, is the way to go.

06 Live streaming commercialization is a trend on the platform. The reason is simple—live streaming for selling products and courses brings in revenue for the platform, which gives it the ability to attract advertisers and brand partners. That's why you'll see more and more big brands entering WeChat Channels.

07 What I envy is your current courage. Make a decision, commit to it, and keep going (´-ω-`)

I've finished writing. Thank you for being the one who makes things happen!
