01 Small Tea Can is 10 years old today, and there's a live stream on WeChat Channels tonight. My feelings are eight words: tears well up, full of anticipation.

02 The live stream starts with a wall full of skepticism, then tells Du Guoying's entrepreneurial journey. From the story, it moves into a modern processing plant, publicly showing the production process of Small Tea Can. Then comes the heavy-hitting segment, launching three new products, and finally interactive chatting and a lottery draw.

03 As a product launch platform, WeChat Channels breaks the limitations of offline events, allowing users and netizens to understand the company and its products from multiple angles. More importantly, it vividly conveys the company's culture.

04 The entire live stream fully showed me Small Tea Can's mission: using a single leaf to warm the world. This isn't just a phrase—it's something that permeates the live stream, something the company has always practiced, and something we can all feel.

05 A company is a group of people, and the core driving force behind its development is its mission. A single leaf has shown us the transformation and development of Chinese tea. This is also the value that the founder is passing on. Du is full of controversy, but he uses his actions to convey his belief to the world.

06 The words emotion, value, and story appearing in the live stream are something to look forward to. Product promotion, mission-driven motivation, and consumer upgrading—this massive advertising sign of live streaming must be used well.

07 After ten years of honing, Du said that the product is the one, marketing is the zero. The product is the way, marketing is the method. Small Tea Can's ten years of development has overturned the traditional tea industry, striving to bring Chinese tea to the world—not just as raw material sold at low prices, but to let the world see Chinese original leaf tea. I have confidence in Small Tea Can and look forward to Chinese tea. Keep it up!
