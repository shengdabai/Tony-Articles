Note 91

### 001 Marketing Begins with Running the Business

What is marketing for, and what should it actually do? In Chinese, the first character in “marketing” is the same one used in “operations.” That is a useful reminder: the business itself comes first. Run it well and focus on what matters.

### 002 Build a Solid Product

The product is the 1; marketing supplies the zeros. Put them together and the possibilities multiply. It is almost impossible to overstate the importance of product quality.

### 003 Staying Grounded Is Harder Than It Sounds

Marketing can amplify your influence, but it can also limit your development. The key is to stay grounded in reality: do not set irrational targets, chase growth blindly, or break the rules. Move forward one step at a time.

### 004 Build Income That Continues After You Stop Working

Marketing can help create leverage and income that continues even when you are not actively working. Writing, independent media, and software are all channels through which that leverage can grow.

### 005 Improve in Small Steps, Through Practice

WeChat Channels is an excellent marketing channel. Make short videos and keep livestreaming. You only discover the real problems by doing the work, and only then can you improve and iterate. Marketing lives in the details, so start small.

### 006 Learn by Using, and Use What You Learn

When analyzing cases, Uncle Mao suggested studying the product pages behind bestsellers on Taobao—something many of us overlook. The same applies to livestreaming: examine how successful rooms design their product pages. Start with the details, imitate what works, then go beyond it.

### 007 Use the Platform's Leverage

The network value of WeChat Channels connects us with resources that may be impossible to price. Through the platform, for example, I have built a network of more than ten thousand contacts and earned six figures in revenue. And that is only the beginning.

### 008 Practice Through Consulting

Consulting is an important way to strengthen your marketing ability. Learn to ask questions, observe, and analyze. Begin by solving real problems.

### 009 The Essence of Marketing

Marketing expert Xiao Masong has said that marketing is ultimately about helping other people succeed and creating value. Help others and you also help yourself; create a genuine win-win. Marketing opens up a much wider world.

### 010 Two Essential Business Skills

Marketing and innovation both depend on a strong product. A company creates customers and meets their needs. Build with care, then set sail for the stars.

That is all ten. If this helped you, thank you for liking and supporting the work.
