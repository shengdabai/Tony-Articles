## Bad Habits Can Only Be Overcome by Building Good Ones

In addition to the guests' shares, the live Q&A from the parents also made the Tea Talk very lively.

Especially when it came to how to "get rid of" bad habits.

Of course, in Li Xiaolai's system of family education, there is no concept of "getting rid of" bad habits:

> Bad habits are "meta-destructive." They destroy everything—learning processes, doing things, and more. And just like good habits, bad habits also have an accumulation effect.
>
> Even more concerning is that a single good habit may not have a big impact, **but a single bad habit can actually be more destructive. One bad habit, on its own, can ruin everything.**

However:

> The so-called "breaking a habit" is difficult… to be precise, it's not difficult, but **practically impossible.**
>
> So, **it's not about correcting bad habits, but about building a good habit to defeat the previous bad one.**

It's undeniable that even after understanding this, it's still difficult to put it into practice in daily life.
