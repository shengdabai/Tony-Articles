# Do It Long Enough

> Published: 2023-11-28 · [中文](../zh/2023-11-28-%E5%81%9A%E5%B0%B1%E5%81%9A%E5%BE%97%E8%B6%B3%E5%A4%9F%E4%B9%85.md) | [English](../en/2023-11-28-do-it-long-enough.md)

---

In modern society, we are always chasing quick success and instant gratification, but we often overlook the importance of patience and persistence.

To accomplish something, you must do it for a long enough time.

It is well known that success is not achieved overnight. Whether you're an athlete, an artist, or an entrepreneur, they all spend a great deal of time and effort to reach their goals.

Long-term persistence not only cultivates our skills but also demonstrates our determination and perseverance, which are key to success.

Almost all success stories share a common thread: they involve many setbacks and failures. However, only those who are willing to keep going can ultimately overcome the difficulties.

As we persist for a long time, we gradually move past the initial struggles, continuously improving our skills and experience, and getting closer to our goals.

Long-term focus and patience are not just for the final success, but for our personal growth and development. In this process, we accumulate valuable experience and lessons, recognize our strengths and weaknesses, and improve through reflection and adjustment. This kind of growth cannot be achieved quickly; it requires our continuous investment of time and effort.

To achieve success in something, you must be patient and do it for a long time. Long-term persistence not only strengthens our willpower and determination but also helps us grow and improve continuously.

Slow down, focus on the present, and invest enough time and effort to achieve your goals. These are essential conditions in our pursuit of success.

Li Xiaolai mentioned in his course that time is our ultimate productive resource.

Grasp time, accumulate continuously, and practice long-term.

Time is a precious and irreversible resource, and how we effectively use it determines whether we can achieve success in our lives and careers.

Time is our most valuable resource because it is fair and non-renewable. Everyone has exactly 24 hours a day, but we often lose ourselves in busyness and waste a lot of time.

A study shows that people waste nearly two hours per day on social media. If we realize the importance of time and treat it as our ultimate productive resource, we can better manage it, focus on improving efficiency, and achieve our goals.

Continuous accumulation is the key to effectively using time. As the saying goes, "A drop of water can wear through stone, and a rope can cut wood," through small daily efforts, we can accumulate great achievements.

For example, if we spend 15 minutes a day learning new knowledge or skills, after a year, we will have accumulated nearly 100 hours of learning time. This habit of continuous accumulation allows our abilities to constantly improve and opens up new paths in our lives.

Long-term practice is key to improving efficiency and skills. Research shows that to become an expert in a field, one needs at least 10,000 hours of practice. This means that only through repeated practice can we reach an exceptional level in a particular area.

We should look for opportunities in our daily lives to engage in long-term practice and maintain consistency, so we can better use time to achieve our goals.

Time is our most valuable resource, and we should fully recognize its value and treat it as our ultimate productive resource. Through continuous accumulation and long-term practice, we can better manage and utilize time, gradually achieving our goals and aspirations.

Let’s cherish every moment, seize opportunities, and focus on doing the things we are doing right now.

Every step has value: do things long enough, even if only a little progress is made each time, over time, you will see results.

In today’s fast-paced society, we often chase instant success and immediate results, and tend to overlook the need for persistence and patience in doing things.

Whether it's learning a new skill, mastering a sport, or achieving a career goal, time and persistence are needed. Even if we only make a little progress each time, the cumulative effect can be significant.

As time accumulates, every step becomes precious and valuable.

In addition to the accumulation of progress, persistent effort also strengthens our willpower and determination. Long-term commitment to something—whether it's building a physique, improving skills, or developing a habit—shapes us into resilient individuals.

This kind of character not only brings results in our current tasks but also helps us navigate future challenges smoothly.

Each small progress brings positive psychological feedback, encouraging us to keep going. Suppose you're learning a foreign language, and you can only remember one or two new words each day. Such progress may seem minor, but as you keep learning, you will gradually accumulate vocabulary and eventually reach fluency. Even if the progress is slow, the positive feedback keeps us motivated and persistent.

Whether it's pursuing a career, personal growth, or skill development, we need to treat time as a friend. Even if the progress is minimal, as long as we keep going consistently, over time, we will definitely achieve significant results.

Set clear goals, break them into steps, and enjoy the sense of accomplishment from each small progress, allowing every step to maximize its value.

Every step is a brick on the path to success, no matter how small, and it is a proud achievement worth celebrating.
