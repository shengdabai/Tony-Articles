Tony Sheng's 94th Original Article

Now is not the time to write—when then? Just write it out!

I've been in the Li Xiaolai Writing Training Camp for two sessions now, and each time felt like a rebirth. In the first session, I was a reviewer for two months. In the second session, I was honored to be the group leader of Group 7, accompanying my group members through a month of writing transformation. I'd like to thank the 202 class operations team for their support, and as an intern deputy assistant, I could serve the class members more. Of course, there are also our group leaders, reviewers, and operations officers. Thank you all for your hard work. Having you around is truly a blessing!

The reason I joined the writing training camp was simple: Li Xiaolai! I joined the *Road to Financial Freedom* course long ago, but didn't take it seriously until 2020, when I started to study it seriously. It really made me feel amazed—what was I doing before? I missed out on such a valuable resource. Seeing Li Xiaolai's writing training camp gave me a lot of motivation. How does Li Xiaolai teach writing? After all, we're all English teachers, and I must have fallen into a logic trap. English teachers' writing skills are strong, right? And the facts confirm that! Plus, this course is so valuable for a year—what reason do I have to refuse it? No one recommended it, but just by following and learning and learning again on the WeChat official account, I decided to sign up. After finishing the first session, I had to continue with the second one. I want to improve my writing and serve everyone—it's an honor!

Thinking back, the biggest challenge in writing before was lack of motivation. I never thought about writing, let alone writing consistently. Even though I knew writing was important, I didn't think it would help me much. At least, it could help me express more, and the more I expressed, the higher my input requirements would be. That's the gap between thinking and doing. To change my habit of rarely writing, joining the writing training camp was a great opportunity.

Starting from the first session of the writing camp, I developed the habit of daily output, no matter how busy or not. Writing 2000 words every day was a must. Some people might think it's hard, but I think that's only if you haven't joined the writing camp. Here, as long as you want to write, nothing is impossible. Writing every day for 200 days, I want to continue for 1000 days, 1 year, 7 years—at least write for 7 years in my lifetime. Plus, with the support of so many writing camp partners, their encouraging words, how could I not have motivation? And being surrounded by excellent people, I just can't stop writing. Excellence is a habit, and this habit is long-term value. Since the first day of the camp, I've been writing every day, and I'm writing more and more. This feeling is amazing, and there are so many partners supporting me, it's so joyful.

Writing consistently, no matter how many words, but writing every day, even if it's just copying and paraphrasing the original text, is still a form of practice. This habit might be hard to break! Learning to appreciate and praise, surrounded by excellent people, sometimes I, who is a bit introverted, don't know how to express it. But when I received so much encouragement and support from my fellow writers, I realized I couldn't be stingy with my encouragement. I also wanted to give more back to my fellow writers. Reviewing is part of writing, and there's also the chance to connect with fellow writers, gaining growth and a lot of love (⑉°з°)-♡ Writing has become more methodical, with techniques like highlighting, sensing, analyzing, comparing, and rhetoric. There are practical and effective methods, and they're easy to implement. With all these tools in hand, I've come to understand writing better. After all, this isn't something that requires a lot of effort or barriers—just want to do it, and you can.

After the training camp ends, will I continue to maintain these learning and writing habits? Maybe I don't need to. I remember Li Xiaolai saying that in his understanding, there's no need for effort or persistence. This saying has stayed with me. Why not use effort and persistence? Because of passion, writing naturally flows from feeling. Before, I needed to persist because I thought it was difficult and had to endure and keep going. Now, for me, writing doesn't seem to require much effort or persistence. Just write, write sincerely.

After the training camp, I write every day, creating a new column on Tony's learning perception, recording my daily learning insights, internalizing knowledge from various platforms, and forming my own understanding for better output. WeChat official account, WeChat Channels, and Moments are all my public platforms for sharing. I plan the content, clarify the logic, express it thoughtfully, and communicate sincerely!

The writing training camp has ended, but we can continue to use our works to connect with others in the post-writing garden. On the New Work platform, I'll record my perceptions. Of course, if you're someone who's always striving for growth, welcome to join the fixed-investment life class led by Li Xiaolai, where we can grow together! Once again, thank you all. Meeting you has been an honor!
