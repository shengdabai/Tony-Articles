## **1\. What is a WeChat Owned Audience?**

Private domain traffic refers to channels that individuals or companies own, which can be used freely, repeatedly, and multiple times, allowing direct contact with users at any time and frequency.

Examples include personal WeChat accounts, WeChat groups, mini programs, and self-developed apps.

Private domain construction refers to a marketing system built on one's own user data, aiming to achieve marketing goals through owned audience traffic. So, what is owned audience traffic? It refers to a user resource that we ourselves have built and accumulated, which can be used for consumption and interaction on our platform. Private domain traffic is characterized by high stickiness, high conversion rate, and high repurchase rate. Therefore, owned audience traffic is an important means to achieve marketing goals.

When building a owned audience, we need to classify and segment our users to better understand their needs and behavioral patterns. For example, we can classify users by gender, age, region, and interests. Through user segmentation, we can better understand their needs and preferences and provide more personalized services.

In the process of owned audience construction, we also need to focus on content production and distribution. Content production involves creating content that meets user needs and preferences. Content distribution is about delivering this content to users so they can better understand and use our products and services. Therefore, in owned audience construction, we need to manage content more meticulously to better attract and retain users.

Private domain construction is a long-term task that requires continuous optimization and adjustment. Only by constantly improving our owned audience construction can we better achieve marketing goals, enhance user satisfaction, and build an excellent brand image.

### **Personal Account**

A personal account is essentially a KOC (Key Opinion Consumer), different from the distant KOL. A KOC is a friend you trust, who can influence your purchasing decisions.

However, the creation of a KOC is similar to that of a KOL in many ways:

  * Content positioning: Clearly define the values and perspectives you want to convey

  * Personality traits: Have a unique style and characteristics

  * Clothing and makeup: Match your content positioning and personality traits

  * Trends: Keep up with the development and changes of the times and industry

  * Editing skills: Master basic text, image, and video editing techniques

For example, the personal account of Perfect Diary — Xiaowanzi — is a real girl image. Her WeChat Moments are carefully managed, with 2-3 posts per day. It's not just product introductions, but also selfies, life reflections, and daily sharing.

Placing such a girl in the Moments won't cause any user discomfort and will subtly deepen the impression of the Perfect Diary brand.

In the marketing field, two important roles are KOC and KOL. KOC refers to Key Opinion Consumer, i.e., key opinion consumers, while KOL refers to Key Opinion Leader, i.e., key opinion leaders. Although both can influence people on social media, their differences lie in:

  * KOC is a regular consumer who can share their purchasing or usage experience through their personal social media accounts, influencing their friends and family.

  * KOL is someone with expertise and influence in a particular field or industry, who can influence their followers by sharing insights and experiences.

Here are some examples to illustrate the difference between these two roles:

  * In the beauty industry, a KOC might share an article about using a particular lipstick, influencing their friends and family to buy the product. A KOL, on the other hand, might share reviews of different lipstick brands, influencing their followers' perceptions and purchasing decisions of these brands.

  * In the travel industry, a KOC might share travel photos and experiences, influencing their friends and family's travel plans. A KOL might share travel guides and experiences, influencing their followers' travel decisions and itinerary arrangements.

### **Community Management**

The most important thing in community management is to treat it as a circle created around a specific goal. A KOC is the host of this salon.

To maintain the community, you should set thresholds to filter out users who don't belong to this circle, making the user base more precise. Also, set an onboarding ritual to make users feel a sense of belonging.

During community activities, besides offering prizes, you should also have KOCs support the event, and finally use the herd mentality to encourage users to make purchases.

In the Xiaowanzi community of Perfect Diary, after each promotional activity, someone would comment that it's a great deal, which would trigger other users to buy.

If personal account posts and community maintenance are about cultivating users, then community activities are the time to harvest.

### **Viralaudience acquisition**

Viralaudience acquisition is a marketing method that leverages users' social relationships to acquire new users. It can effectively reduce the cost of user acquisition and improve user conversion rates and loyalty. The core of viralaudience acquisition is to stimulate users' sharing desire, encouraging them to actively promote the product or service. To achieve this, we can use the following methods:

  * Rewards: Offer users material or spiritual incentives, such as coupons, points, red envelopes, or gifts, to encourage them to invite others to use the product or service.

  * Psychological identification: Let users feel that the product or service aligns with their values, interests, or social identity, thus generating a sense of identification and belonging, and willing to recommend it to others.

  * Distribution: Let users become partners of the product or service, earning commissions or bonuses through sharing, forming a long-term incentive.

  * Multi-methods: Combine different platforms and channels, such as official accounts, personal accounts, communities, and mini programs, to design various viral strategies and communication scripts, increasing user participation and interactivity.

These are several common practices for WeChat owned audience marketing. Enterprises can choose the most suitable methods based on their own circumstances to achieve more precise and effective marketing.
