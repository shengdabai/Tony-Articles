Six Classics Interpret Me, I Interpret the Six Classics #Plain Language #Case Sharing#

01 When I need it, I can always find books to help me—books are my brain trust.

02 Although many people think that being a book reviewer isn't suitable, I have to say that creating products related to reading can effectively show your level and expertise to users.

03 Often people say they read a hundred books a year, but a rational analysis shows that spending three hours a day on deep reading to complete 100 high-quality books in terms of input and understanding is extremely difficult.

04 Statistics show that people with higher education levels read 17 books in a year, which is more than the average of 12. However, two-thirds of people can't even read that many books in a year, let alone deeply read and think about them, not to mention applying the knowledge from such books.

05 Goal-oriented reading is a good choice. For example, video creators need to read more about communication and psychology to improve their writing and content creation.

06 Understanding users, finding their needs, and figuring out how to solve them is the origin of product development. For instance, if you want to make videos about elderly care, it's too vague—without specific scenarios or problems, how can you create a product?

07 Narrow down your goals, find a clear and specific problem, then read extensively to solve it, create the product, and wait for your good news.
