Note 89

How WeChat Channels Infected You

001 Network Effects

WeChat's network effects have made today's internet users unable to live without it. WeChat Channels further connect the various parts of WeChat, and sooner or later, they will infect you too.

002 Mutual Influence

Users influence WeChat Channels, and WeChat Channels influence users. WeChat users change their decision-making behavior under the influence of WeChat Channels, such as watching news on WeChat Channels.

003 Simplified Decision-Making

Seeing WeChat Channels naturally makes you think of Douyin and Kuaishou. From the start, you're biased toward them, imitate other video platforms, and copy their style on WeChat Channels—obviously, it doesn't work.

004 Uncontrollable Impulse

Driven by mirror neurons, seeing others create WeChat Channels makes you feel compelled to open one yourself, regardless of experience. Just imitate and do it.

005 Group Pressure

WeChat's familiar social network makes it hard to act freely. To maintain an image, you won't comment or like as freely as on other platforms. Group pressure makes users ignore their potential interests.

006 Standing Out

Confident in your creative content, if the data isn't good, it's the users' fault. Stand on your own perspective and build your own brand.

007 Differentiation Competition

Create small but beautiful content, yet aim for broad traffic. It won't last long, and doubts will arise. The original differentiation eventually fades away.

008 Content is King

At its core, WeChat Channels are the entire internet's traffic. When imitating or differentiating, you must think clearly: what is the content? Focus on creating long-term valuable content.

009 Personal Value

Pass on the information you want to share. Don't be swayed by temporary data or short-term fluctuations. WeChat Channels are your amplifier—just stay focused.

0010 Commercial Positioning

Pay attention to identity signals and build a complete commercial loop. One account, one positioning. Different positions mean different values. Develop in a matrix and build a commercial ecosystem.
