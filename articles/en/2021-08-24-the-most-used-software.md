Don't you know which app you use the most?

WeChat? Douyin? Kuaishou? Xiaohongshu? Weibo? Toutiao...

My most used app is Dedao!

I met Dedao in university, which accidentally exposed my age. After several years of getting along, I've come to really love it!

I think there are many reasons why I like it, but to summarize, there are a few key aspects:

1. Diverse course types: mainly audio courses with accompanying text, video courses, live courses, and readings. The courses cover various areas such as business, leadership, literature, medicine, etc...

It meets my diverse learning needs, and the listening mode is my favorite. Compared to other platforms, the content is more carefully refined, combining academic and practical aspects with solid, interesting, and practical material.

2. Different sections: courses, audiobooks, e-books, and Nuggets.

Especially the e-books. Although there are many free e-book platforms, Dedao has a lot of new book copyrights. Not only can I read freely, but I can also see notes from some big names, which is really great. Audiobooks are also something I really enjoy—being able to quickly grasp a book in 20 minutes. The Nuggets section really moved me. After all, life often presents problems that require different strategies and techniques, and this section is very practical.

3. A rich community. In a course, it's easy to meet like-minded people, making it convenient for communication. Of course, the most exciting part of the Knowledge City is that the course instructors and Dedao's big names also post on the platform. This is a rare opportunity for social interaction. Some discussions and even debates are really great.

4. The last point is especially important: the reward system. Dedao gives a certificate upon completing each course, which gives a sense of achievement. Summarizing my learning outcomes is very meaningful. The various types of medals are also great—each one comes with different discount coupons, which allows me to purchase courses at a discount. I've invested a lot because of this, giggling to myself, but I'm really happy.

The last thing is the excellent certification. I really like these different tags. The verified V certification gives me more exposure.

For Dedao, I have a lot of love!

Knowledge is on Dedao. Dedao is about learning. Loving life, loving knowledge!
