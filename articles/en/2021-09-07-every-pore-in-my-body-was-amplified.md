Tonight, listening to the conversation between Mr. Xu Zhuoyun and Teacher Li Shanyou, made every pore in my body feel amplified, with tears constantly welling up in my eyes!

Here are some excerpts from the dialogue:

1. **Mr. Xu Zhuoyun**: I like the idea of a world at peace for all time, but it wasn’t until I was 90 that I realized the world isn’t peaceful. Because human desires are never satisfied; one greed, one selfishness, and if your heart isn’t at peace, how can the world be?

2. **Teacher Li Shanyou**: Why are you, at 90 years old, not resting well?

**Mr. Xu Zhuoyun**: Time is not on my side. For me, time is up.

I can’t make it through five more years.

Without the care of relatives and friends, I can’t live.

I want to give back to society!

If you organize a group of friends to talk with me, am I not grateful to you?

So, time is not on my side, and I’m doing my best.

3. **Teacher Li Shanyou**: What do you regret most?

**Mr. Xu Zhuoyun**: I regret that I read too few books. I wish I could live another 20 years.

From being unable to sit to being able to sit and walk, everything was made possible by others’ help.

If you compare my books to Mr. Qian Zhongshu’s, his are far more numerous.

4. **Teacher Li Shanyou**: Can you give a message to the young people atChaos (Chaos)?

**Mr. Xu Zhuoyun**: I hope everyone seizes the opportunity to enrich themselves.

To make the world better because of you is the path of having talent.

...

At 91 years old, Mr. Xu uses his perspective to study large-scale history, with rigorous expression and clear logic. In that moment, I felt very small.

After nearly an hour of sharing, Mr. Xu needed to rest on the table for half an hour, yet he said he had no complaints. He just wanted to do something.

Mr. Xu is grateful to every person who helped him throughout his life. He feels he owes too much, so he wants to do something for society in the remaining years, sharing his thoughts with more people.

Yet he said his regret is not having read enough books. I don’t know how many people, if not almost everyone, would feel ashamed about this. Reading is a lifelong endeavor.

Every word from Mr. Xu felt like a bell, ringing in my heart from time to time. Thank you for your precious teachings, Mr. Xu.

These four excerpts are shared with you, the reader of this article. May we remain calm and read well, and work earnestly.

I recommend you watch *Thirteen Questions* Season 4, where there is an episode with Mr. Xu. At 89, he was still typing and writing on the computer. I sincerely invite you to watch it!

Good night, may the world be peaceful forever!
