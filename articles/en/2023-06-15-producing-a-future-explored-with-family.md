A person born after 1995 just entering the workforce may not have money worries, but they often end up with shoulder pain and dark circles under their eyes, trapped in a vicious cycle of physical and mental exhaustion.

Those indifficult circumstances often long for change, but the transition from qualitative to quantitative change requires time and consistent action.

Producing together with family is an effective way to break free from this cycle and move toward a better life. Today's story is about a young couple in their 90s who started from scratch, and perhaps it can offer you some inspiration.

#

**01**

Hello everyone, it's a great honor to share my experience. First, let me introduce our family. I work in the public sector, my husband is a programmer, and we have a little baby.

Five years ago, I was an architectural designer, and my income depended on the volume of drawings I produced. I often worked late into the night, and with a future plan to have children, my career was about to be interrupted. The high workload led to shoulder strain and sleep deprivation, leaving me with little time for socializing, dating, or even career prospects.

But the immense work pressure also pushed me into the trap of compensatory consumption. The reality was a vicious cycle, and I began to seek change. Reading *The Art of Time* opened my mind, making me realize I wasn't alone.

Faced with difficulties, I needed time to gradually improve and accumulate. I could use my mind to gain freedom. But how could I increase the value of my time?

I wanted to draw faster and better, and take the exams for the second and first level construction engineer to increase the value of my career.

Based on this thinking, I started working diligently on drawings during the day and studying for the second-level construction engineer exam at night. During this period, I unexpectedly got an opportunity to take a civil service exam. There was a lot to learn, and there was no shortcut, as Li Xiaolai's book said: exchange is the key. I worked day and night, studying and finally passed the exam, changing my production method for the first time.

In 2,022, besides my salary, I started exploring new production methods from my strengths.

Since I was good at Chinese in school, I began to try writing news articles.

I set a goal of 60 articles a year, or five a month. I completed the goal in the first year and earned over 10,000 yuan in royalties.

In the second year, I wrote for half a year and completed about 30 articles, then took a maternity leave. Writing not only brought in income but also earned me recognition and a lot of growth potential.

What do people usually do after work?

My husband used to play video games and watch Bilibili after work.

After getting married, I constantly encouraged and motivated him to study programming knowledge after work. After all, learning is the best form of productivity.

After three months of hard study, he passed an interview and joined a top company in the industry, doubling his income. Moreover, he even started a ride-sharing service on his way to and from work every weekend.

Through the accumulation of capital and time as production resources, we generated income.

In 2020, I started to have savings and bought Li Xiaolai's *The Road to Financial Freedom* course, learning about the concept and methodology of regular investing.

Following the smile curve principle, I tried regular investing with a small amount, and after achieving a 30% return, I sold it. With 6,000 yuan in principal, I earned over 2,000 yuan.

Later, I developed the habit of investing a few thousand yuan each month, following a stable mindset and walking the smile curve. I experienced the A-share market falling to over 2,000 points, but now the stock market is rising again, entering a profit-making phase.

When should I sell? Long-term holding for 3 to 5 years.

Regular investing has made me cherish money more, because money can generate more money.

Future directions for exploration and improvement:

Our family's production method has transitioned from increasing the value of time to selling one unit of time multiple times. I continue to explore in writing, focusing on inputting and outputting high-quality content, improving my writing skills and content creation.

My husband is enhancing his programming expertise, achieving personal productization in programming.

Learning good family education, building a strong family, and having more children is a very economical and high-reward production.

That's my sharing. I hope it can offer some inspiration to you if you're in a similar situation. Thank you.
