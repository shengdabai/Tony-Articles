# Then I'll Do It Myself

> Published: 2023-12-07 · [中文](../zh/2023-12-07-%E9%82%A3%E6%88%91%E8%87%AA%E5%B7%B1%E6%9D%A5%E5%90%A7.md) | [English](../en/2023-12-07-that-s-me-i-ll-do-it-myself.md)

Once upon a time, a little red hen found a grain of wheat in the barnyard. She said, "Who will plant this grain of wheat?"

"I won't plant it," said the dog.

"I won't plant it," said the cat.

"I won't plant it," said the goose.

"I won't plant it," said the turkey.

"That's me, I'll do it myself," said the little red hen.

So she planted the wheat.

A little while later, the wheat grew and matured. The little red hen said, "Who will harvest this wheat?"

"I won't harvest it," said the dog.

"I won't harvest it," said the cat.

"I won't harvest it," said the goose.

"I won't harvest it," said the turkey.

"That's me, I'll do it myself," said the little red hen.

So she harvested the wheat.

"Who will grind this wheat?" the little red hen asked.

"I won't grind it," said the dog.

"I won't grind it," said the cat.

"I won't grind it," said the goose.

"I won't grind it," said the turkey.

"That's me, I'll do it myself," said the little red hen.

So she took the wheat to the mill and came back with the flour.

"Who will bake this flour?" the little red hen asked.

"I won't bake it," said the dog.

"I won't bake it," said the cat.

"I won't bake it," said the goose.

"I won't bake it," said the turkey.

"That's me, I'll do it myself," said the little red hen.

So she baked the flour and made a loaf of bread.

"Who will eat this bread?" the little red hen asked.

"I'll eat it," said the dog.

"I'll eat it," said the cat.

"I'll eat it," said the goose.

"I'll eat it," said the turkey.

"I'll eat it," said the little red hen, and then she ate the whole loaf.

This classic story of the little red hen was first brought to my attention by Mr. Munger. The story mainly conveys the idea that "a little effort brings a little reward," but Mr. Munger focused on the line, "That's me, I'll do it myself."

When no one teaches you to do something, or no one is willing to do it, you can be the first to take action. Don't let the lack of others' involvement negate your own ideas.

Mr. Munger mastered everything he needed through self-study, by reading, learning, and building his knowledge system and classic mental models.

Teacher Li Xiaolai also expressed that everything is self-taught. Most of the time, we just don't invest enough time. Time is the foundation of everything.

A hardworking hen doesn't rely on others. She lays eggs, hatches them, and finally enjoys the bountiful results. This story is not only a classic in children's education but also an inspiring tale for adults.

Independence is the key to success: the little red hen is a brave, determined hen. Although many animals around her take the easy way out, the little red hen still chose the path of self-sufficiency—finding her own food, laying eggs, and hatching them.

She understands that only by relying on herself can she steadily achieve her production goals, and she courageously puts her plan into action. This is an inspiring story that tells us to believe in our own abilities and not to hand our fate over to others.

The power of self-reliance: the little red hen achieved her success through self-reliance. She found her own way, through her own efforts, to achieve success.

In real life, working on our own not only helps us solve problems but also boosts our confidence and self-respect. This allows us to fully realize our potential and achieve our goals, making us better people.

Self-improvement is the only path to success: the little red hen never let others influence her during her growth. She knew that only by becoming strong could she ultimately achieve her goals.

On the path of life, encountering difficulties is normal, but we should face them calmly and move forward. If we can maintain confidence and focus, and use our own strength calmly and with a plan, we will certainly perform well when facing challenges.

The story of the little red hen is full of wisdom, reminding us to stay brave, confident, and persistent on our journey to success. We should take responsibility for ourselves and often step out of our comfort zones, bravely walking our own path.

Just like the little red hen, only by working hard on our own and bravely moving forward can we realize our value and create our own beautiful future.

Learn from the little red hen and understand the life philosophy she offers us for a lifetime.

The power of collaboration: when the little red hen was hatching her chicks, she also faced difficulties. However, she didn't give up and actively sought help. After receiving assistance from other animals, she successfully brought each chick into the world.

Collaboration can help us overcome difficulties and achieve our goals. In real life, we also need to work with others, put in collective effort, and achieve success.

The importance of mutual aid and sharing: in the story of the little red hen, she formed a relationship of mutual aid and sharing with the other animals. She hatched the eggs herself, but in the end, she shared the newly hatched chicks with the other animals.

This mutual aid and sharing not only helped her but also benefited the whole group. In real life, when we are willing to help others and share with them, we build better relationships and gain more support and help.

Collaboration creates greater achievements: the little red hen's collaboration with the other animals not only helped her overcome difficulties but also led to greater success. Her eggs were hatched in a better environment, and the chicks received better care.

Only through collaboration can we achieve greater success and create a better future. Whether in work or life, teamwork can gather the strength of many to achieve common goals.

The story of the little red hen is not only a fable about courage and perseverance but also offers insights into human relationships. Through collaboration, mutual aid, and synergy, we can overcome difficulties together and achieve success.

Be proactive in collaborating with others, develop good relationships, and create a better future. In this process, remember: success depends not only on our own efforts but also on working together with others.

Take control of your destiny—on your own is better!

In the era of social media, we often see stories about team collaboration and social networks' success. However, we sometimes overlook an important fact: being on your own is better.

Autonomous decision-making and self-development: being on your own means we can make our own decisions and set our own roadmap, without being influenced by others. Independent action allows us to pursue our dreams and goals more freely.

By relying on ourselves, we can better understand our needs and values, and our self-development speed is faster. Whether in our careers or personal lives, independent action allows us to better control our own destiny.

Rapid adaptation and flexibility: by relying on our own strength, we can adapt more quickly to changing environments. Without the limitations of relying on others, we can more flexibly adjust our plans and strategies.

When facing challenges, we can make decisions and take action quickly, without needing to consult or wait for others' opinions. This autonomy and adaptability are key to success.

Personal achievement and self-confidence: achievements made by ourselves enhance our confidence and inner motivation. When we independently complete a task or achieve a goal, we feel a greater sense of accomplishment and satisfaction.

This self-confidence will inspire even stronger motivation and determination, pushing us to achieve even greater success. Independent action not only brings us professional achievements but also makes us stronger and more confident.

The importance of teamwork and social networks is undeniable, but we cannot ignore the significant advantages that come from relying on ourselves.

By acting independently, we can make our own decisions, adapt quickly to the environment, and achieve personal success. We should cherish the freedom and autonomy that come from relying on ourselves and fully realize our potential, taking control of our own destiny.

Believe in yourself, be brave in pursuing your goals, rely on yourself, and let your success shine brightly!
