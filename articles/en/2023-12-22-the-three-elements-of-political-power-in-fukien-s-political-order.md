Francis Fukuyama thoroughly analyzes the origins of political order in his book *The Origins of Political Order*. Li Xiaolai, in his column on *Dedao* titled *Li Xiaolai on AI-era Family Education*, further refines these elements of political power into three key components for children's development: physical strength, intelligence, and charisma.

Power is an essential element in social life. In the context of national politics, it manifests as the combination of violence, intelligence, and charisma. In personal development, it is reflected in the integration of physical strength, intelligence, and charisma.

In national politics, violence represents the state's macro-level power, serving as the foundation for maintaining national security and stability. Intelligence is demonstrated through political decision-making and governance capabilities, embodying the state's wisdom. Charisma reflects the nation's influence and appeal on the international stage, representing its soft power.

Violence, intelligence, and charisma are interdependent and indispensable. As citizens of the nation, we should follow the country's guidance, actively participate in social life, and contribute our own strength.

For individuals, physical strength, intelligence, and charisma are equally important. Physical strength is the fundamental energy of the body; maintaining good physical health provides a sense of fulfillment and vitality in daily life. Intelligence is the capacity for thought and cognition, serving as the source of wisdom for addressing various challenges. Charisma refers to personal charm, attractiveness, and personal magnetism, enabling us to gain others' recognition and social opportunities. Together, physical strength, intelligence, and charisma shape an individual's influence and competitiveness in social life.

In daily life, we can develop and apply these three elements through specific methods to enhance personal power. First, maintain good physical health by exercising regularly, paying attention to diet and rest, and improving physical strength. Second, continuously learn and think, broadening knowledge and refining thinking patterns to enhance intelligence. Finally, focus on building and maintaining relationships, cultivating personal charisma and influence, and improving social skills.

By developing and applying these elements, we can fully realize our personal potential in daily life and better achieve our personal goals. Power is not only a characteristic of the nation but also a crucial factor in personal success and happiness.

Whether in national politics or personal development, the three elements of power—physical strength, intelligence, and charisma—are indispensable. By developing and utilizing these three elements, we can better face life's challenges and realize our values and goals.

Physical strength is the fundamental power of the body, supporting our daily activities and movements. Maintaining good physical condition not only helps preserve health but also enhances work efficiency and the quality of life. As an old saying goes: "The body is the foundation of revolution." Through regular exercise, good dietary habits, and sufficient rest, we can unlock the body's potential and have more energy to pursue our dreams.

Intelligence is the ability to think and solve problems, serving as a crucial foundation for personal development. Continuous learning and constant thinking are key to unlocking intellectual potential. Through extensive reading, participating in courses, and engaging in discussions with others, we can expand our knowledge base and broaden our thinking. Enhancing intelligence not only helps us overcome challenges but also opens the door to success.

Charisma is a vital factor in attracting and influencing others. It is not only related to appearance but also involves our expressiveness, communication skills, and personal magnetism. By cultivating a positive attitude, building confidence, and improving interpersonal skills, we can establish good relationships and enhance our influence. Charisma makes us more likable and appreciated in both work and life, offering more opportunities and the possibility of success.

Physical strength, intelligence, and charisma—these three elements together form our personal power. By developing and fully utilizing these three elements, we can unlock our personal potential and move toward a more successful and fulfilling life. Whether pursuing personal goals or facing challenges, we need the support of physical strength, the guidance of intelligence, and the release of charisma.
