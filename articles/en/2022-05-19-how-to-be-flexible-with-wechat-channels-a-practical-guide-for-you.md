02 If you haven't done a WeChat Official Account, do you still need to? You can skip it. Just leave your WeChat ID in the WeChat Channels introduction, respond to private messages frequently, and guide users to your personal WeChat.

03 Without registering a small shop for your business, can you still post products on WeChat Channels? You can use the platform's built-in small shop product for distribution, and direct traffic to your personal WeChat. If you really want to post your own products, you can list them on platforms like Aiguo and Moguai for under 1,000 yuan. Merchant registration requires fees. In this case, the small business shop is free. You can use the business credentials of family or friends, clear up the bank account issues, and even help others' small shops with distribution.

04 Can I register an Enterprise WeChat account without any qualifications? Yes, you can. It allows up to 200 users, but there's no limit on how many people can add you. Just set up an automatic reply message to guide them to your personal WeChat.

05 Must you use Xiaoe Tong for knowledge-based paid courses? Let me tell you, you don't have to. Enterprise WeChat offers 20GB of free storage, and 100GB after certification. It's better to save costs and keep the service fees for yourself. There's also the Feishu software, which is very powerful, free to use, and can upload audio and video without any issues.

06 There's always a way around difficulties. If you're unsure, ask more questions. If the operation isn't clear, find the customer service. Believe me, this is faster than tutorials. Use it with questions, and you'll learn best by doing.

07 If it feels too complicated, go for a business license. Just search online, make sure to use the official website of the national or local government, and if you're still unsure, call to confirm. I hope the above helps you. Be flexible, and there's always a way!
