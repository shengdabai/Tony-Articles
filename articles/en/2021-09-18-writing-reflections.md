154th Original Piece

Sorry for keeping you waiting—I’ve been on a three-day break from posting. I’ve had some new reflections on writing, and I’d like to share them with you now!

During the WeChat Official Account writing phase, my growth has been somewhat limited, or perhaps I’ve even come to a standstill, even though I keep updating articles daily.

The writing process itself isn’t difficult for me—I can maintain a steady output. But the issue is that once I publish an article, I tend to think it’s done. In reality, I need to pay attention to feedback and data.

Unfortunately, very few articles receive meaningful feedback. There are three main reasons for this: new WeChat Official Accounts don’t have a comment feature, even though you can choose to “Like” and comment there, which is still rare; the platform’s distribution mechanism makes it very unlikely for content to be seen in the public domain, and I currently only have one article that has been read by nearly a thousand people, received some tips and likes, but comments are still few and far between; for an ordinary person like me, the platform’s opportunities are actually quite challenging, and even with cross-promotion through WeChat Channels, the results aren’t great.

I don’t really care about the so-called traffic dividend (traffic dividend is a term used in Chinese internet culture to refer to the sudden surge in traffic or popularity). I believe that when I’m ready, I can always seize the opportunity. But I overlooked the difficulty of spreading content during non-traffic periods. I don’t have any existing traffic or continuous exposure, which makes it harder for me to appear more in the public domain. This might be my chance.

After some research on the platforms, I’ve decided that I should focus on Xiaohongshu and WeChat Channels now.

Xiaohongshu can be a platform for writing and content output, focusing on sharing experience and practical tips. For example, my experience as an English teacher could be valuable to other teachers, as well as my experiences in education and other things I want to share. Shorter content, more traffic, and most importantly, receiving feedback. I always remember a saying: no feedback is also a kind of feedback. My content needs further adjustment and optimization.

For video creation, I’ll continue with my WeChat Channels content. I need to deeply refine the script, and I plan to create content in one go per theme, with the focus afterward being on operations and observing feedback. Regarding topic selection and material gathering, I need to enrich my personal resource library and learn from similar accounts. On the other hand, I’ll continue with live streaming experiences, but not every day. It should be more spontaneous, after all, live streaming is about making people happy, and I’ll learn as I go.

Regarding the positioning of the WeChat Official Account, I plan to use it as a platform for interaction with the content from my WeChat Channels. The update frequency will decrease, and I’ll focus more on delivering content that users need. My daily reflections and learning insights will be posted on a new platform, a public and free writing platform, where everyone is welcome to exchange ideas.

Finally, writing requires not only continuous output but also adjusting plans. I need more feedback, accumulate traffic, and create breakout content. I’m always on the road.

Thank you for your time. Let’s walk this path together!
