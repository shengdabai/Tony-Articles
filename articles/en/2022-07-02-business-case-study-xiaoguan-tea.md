Xiaoguan Tea's Ten Years

001 Concentration is the Essence

Condense the brand into a tag. "Warming the world with a single leaf," Xiaoguan Tea's mission has entered the minds of users.

002 Features Matter More Than Colorfulness

In an era of scarce attention, being instantly recognizable is key for a brand. Xiaoguan Tea's black-and-white logo, made up of pinyin, is instantly identifiable. Can a brand with colorful designs, English letters, and symbols really be recognized by users?

003 Better Than Good, Different Than Unique

Focus on master-crafted tea, deeply researching tea leaves. The newly released tea brewer, like a coffee machine, allows users to no longer worry about how to brew tea, making tea drinking standardized and opening up a differentiated path.

004 Open Up to Competitors

Xiaoguan Tea is committed to advancing the Chinese tea industry. Modern factories and research centers are open for visits and exchanges, creating a win-win situation.

005 Pricing is Unique

Criticism continues, such as whether masters can do so much, or whether Xiaoguan Tea is charging for intelligence. Positioned as a high-end brand for business, it's difficult to produce affordable products without sufficient channels and understanding of tea. To some extent, the so-called "IQ tax" is actually a brand tax, and it's justified.

006 Build a Solid Foundation for Brand Extension

Xiaogand Tea has been cultivating for ten years. In July, they launched three new brand directions, based on user research and demand analysis. However, before that, the only ten premium tea products were their core, and the team's understanding of tea was also a ten-year accumulation.

007 Brand Over Company

The brand of Xiaoguan Tea is more important than the company itself. As the company grows, it will naturally create sub-brands, but what consumers care about is the brand, which has always been emphasized in the company's development.

008 A Heart for Global Expansion

A brand that carries its country's unique mental resources has the potential to become a global brand. Xiaoguan Tea carries Chinese tea, warming the world with every leaf. I'm full of hope.

009 A Changing Market, an Unchanging Brand

The market may change, but the brand should not. Although many believe there are more tea options and various brands are rising, Xiaoguan Tea's core product line has remained consistent for ten years without change.

010 Love and Courage

Love is the highest and most powerful commercial driving force.
