Note 79

PiaoHan YiZiCat shares: In a certain sense, WeChat Channels are actually a video-based "verification field." We should upload videos that can encourage users to make transactions with us, letting users "verify" our content and help them make decisions. *PiaoHan 100 Tips*

01. Value free content and verification, because free content represents your level! 02. The cost of spreading content today is almost zero, and content is the key element. Most people think free means just doing it casually. 03. Study short videos and live streams; experts share their best insights without holding back, and this is free. What is expensive? 04. What's expensive is your attention and your ability to act. 05. For example, Teacher Cai Zhizhong shared his thoughts and years of creative insights during his first live stream on Douyin. How much would you need to pay for that? The issue is, you listened, understood, thought, and acted... 06. Another example is advertising slogans. Teacher Hua Shan created many famous slogans, like "Love Cleanliness, Stay at HanTing," six characters, four words, worth millions. You saw it, but would you use it? Or if I used it for you, would it really help you do it well? Advertising is just part of brand marketing. 07. Share the best content for free, and believe me, it will attract people willing to pay for your services.

I've finished writing. Thank you for your likes and support if you're able to make things happen!
