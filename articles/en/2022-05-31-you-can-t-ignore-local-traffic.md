Take Local Live Streaming Seriously #LiveStreaming##Secrets#

01 WeChat Channels live streaming is in full swing, and newcomers, internet celebrities, small businesses, and big companies are all getting ready. Where is your opportunity?

02 First, you need to clarify your positioning. In simple terms, are you focusing on quality or quantity? Do you need to define content and strategy, or do you need to focus on volume and pricing?

03 During my consultations, I often find that users haven't clearly identified their needs. For example, someone might say they want to make more money, but what exactly? Is it through tips, selling products, teaching courses, or collaborations? What exactly are they after?

04 Next, you need to make choices. You must let go of external factors that aren't so important to you right now. For instance, you don't need to consider the entire internet; for you, the local area is the core.

05 WeChat Channels is a massive billboard. How to make your ad more effective? Naturally, it's by targeting your immediate surroundings.

06 Imagine your city, then the city district, then the neighborhood. If you become the top player in this area, what kind of value will you gain?

07 Becoming a local leader means connecting with all local resources. You have a loyal local fan base, and you've already surpassed 90% of ordinary people and businesses!
