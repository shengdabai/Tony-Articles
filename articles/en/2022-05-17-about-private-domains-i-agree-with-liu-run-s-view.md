What is a owned audience? #Plain Language##Little Secret#

01 Liu Run defines a owned audience as: it's something you directly own, repeatable, low-cost, and reachable for free.

02 The two key obstacles to commercial development are information asymmetry and non-transmissible trust. In my view, by building up your owned audience, you can attempt to solve these two issues within it, thereby developing business and creating greater commercial value.

03 During the process of doing a WeChat Channel, you might see a lot of people doing owned audiences. Whether a owned audience is big or not depends on its information reachability. Don't focus on how many followers you have; you need to understand that the real owned audience is extremely limited.

04 For example, someone clearly has tens of millions of followers across the web, but how many of them are real fans? At most, it's 20,000.

05 Generally speaking, there aren't any particularly big accounts on WeChat Channels. Someone once asked me, "If someone has a million followers on Douyin, would they be a gold V blogger on WeChat Channels?" But apparently, they only have a few hundred thousand followers. This is normal. This is WeChat, a social platform. There are a lot of real people here, and the number of people who can accumulate and build a owned audience is even smaller.

06 I recommend an old streamer, a teacher I really admire: Du Zijian. He has reached the level of tens of millions of followers across the web. On WeChat Channels, he did something really impressive: he livestreamed every day for a whole year. His fan group started using the official fan group feature when it was launched, and now I've found that there are 30,000 members in his fan group, and it's still growing every day.

07 Using the WeChat Channel fan group as an entry point to build a owned audience is a great idea. Of course, building it into personal WeChat, corporate WeChat, or WeChat groups is also possible. The next step is to think about how to continuously nurture these relationships.

I've finished writing. Thank you for being the one who makes things happen!
