Catalyzing English Development at New Oriental

001 Catalyzing, Not Pushing

Pushing is from the perspective of oneself, while catalyzing is from the perspective of the other. New Oriental is committed to eliminating obstacles for learners, changing barriers to English learning, and enabling more students to pursue overseas education.

002 Catalyzing Holistic Education

New Oriental's teaching philosophy and development path remain innovative, allowing learners to grow happily, achieving diversified development across all age groups and subjects, and striving to help students overcome one learning obstacle after another.

003 Inspiring Learners

The main subject of teaching is the learner. Understanding students' needs and identifying their learning motivation, New Oriental teachers have made many efforts to inspire learners, and transformation begins here.

004 Telling Stories and Chatting Lightly

New Oriental teachers seem to be associated with being talkative and fond of telling jokes. The relationship between teachers and students becomes closer through interaction and laughter.

005 Transformation of the Teaching Profession

New Oriental teachers can earn higher salaries, and at one time, it was also a stepping stone for going abroad or changing careers. There are various possibilities here.

006 Transferable Skills

In the face of uncertainty and career transitions, how to make choices amidst many factors? New Oriental cultivates transferable skills in teachers, such as public speaking, understanding needs, and refining details.

007 No Genius, Only Standards

Lessons should have word-for-word scripts, and courses are refined repeatedly. Perhaps a lesson that comes naturally has been taught over a hundred times, with repeated practice, no talent, no shortcuts.

008 Encouraging Exploration and Innovation

There are no absolute requirements. From standardized courses to unique ones, New Oriental encourages exploration and iteration, offering teachers more possibilities through different training methods.

009 Small Goals, Long Accumulation

From overseas study exams to teaching goals, small goals catalyze a larger educational vision. Everything comes down to accumulation, with countless people served over 20 years.

010 Setting Sail

New Oriental has experienced ups and downs on its journey, leading a group of people to explore better education, eliminating obstacles, catalyzing iteration, and full of anticipation.
