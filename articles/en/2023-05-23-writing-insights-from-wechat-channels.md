Short videos don't have to be posted daily, and live streams don't need to be broadcast every day. Decide based on your own situation. After all, if you can't sustain it, there's no accumulation to speak of. For example, when I write for my knowledge planet, I often write several pieces at once, focusing on content expression rather than chasing quantity.

Content should be simple and easy to understand. For me, elaborate language and elegant writing style are extremely difficult. I choose to practice precise expression—logical and evidence-based. From the beginning, I made this clear, and later I wouldn't second-guess.

Short videos may not need excessive repetition, but live streams require a lot of repetition. A single story can be told in 100 different versions. When users come in, they often don't know what's going on, and they end up scrolling away without realizing it.

Content is the core. Value transmission needs to be perceived by the user, and usefulness is a key point. For instance, in my consulting work, I've accumulated many cases, which allows me to help users solve problems more quickly. I also repeatedly think about whether my writing is helpful to those in need.

Repeatedly refining high-quality content is always worth emphasizing. After all, this is your long-term inevitable choice. For example, if you make a funny video or ride on a trending topic, the users you attract are likely not interested in your other content, unless you remain consistent.

Inspiring users is powerful. Confidence, courage, and hope are three key words. If you can give someone a little gain, it's extremely valuable. One feedback I received in WeChat Channels business was giving people confidence and motivation to take action. Every time I get such a response, I feel particularly excited.

Creation is based on a long-term choice. After all, a diligent and content-rich producer won't do too badly.

Create while earning. It sounds unrealistic, but you should believe you can. "Gongshenruju" (to immerse oneself in the situation) is the choice of the practical, and it's also a necessary requirement of long-termism. For example, through WeChat Channels, I've met many people, and I've tried selling books, live stream equipment, and courses. In the process, I've received more feedback and income, which has helped me improve further.

Find your target, and study thoroughly. Study good works, analyze deeply, and break down the work itself, like watching a movie. Most people focus on the plot, but few observe the filming methods, creative thinking, or even the budget. From the perspective of observation and study, you can see deeper.

Don't worry that your research ability is insufficient and you'll gain nothing. Basically, we rarely have strong research abilities. Find a specific thing, and improve as you go. For example, study the next viral video: "My Ten Years/…Years."

Researching an industry also involves collecting information from classic books and literature. WeChat Channels can do this too. While searching, you can dig up information. For example, you can analyze how the same blogger operates on different platforms.

I highly recommend Li Xiaolai's book *WeChat Internet Plain People Entrepreneurship*. It offers a systematic and comprehensive analysis with a unique perspective and a plain, logical writing style. It tells you how to do WeChat Channels, and more importantly, how to make money with WeChat.

Sharing is also creating. Through sharing and interaction, you co-create content. In my community, through interaction and exchange, I've accumulated a lot of material, including many questions and cases, which allows me to continuously produce content and always have creative material.

Repeat, repeat, and repeat again. There's no shortcut for anything. Just solidly train your basic skills. After making over 1,000 videos, I've started to feel a bit more. Start with quantity, and gradually improve quality. Keep doing it, keep repeating it.

Life itself can be a form of creation. Treat creation as a top priority. Never underestimate your observation. Record it down. For example, my articles—right now, I still have over 20 pieces in stock.

Don't let laziness hold you back. Set up mechanisms. For example, with knowledge planet articles, recently, due to writing a paper, I've mainly been working on long articles and English logical writing training. I've set a goal to produce 365 articles this year, maintaining my frequency at 215, and I'm fine with that.

Professional content shouldn't be limited by professionalism. This immediately made me think of marketing. Simply explaining marketing theory or cases is very limited. Step out of that, and realize that "marketing" comes from "management." This point is deeply studied by Xiao Ma Song teacher in the book *Marketing Notes* (Xiao Ma Song - *Marketing Notes*).

Piaohan Yizhi Cat, Xiao Ma Song, shared that content should be considered for different groups and audiences. Three roles should always be in mind: expert, coach, and rich person. This is very important for positioning as a video creator. Have you thought about your role yet?

A successful product-selling article: benefit, example, reasoning, and power. These are the four core elements of writing. *One Year Equals Ten Years* is a classic that consistently integrates these four elements. I also frequently read this book by Xiao Ma Song and gained new insights. As long as you achieve these four points, WeChat Channels has no reason to be done poorly. You're on the path to becoming better.

Life should be chosen based on creation. If you want to film something, create the scene as much as possible. For example, find the most famous trainer at a gym, the best hairdresser at a salon, and create a scene there for research. While writing for my knowledge planet, I focused on WeChat Channels and immersed myself in the corresponding scene for input and output.

Short-term intensive creation—secret: don't work (have enough time). Be stimulated, be pushed, set up mechanisms. I also did a lot of input and output during a one-month training session in July, producing over ten thousand words in English, which shocked me myself.

I hope this gives you some inspiration.
