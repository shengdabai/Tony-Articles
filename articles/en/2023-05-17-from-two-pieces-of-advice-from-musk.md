Musk's advice is simple: reading and communication can teach us everything. This theme inspires us to emphasize learning and exchange in self-media content creation, which is crucial for our growth and progress.

Lifelong learners must continuously acquire new knowledge and master new skills to enhance their creative power and professional level. For example, an influencer improved their photography skills by constantly learning new software and techniques, resulting in more professional and outstanding photographic works.

Reading is a highly effective way to learn, helping us expand our knowledge base, enhance our thinking abilities, and boost creativity. For instance, by reading biographies of famous people and scientific literature, one can gain more inspiration and materials for their own creations.

Communication is also a vital learning method, allowing us to deeply understand others' thoughts and experiences, and share our own ideas and feelings. For example, an influencer actively participated in various social events and professional training, interacting with other media professionals, thereby gaining more connections and resources.

Learning will never disappoint us. We need to draw wisdom and inspiration from our own reading and communication experiences to create more brilliant and unique content. For example, by attending lectures and exchange meetings, one can gain more insights into current events and global trends, leading to the creation of respected in-depth reports and commentaries.

Learning can cultivate our thinking and creativity, making us smarter and more innovative. Influencers need to find their own learning methods and techniques to enhance their professional level and broaden their horizons. For example, an influencer enjoys learning new topics and creative approaches through documentaries and nature photography.

Reading and communication are two essential aspects of learning, enabling us to continuously improve our creative power and professional level in self-media content creation. Through continuous learning, deep exchanges, broadened perspectives, mastery of new skills, and constant enrichment of our knowledge and experience, we can stand out in an increasingly competitive content creation market.

According to the 2022 release of the *19th National Survey on National Reading*, the average number of physical books read per person in China was 4.76, while adults spent five times as much time on online entertainment as reading. The rise of various "reading influencers" and AI has further made reading more fragmented and superficial.

In a recent set of data, the average daily reading time for Chinese people was only 21.05 minutes, compared to 101.12 minutes spent on mobile phones daily.

My suggestion is to make reading a productive form of reading, rather than merely a consumerist one.

Productive reading refers to the process of intentionally selecting reading materials, literature, and books to solve real-life and work-related problems, in contrast to consumerist reading, which is more goal-oriented.

Here are a few examples:
(1) A sales manager looking for the key skills his team lacks might read professional books, study market trends, and examine successful cases from his peers in similar situations.
(2) A law intern preparing for her first trial might read relevant legal texts, review past cases, and discuss strategies with experienced colleagues to better serve her clients.
(3) A marketing expert wanting to better utilize market promotion tools might read articles on digital marketing, search engine optimization, and advertising, learning about the latest trends or case studies.

Productive reading is a targeted reading process related to specific goals and needs. It helps us better solve real problems or improve our skills and knowledge, and can be applied across many fields and professions.

Self-media content creation places more emphasis on marketing and entertainment, with content being more casual and free. For example, many self-media influencers intentionally create "controversy" and "hot topics" to attract attention. The competition in the self-media field is fierce, and content diversity and quality vary widely. Many creators use clickbait tactics to attract attention, leading to misinformation and wasted time for readers.

In contrast, productive reading focuses more on the practicality and operability of information. It is not aimed at attracting attention but at solving real problems. Readers need to make targeted choices based on their own life or work scenarios.

In productive reading, the relationship between the source and the reader is relatively stable. Some official accounts and institutions provide more authoritative and reliable book information. Readers don't have to search through a sea of information for accurate resources, saving time and effort and allowing them to focus more on solving real problems.

Productive reading and self-media content creation are not contradictory; each has its own specific role and positioning. Readers should distinguish between reading resources and focus on matching their actual needs and scenarios. At the same time, self-media creators need to consider the value and quality of their content, adding more meaningful thought and research.

We now live in an age of fragmentation, where we are constantly exposed to a large amount of fragmented information. Social media, news apps, and short videos all provide fragmented reading materials.

However, facing a large amount of fragmented information, we can choose to rationally select the information we need, building our own knowledge system rather than having a superficial understanding of it.

In an era where this ability is increasingly important, self-media, as a form of creation, is also striving to meet readers' needs and provide better interactive experiences and higher-quality content.

At the same time, self-media is also the "main culprit" behind fragmented reading. Many self-media platforms and platforms like them like to release content in segments, letting readers only read the parts they are interested in, which brings some negative impacts.

First, fragmented content is not conducive to deep reading and thinking. Most self-media platforms, in order to capture users' time and attention, tend to release short, fast, and interesting content, making it difficult for readers to spend time deeply understanding a topic or a viewpoint.

Second, fragmented content can easily lead to low-quality content. The competition in the self-media industry is fierce, and to gain more traffic and followers, many self-media creators use eye-catching titles and images. However, this short-term approach to gaining attention does not bring long-term value.

Therefore, if we want to effectively respond to and utilize fragmented reading, we need a certain ability—filtering and selecting information. Only by grasping the essence and discarding redundancy can we accurately acquire knowledge and improve our quality and competitiveness.

To gather ideas from a lot of reading, learning, and thinking, and finally output a piece of valuable content.

Creating valuable content is not a simple process. It requires a thorough understanding and preparation of the content you want to write, deep thinking, and practical testing. In this process, the time and effort invested are considerable, requiring continuous learning and reflection.

During the input process, a lot of time and effort must be spent reading various materials from different fields. Not only do these materials help enrich your knowledge and make your views more solid, but they also allow you to collect various materials.

To "output 1," you need deep thinking, integrate your ideas systematically, and then express them clearly and smoothly, ultimately creating content with uniqueness and value. This process requires establishing your own column and content refinement workflow, gradually tracking your work results, further improving product quality, and attracting more readers.

To illustrate with the operation of a self-media account, simply posting some simple news is difficult to gain user attention and reading; whereas creating excellent content, building your own reading system, and regularly updating high-quality content can win more fans and users.

In addition, a media account is not just a simple information transmission platform; it is also a public service and interactive system. Creators must pay attention to the information push, reading habits, and requirements of the user group, and continuously learn and improve in these areas to enhance content quality and traffic.

However, it should be noted that the goal is to "output 1," but in actual work, we often need to conduct repeated experiments and tests, continuously modify and optimize our work to finally create content with value. Therefore, during the input process, we also need to pay attention to planning and integration, with a more systematic plan to improve work efficiency in practice and better achieve the goal.

"Input 100, output 1" is not a single process, but one that requires mastering various skills and applying them. In self-media creation, creators need to deeply study various materials, think about problems, and publish valuable works, continuously experimenting and seeking innovation points to enhance their influence in the self-media field and gain more users and followers.

The same tool, different outputs.

With the help of AI-assisted writing tools, content output varies greatly, so having a basic ability is very important.

Writers, like ordinary people, can use AI-assisted writing tools, but excellent writers have more basic abilities and writing skills.

Basic abilities include, but are not limited to, language and literary literacy, logical thinking ability, and literary cultivation.

Through continuous practice, basic abilities can be improved, thereby enhancing the quality of the work. For example, Yang Ji, a poet from the Ming Dynasty, widely studied various disciplines before dedicating himself to literature, laying a solid foundation for his later writing.

While using AI-assisted writing tools, it is also necessary to continuously improve one's writing style. For instance, Qian Zhongshu improved his creative level by studying classical literature such as Tang and Song poetry.

Reading is also a method to enhance basic abilities, allowing one to learn various writing techniques and good expression methods from reading.

Only through continuous practice and improvement of basic abilities can one write excellent works, and the use of AI-assisted writing tools also requires the support of basic abilities.

Read well, communicate often, share continuously, and the value is immense.
