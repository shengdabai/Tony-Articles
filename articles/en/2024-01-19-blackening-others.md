# Tearing Other People Down

Why do people like to attack big influencers to gain attention? —— A Dilemma Between Speech and Personal Development

A phenomenon has emerged on social media: many people enjoy attacking big influencers to attract attention and gain traffic. They believe that as long as they have something to say, they can spark controversy and eventually become popular. However, this shortcut comes with a hidden risk: we often make mistakes in our speech.

The Psychological Need for Attention

People like to attack big influencers for traffic because they crave being the center of attention and gaining others' recognition and attention. With information overload on social media, standing out on this big stage often requires some sensational effect. By attacking big influencers, people try to highlight their own presence and value, calling attention to themselves.

A Psychological Defense Against Risk

On the other hand, attacking big influencers is also a form of psychological defense. People try to shift their focus by attacking others, avoiding the risks and pressures they might face themselves. Big influencers are usually highly exposed, and every move they make is under scrutiny and judgment.

Ordinary people, however, may face more criticism and negative feedback if they make mistakes in their speech, which could even affect their future development. Therefore, by taking the side of the attacker, people try to protect themselves from the discomfort of negative evaluation.

Balancing Speech and Personal Development

While attacking big influencers can bring temporary attention, this attention is often negative, offering traffic rather than genuine recognition. At the same time, we should understand that making mistakes is inevitable; everyone may have moments of misstatement. To truly build a reputation and influence, we need to earn others' trust through positive speech and action.

Attacking big influencers to gain traffic satisfies some psychological needs, but it also brings risks and dilemmas.

We should recognize the power of speech and the importance of personal development. When attacking others, we should remain rational and objective, while also being mindful of our own words and actions, to build a real reputation and influence in a positive way.

Unveiling the Psychology Behind Attacking Others: The Truth Behind the Pursuit of Attention

We often see people who enjoy attacking others, whether they are celebrities, political figures, or ordinary people. These people seem to only seek temporary attention and hype, without caring about whether their methods or words hurt others.

Attacking others often brings a lot of attention and discussion. On social media, attention is often a key indicator of one's influence.

Some people, driven by the desire for hype and attention, choose to attack others to attract eyes and spark discussions. They believe that as long as they are talked about, whether positively or negatively, their existence becomes more prominent.

Attacking others often gives people a sense of psychological satisfaction. When we criticize others, we may feel smarter and more superior. This behavior of blaming others makes us feel better, boosting our self-esteem and confidence.

This psychological satisfaction leads some people to enjoy attacking others, whether out of a sense of comparison or to satisfy their vanity.

The act of attacking others may also stem from a lack of understanding and respect for others. Some people may not realize that their words and actions can hurt others and cause negative impacts.

They believe that as long as they gain attention, regardless of the means, it's acceptable. A lack of understanding and respect for others leads them to use aggressive language to achieve their goals.

Although attacking others for attention is accepted by some, we should be aware of the unhealthy motivations behind this behavior.

To build a more friendly and respectful society, we need to value mutual understanding and care. Focusing our attention on positive and meaningful things can not only enhance our own happiness but also spread positive energy to those around us. Choose to focus on things that truly have value and meaning.

Seeking Users with Shared Values — The Key to Precision Marketing

In today's increasingly competitive market, precision marketing has become one of the key factors determining whether a business can succeed. To achieve precision marketing, we need to find users who align with our brand and share similar values.

Users who share our brand values are more likely to accept our brand's philosophy. If a user's values align with ours, they are more psychologically receptive to the information and ideas we convey, thus developing a sense of identification and loyalty toward our brand.

In contrast, if a user's values differ significantly from ours, they are more likely to resist our brand when they encounter it. Therefore, by finding users who share our values, we can more precisely communicate our brand's philosophy and culture.

Users who share our brand values are more likely to become loyal customers. If a user's values align with ours, they are more likely to become a loyal customer, even a brand advocate.

By building connections with users who share our brand values, we can better attract potential customers, retain existing ones, and enhance our brand's reputation.

Building precision marketing through data analysis and careful observation. Finding users who align with our brand and share similar values is not an easy task. However, through data analysis and careful observation, we can more accurately determine which users are more suitable for our brand.

For example, we can use social media analysis tools, consumer profiles, and purchasing behavior to gather data and identify our target audience. At the same time, we also need to follow up on user behavior and feedback to understand their preferences and needs, so we can better serve and promote our products.

Seeking users who align with our brand and share similar values plays an important role in building precision marketing. When identifying target users, we can use data analysis and careful observation to determine our target audience and better understand their needs and preferences.

Only by finding users who align with our brand and share similar values can we gain a greater chance of success in a competitive market.
