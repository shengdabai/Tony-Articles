**Reading, Fitness, Investing, Helping Friends, Being with Family**

**Five Core Values — Which One Do You Disagree With?**

**Reading**: You know the importance of reading, but how long can you keep reading? How many books have you read? How many have actually changed your actions? Often, reading lacks motivation, and you end up giving up halfway. Is a book an asset or a liability to us? Look at the books on your shelf, covered in dust — I think you've already formed your own opinion.

Reading is often seen as a personal experience, but how long can individual action last? The excuse of being busy and not reading is something we all face frequently. If you want to keep reading, it's not that hard — just believe that reading is important enough. Important enough that if someone stops you from reading, you'll get angry with them. Only when we assign enough significance to something will we be moved to act. More importantly, you need to spend time and effort to read seriously.

Reading is just reading. Why can't you keep going? Alone, your mind may fluctuate, and it's okay not to read. But with a group? Not only do you read, but you also express your thoughts. Writing is the way we express our voices, record our perceptions, and share them with others.

There are people who read continuously, reading whenever they have time or even when they don't. No matter what happens, they just keep reading — they can't stop!

**Fitness**: This word sounds professional and might make people hesitate. Let's say it another way — exercise! Still hesitant? Then let's call it "working out." Do you work out? Do you work out every day? Have you clearly thought about why you need to?

If working out is just to lose weight and look better, that's not enough of a motivation for me. For me, the motivation comes from living a healthy and happy life, with a good body to welcome future wealth and a rich life ahead. This makes me excited, and I can make working out simple enough to do every day.

Doing 100 push-ups a day, running five kilometers, or working out for half an hour at the gym — these all sound exciting, but how many can actually do them? Break down your actions: one push-up, tie your shoes, choose a gym near your home — making the first step easy enough to take in just two minutes. Then, let micro-habits help us keep moving.

A group of people, working out every day, showing off their results — what do you think?

**Investing**: For ordinary people, investing may be the best, if not the only, way to cross class boundaries!

You might already be investing, and you've had various experiences. When prices fluctuate, what do you think? The fixed investment strategy has been proven to be the simplest for ordinary people to execute, and its returns are also considerable. You may know about fixed investing, and you might want to try it — but what should you invest in?

Choosing quality investment targets — real estate, funds, stocks — how would you choose? Should we follow the big names? Apple, Moutai, Coca-Cola — these are all quality stocks. Can you really afford to start investing in them? If you're not sure, you can look it up.

A group of people, continuously investing, undeterred by storms or price fluctuations. They just keep reading, working out, doing what they need to — and investing.

**Helping Friends**: "Wherever there are friends, the world is near!"

Every day, offer a little value to your friends — helping with something, chatting, or doing anything else. Giving value to your friends.

Joining communities and learning platforms, I've met many friends. They call me "Tony," and they feel warm. Why? Because when I give value to others, I already gain something in the process. I enjoy it. I always believe: **Helping others is helping yourself.**

A group of people, actively sharing, helping friends around them. If you need anything, they'd be willing to give everything. As long as you want to learn, nothing is impossible!

**Being with Family**: Family is our first connection to the world and will be our longest *tugging at heart* in the future.

After the pandemic, I'm most grateful for the time I've spent with my family. Being with them is the longest and most sincere form of affection. Being with family, sharing the beauty of life together.

A group of people, no matter how busy or not, will chat with their family, call them, and show care. Being with family is their choice.
