Note 102

01 WeChat Video, no matter how far we go, there is only one point we must pay attention to: content!

02 They give you traffic, they give you followers. Can we really keep them? Attractive visuals, exquisite production, cool visuals... these may be important, but can they definitely not be a burden?

03 In most cases, the right thing is just a few words. As a Video Creator, as an ordinary internet citizen, our characteristic is being ordinary. Ordinary people should do their unique content well. Ordinary is the best content.

04 WeChat has already surpassed a social platform and evolved into a commercial world. Within the WeChat ecosystem, all commercial links are seamlessly connected. Here, it's natural to do business. Are you going to miss out?

05 Video is an excellent opportunity for ordinary people to turn things around on the internet. I don't have a good feeling about the word "turning things around." We are evolving, and Video is one of the tools.

06 In the long run, you will accumulate followers, content, and value on Video. When you have 100 loyal fans, 1,000 loyal fans, or even more, can you imagine the value behind that?

07 Amidst the dazzling world and complex network, you and I should still stay grounded, do content well, and be a WeChat merchant.

I've finished writing. Thank you for your likes and support!
