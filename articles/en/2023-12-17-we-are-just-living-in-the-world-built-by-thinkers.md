# We Are Just Living in the World Built by Thinkers

> Published: 2023-12-17 · [中文](../zh/2023-12-17-%E6%88%91%E4%BB%AC%E5%8F%AA%E6%98%AF%E6%B4%BB%E5%9C%A8%E6%80%9D%E6%83%B3%E5%AE%B6%E6%9E%84%E7%AD%91%E7%9A%84%E4%B8%96%E7%95%8C.md) | [English](../en/2023-12-17-we-are-just-living-in-the-world-built-by-thinkers.md)

---

The profound insights offered by great thinkers and philosophers are undoubtedly an indispensable spiritual treasure in the world.

But we should also realize that we are not just living in a symbolic world—we are living in a world constructed and defined by thinkers.

All of our thinking depends on symbols and language. These symbols and languages are defined by our surrounding culture and society.

For example, when we see a red apple, we describe it as "red." This "red" is merely a linguistic symbol decided by humans, not an objective reality.

In this way, from vocabulary and language to social customs and cultural backgrounds, all concepts and definitions are rooted in human culture and history. Therefore, all of our thinking and actions take place within this symbolic world.

A large number of thinkers and philosophers have built our understanding of the world. Their ideas and perspectives have shaped our culture and play a crucial role in our values and behavioral patterns.

For instance, Descartes' "I think, therefore I am" can be seen as defining the notion of personal mastery over one's life, while Adam Smith, the founder of the London School of Economics, influenced the Western economic model and established the framework of free-market capitalism.

Ideas and beliefs not only shape how we perceive the world but also influence our actions. Human beliefs and values have a significant impact on behavior.

Today, technological and scientific development has, to some extent, freed itself from the constraints of history and culture, yet thinkers continue to shape our values.

We live in a world built by thinkers. This does not only mean that we rely on language and symbols to understand the world, but also that thinkers have profoundly influenced our culture and values.

But in fact, this influence of thought and culture has certain limitations. Therefore, we still need to think carefully in our actions, otherwise they will act like another power we cannot escape, taking away our key decision-making authority.

As part of humanity, our understanding of the world has been enriched by the wisdom of thinkers. Their ideas and perspectives provide us with new ways of seeing and understanding, allowing us to gain a deeper insight into this diverse world.

Great thinkers often push the boundaries of human thought through rational reflection. They have proposed various views and theories, such as Plato's world of ideas, Nietzsche's concept of the Übermensch, and Kant's notions of reason and free will. These thinkers' wisdom inspires us to understand the world from a more macro and profound perspective, rather than remaining stuck in surface-level phenomena.

The ideas and perspectives of thinkers can help us transcend our cultural backgrounds and limitations, enabling cross-cultural dialogue and understanding. For example, the concept of cultural relativism highlights the differences in perspectives across cultures. This way of understanding makes us more open and inclusive, allowing us to respect different cultural traditions and viewpoints.

The wisdom of thinkers is not limited to academic and theoretical levels—it can also influence our personal growth and the meaning of our lives. For instance, Zhuangzi's attitude of freedom and reflection can help us balance internal and external pressures, cultivating inner peace and happiness; while Descartes' "I think, therefore I am" encourages us to think independently and pursue truth. These thinkers' wisdom guide and inspire us in our daily lives.

The wisdom of thinkers not only broadens our ways of understanding the world, but also inspires our personal growth and the meaning of our lives. Through their rational reasoning, cross-cultural dialogue, and personal growth insights, we can gain a deeper understanding of this diverse and complex world.

In a busy world filled with various information, we often overlook the significance and value of thinking about life. Philosophy, as a deep and ancient discipline, offers us a new way to understand the world and ourselves.

Whether in the West or the East, philosophy has always sought to explore the meaning of life. Philosophers such as Plato, Aristotle, Zhuangzi, and Mencius have proposed unique views and theories, guiding us to explore the meaning and value of life more deeply. Through studying philosophy, we can stimulate our ability to think critically, reflect on the deeper questions of life, and find our own meaning in life.

Philosophy is not only a tool for exploring the meaning of life but can also help us broaden our perspective on the world. For example, Kant's idea of enlightenment emphasizes the importance of independent thinking, while Freud's theory of psychoanalysis reveals the deeper psychological mechanisms of humans. Studying philosophy allows us to go beyond surface phenomena and gain a more comprehensive understanding of this complex and diverse world.

Studying philosophy cultivates our logical thinking and critical thinking abilities, helping us better solve problems. For instance, Socrates' dialectical method and Aristotle's logic provide us with tools for thinking and analyzing problems. These philosophical approaches can help us think more comprehensively and solve life's difficulties, enhancing our wisdom and achievements.

Philosophy is an important way for us to understand the world and ourselves. Through the study of philosophy, we can explore the deeper meaning and value of life, broaden our perspective on the world, and improve our ability to think and solve problems.

Whether in daily life or professional development, the wisdom of philosophy holds significant guiding value. Starting a journey of inner exploration can make our lives more meaningful and rich.
