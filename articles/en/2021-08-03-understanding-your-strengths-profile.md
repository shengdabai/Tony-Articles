II. Communicate with your partner, reflect on the past, and share your strength examples

During a team design task in university, I could stay at the office from 7 AM every day, eat lunch for no more than half an hour, and work until 11 PM every night. This consistent state allowed my teammates to believe I had strong learning abilities, confidence in tasks, and the capacity to maintain long-term focus. I also enjoyed sharing and helping others, and communication was one of my favorite things. This led me to pursue a career in education after graduation. The teachers in the team all thought I had strong learning abilities, and whenever there were problems, they would come to me for discussions. I was open-minded and willing to try various new teaching methods, using diverse and interesting teaching tools that often sparked deep thinking among others.

III. Summary

Personal Development Suggestions
● Recognize the value of others' opinions
● Balance your work and personal life
● Seek feedback and suggestions
● Learn to appreciate others
● Learn when to let go of unrealistic ideas
● Pay more attention to the impact of your thoughts on others

The above information comes from various assessments and conversations with people around me. Overall, I am passionate about learning, and lifelong learning is my enduring motivation. I also enjoy sharing and expressing myself. Tests are only relative references; results might change after a few months, so don’t take them too seriously. Give yourself more time to understand yourself. Through the course on the Ideation World, I gained a clearer understanding of what I truly want. When facing problems, first ask yourself what your inner voice is saying. Be present in the moment, and break down problems using rationalized thinking or step-by-step operations.
