# The Moment of Sharing Is Already a Harvest

> Published: 2021-09-26 · [中文](../zh/2021-09-26-%E5%88%86%E4%BA%AB%E7%9A%84%E7%9E%AC%E9%97%B4%E5%B7%B2%E7%84%B6%E6%94%B6%E8%8E%B7.md) | [English](../en/2021-09-26-the-moment-of-sharing-is-already-a-harvest.md)

---

The word "sharing" has probably been said to you countless times. Do you have your own feelings about it?

There was a friend of mine who, while chatting with you, would suddenly start his performance: You should do this, you should do that, listen to me...

After listening, you'd probably feel uncomfortable, and maybe even annoyed. But if he instead said, "Let me share a bit of my thoughts..." wouldn't that be easier to accept?

People who like to give advice are often a sign of low emotional intelligence. Like me, I'm one of them. Now I've learned to control this impulse, to listen more and give advice less.

However, people around me often say I'm like Doraemon, all-knowing, and that I can answer anything they ask. It sounds great, and I might feel happy for a while. But upon reflection, I realize I'm just trying to be helpful.

My abilities have limits, and learning to say no is essential. Sometimes I agree to help without thinking, but solving the problem actually takes a lot of effort.

Sharing should be within my capacity, and I shouldn't share too easily or talk endlessly. The reason is to consider the other person's perspective. What seems simple to me might take a long time for them. Don't think only from your own point of view.

My reason for sharing is simple: to share what I know and what I have with others.

When I ask myself, "What do I gain from sharing?" my answer is: the moment of sharing is already a harvest. I gain friendship, I refine my skills, and I open up infinite possibilities.

There's nothing to hide. Helping others is helping myself. Don't be afraid of the hassle. If you can simplify complex problems and make it easier for friends to get started, isn't that a talent?

In short, sharing is one secret: be a thoughtful person.

When you come across good content or find useful resources, collect them right away. For example, in WeChat, just save them. You can search for what you need in the saved page. Use cloud storage for files, and take notes for text and images. It doesn't have to be these specific tools—just do what's convenient.

How can you use software and websites efficiently? Simply put, use them yourself. Don't be afraid of the hassle. Try out all the functions and become proficient with the most useful ones. If you don't know how to operate them, trust the power of the internet. There are so many tutorials—just search for them.

Actually, the most important thing when looking for resources is to be willing to spend time. Do you think it's worth the time? I believe it must be. I've used some resources that greatly improved my later learning efficiency. Plus, it helps friends avoid unnecessary detours. Why not?

Sharing is also an exchange of resources. Finding like-minded friends and sharing with each other will surely lead to surprising discoveries.

Of course, in the internet age, there are so many resources. As long as you really put your heart into searching, you can definitely find them. If you still can't, just follow me and add me as a friend!

Finally, time is the ultimate test. So, let's make good use of it. Goodnight 😊
