Tony was in middle school from seventh grade to senior year, which coincided with Victor's healthy and happy growth period. For six years, he mostly lived with his parents, and later attended preschool for over three years. There was once a very dangerous incident between Victor and his father.

At that time, his parents were running a restaurant, and the business was still going well, with a busy daily routine. The restaurant specialized in Sichuan cuisine and donkey meat with yellow wine. Speaking of yellow wine, Tony occasionally helped out at the restaurant, doing only one thing—boiling yellow wine. How does one boil yellow wine? You pour a bottle of packaged yellow wine into a copper pot, then add slices of ginger, red dates, longan, and a bit of rock sugar in sequence. After boiling, the aroma is so enticing it's almost intoxicating. Another specialty of the restaurant was donkey meat, prepared with a secret recipe and simmered for dozens of hours each day. Obviously, such an important task could only be done by Dad. One day, Dad, as usual, brought the fresh donkey meat back to the processing room, and Victor accompanied him to prepare the donkey meat.

The processing room was small, with only a simple wooden bed and a large pot for simmering the donkey meat. Considering the heat, a gas cylinder was used for continuous heating. That day, Dad followed his usual method, prepared everything, then lay down on the bed to rest for a while, while Victor played nearby. However, the weather was quite cold that day, so Dad closed the window. Unbeknownst to him, the gas cylinder started leaking. Victor, who was less than three years old, smelled the strange odor and began to cry loudly. Dad was awakened by the noise and suddenly realized the gas leak. He quickly turned off the flame, opened the window, and took Victor outside. This time, it was really a close call. Victor averted the danger, like an angel.

Life at the restaurant was also very busy. When lunchtime got too hectic, Victor would go to the small supermarket nearby and play with the boss's child. Why did he choose to go to the supermarket? You know why—every child loves candy. Of course, humans actually all need sugar. There's a natural craving for it, which might be why people of all ages still enjoy that sweet taste. However, human genes have not changed much over the years, and we still have a strong demand for sugar. In ancient times, a little sugar was enough, but today, we can easily access all kinds of sugar, making it so common that it's almost normal. Here's a cold fact: in the past, only the nobility in Western countries could enjoy afternoon tea. To show their status, they would add ingredients that were hard to obtain at the time—sugar. As a result, their teeth turned black one by one, and they even considered it a sign of noble status.

Before starting preschool, Victor spent most of his time at home, and even more so at the restaurant, playing happily. Honestly, he didn't receive any formal education, just simple and pure joy in play. On another level, his personality became more outgoing, making him a popular kid among his peers.
