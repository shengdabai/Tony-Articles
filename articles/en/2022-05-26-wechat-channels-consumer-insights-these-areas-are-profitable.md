WeChat Channels Consumer Insights #Live Streaming #Short Video #Tools#

01 In the past year, the sales revenue of live-streamed product sales on WeChat Channels has grown 15 times. More brands and merchants are joining WeChat Channels, and for us with limited online experience, it's crucial to seize the opportunity and invest in WeChat Channels to establish our own territory.

02 People are increasingly focused on individuality—loving food, beauty, and fashion. Their demand for products is shifting from the material to the spiritual level.

03 The age and related attributes of WeChat Channels's consumer base should be analyzed based on the account itself. Overall, the main demographic is middle-aged people, so there will be more demand in areas like maternal and infant care and wellness.

04 The female consumer group is gradually taking over the majority of e-commerce shopping. Their pursuit of refined and beautiful living is something we need to pay attention to in content creation and product selection.

05 Don't overlook everyday consumer goods, such as food categories. You can refer to relevant data and content to find a more suitable positioning for yourself. It's also important to reasonably select low-priced products.

06 According to Youwang data, 60.13% of products sold in WeChat Channels live streams come from small shops. The convenience of one-click product promotion allows many creators without inventory to directly select products from small shops for resale and earn some commission.

07 Consumer insight data provides us with trends, but if you have a clear user base and target for your product, there's no need to worry too much. Just be a good merchant.
