"Education is not merely the transmission of knowledge, but the shaping of the future." The 2024 International Forum on Innovations in Basic Education was more than just a gathering of global education leaders—it marked the beginning of an exploration into the evolving landscape of education.

Core Themes:
1. **Digitalization of Education**: How is technology redefining learning?
2. **Curriculum Innovation**: How to integrate local culture with a global perspective?
3. **Teacher Training**: What new skills should future educators possess?
4. **Educational Equity**: How to build an inclusive learning environment?

Research Findings:
*The 2024 International Report on Innovations in Basic Education* accurately captures the pulse of global education. Eight key trends—from digital literacy to STEM education, from career planning to the transformation of the teacher's role—reflect the multidimensional nature of future education.

Intellectual Exchange:
At the forum, experts from diverse backgrounds engaged in deep discussions on how to adapt in the face of challenges and how to move forward amidst change. Their insights and strategies provided a practical framework for global educational innovation.

Looking Ahead:
The report outlines four key outlooks, pointing the way forward for educational development, especially emphasizing the importance of openness and collaboration. It calls on global partners to work together to build a more inclusive, equitable, and forward-looking educational ecosystem.

The International Forum on Innovations in Basic Education is not just an academic event—it represents the direction and hope for the future of education.

Basic education, as the starting line of personal growth, not only builds the foundation of knowledge but also serves as a crucial step in unlocking potential and shaping the future. In this era of rapid knowledge expansion and technological advancement, the significance of basic education for personal development has become increasingly evident.

Basic education is a critical period for building an individual's knowledge framework. It imparts fundamental subjects such as language, mathematics, and science, laying the necessary academic foundation for future learning and career development. Yet its value extends far beyond this:

1. **Cultivation of Cognitive Abilities**:

Basic education is the golden period for developing cognitive and logical thinking skills. Scientific evidence shows that the brain develops rapidly during childhood and adolescence. Through stimulating teaching activities and cognitive training, basic education promotes brain plasticity and fosters problem-solving abilities.

2. **Formation of Social Skills**:

School is a microcosm of society. During basic education, students learn social skills, teamwork, and leadership through interactions with peers. These skills are essential for success in both personal life and future careers.

3. **Shaping Values and Moral Outlook**:

Basic education is also a crucial period for shaping values and moral outlook. Through the study of history, culture, and civic education, students develop an understanding of social responsibility and the public good, laying the foundation for becoming contributing citizens.

4. **Development of Emotion and Identity**:

School education provides opportunities for self-expression and the discovery of personal interests. Art, sports, and other extracurricular activities help students develop their emotions and are also a process of exploring and confirming their identity.

5. **Adaptability and Foundation for Lifelong Learning**:

With the rapid changes in technology and society, adaptability has become one of the most important skills of the 21st century. The diversity and flexibility of basic education foster adaptability, laying the foundation for lifelong learning.

The value of basic education for personal development lies not only in the accumulation of knowledge but also in its role in providing individuals with the essential cognitive abilities, social skills, values, emotions, and adaptability needed to navigate the complexities of society and their careers. These are the foundations and wings that enable individuals to move forward.

Whether as students, parents, or educators, we all have a role to play in improving the quality of basic education, ensuring it provides a solid foundation for the all-around development of every individual.

In the journey of a child's growth, basic education is a crucial phase, and the guidance of parents is the compass in this process. Outside of school education, family education provides emotional support, values, and the initial learning of social norms, making it an inseparable part of basic education.

Parents play multiple and important roles in basic education.

Foundation for Emotional and Cognitive Development:
Parents are the first teachers in a child's early emotional and cognitive development. Through daily interactions, parents' language and behavior patterns serve as learning models, influencing the formation of a child's cognitive abilities and emotional well-being.

Shapers of Values and Behavioral Norms:
The family is the child's first classroom for learning values and social norms. Parents' beliefs, attitudes, and behaviors subtly teach children how to distinguish right from wrong and how to live harmoniously with others.

Cultivators of Learning Habits and Attitudes:
By setting rules and providing daily learning guidance, parents help children develop good learning habits, such as completing homework on time and developing reading habits. They also shape a positive learning attitude through encouragement and praise.

Guides for Social Skills and Emotional Management:
Parents play a decisive role in the development of a child's social skills and emotional management. They teach children how to express their emotions, how to understand and respect others' feelings, and how to navigate various social situations in daily life.

Basic education is not only about learning in the classroom; much of education takes place at home. Parents' influence runs through this process. Their words and actions lay a solid foundation for children in cognition, emotion, sociality, and morality, enabling them to better adapt to school education and social life.

Through daily interactions and by serving as role models, parents create an environment filled with love, inspiration, and positive energy. Let basic education become the solid foundation for a lifetime of success.
