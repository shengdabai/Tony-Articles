Personal branding is "reputation," and monetizing a personal brand is "profit." Since ancient times, people have long pursued and desired both reputation and profit. In terms of personal branding, I especially want to share two key points from Li Xiaolai's lectures:

1. Managing the sum of others' opinions about us
2. Inner qualities are more important than outward appearance

I would like to add two more words: **authenticity** and **not pretending**.

Personal branding is the embodiment of reputation, and it can be built in one's career and on social media. Take well-known figures as an example; their reputation has become their brand image. For instance, LeBron James' personal brand is embodied in his reputation and image as an NBA superstar, which allows him to reach a broader audience and exert influence across various marketing efforts.

Monetizing a personal brand means that the brand can generate income and profit. This can be achieved by distinguishing oneself, building a personal brand, and selling one's labor, thus creating a lifestyle of long-term, sustainable growth. For example, ainfluencer who gains popularity for explaining life skills on social media can attract more followers and sponsors as their fame grows, leading to more brand endorsements, advertising opportunities, and increased income.

In addition, self-employed individuals or business owners can also monetize their personal brands by placing their reputation at the forefront of profit conversion. For instance, self-employed lawyers or consultants, bloggers, and social media marketing experts can convert their knowledge and skills into higher-paying services.

Although reputation and profit have always existed, for a successful personal brand, reputation and contribution are always the top priorities. If a personal brand lacks contribution and visibility, profit is only temporary. For example, celebrities in various fields gained fame and recognition through relentless effort and dedication before becoming well-known, which is also the direction every person building a personal brand should consider and strive for.

Yang Tianzheng's Big Size Women's Clothing

Yang Tianzheng, a former agent turned brand founder, made a clear and precise positioning that left a strong impression. The target audience, product attributes, and positioning are clear: big size women's clothing. The user pain points they solve are also very direct, which is something many people overlook when developing products. Sometimes, when designing products, people don't start from the demand side but instead look at what others are selling and follow their own assumptions about what people need, which creates a distance from the actual users.

Previously, I consulted with a streamer who wanted to sell local specialties. She believed the product was excellent, but the question was whether users actually had such a demand. Had she done any research or listened to user opinions? The lack of clear positioning easily leads to the trap of trying to do everything, which is clearly illogical. For instance, myself, when I first started in the WeChat Channels direction, I thought I could do anything, but after consulting and managing projects, I realized that most users' problems stemmed from a lack of personal branding.

Initially, without building a complete business cycle, it was impossible to accumulate users. Even if videos and live streams became popular, it was hard to generate revenue. For example, take a female streamer—do the users she attracts actually have purchasing power? Focusing on brand positioning, combining one's strengths and characteristics, and doing long-term valuable work may not yield immediate benefits.

Personal branding is essentially about managing how others perceive you.

Building a personal brand requires viewing oneself objectively, understanding one's strengths and weaknesses, and continuously improving and enhancing one's image and performance. For example, in interviews and the workplace, one's image and performance can lead to higher evaluations and more opportunities.

In the process of building a personal brand, it's important to plan goals and steps based on one's own capabilities. For instance, Greta Thunberg, the Swedish environmental activist, built a highly respected personal brand by fighting for environmental causes and delivering public speeches continuously.

Focusing on one's online presence, such as blogs, social media, and websites, which are forms of online data, can help more people understand you and build your personal brand and reputation.

Building a personal brand requires combining it with one's production and professional capabilities. In specialized fields, one should focus on refining one's skills and knowledge rather than blindly following social trends. For example, Patrick Bet-David, a commercial analyst, successfully integrated his personal brand with strategic development by leveraging his personal abilities and professional experience, thereby establishing his brand.

Pay attention to collecting and integrating information. By following media, industry trends, and various information sources, one can understand the needs of the target audience and create a brand image that aligns with both personal characteristics and market demands.

It is also important to focus on interaction and communication with others. These interactions and communications are the foundation for building trust and close relationships, which require continuous maintenance and management. For example, musician Lu Siqing built his personal brand by showcasing his musical talents and skills at various concerts, establishing a solid connection and mutual trust with potential audiences.

Understand one's strengths and weaknesses, and develop a plan based on one's actual situation to reduce unnecessary risks. Through continuous effort and improvement, one can ultimately achieve personal goals and expectations, creating a personal brand with market competitiveness.

Inner Qualities Are More Important Than Appearance

One of the most important internal factors in personal branding is personal value and character. A good personal brand must be built on excellent performance, honesty, and responsibility toward others. For example, Elon Musk built his personal brand through his efforts and outstanding performance in Tesla and SpaceX.

Personal branding also requires a foundation in one's professional capabilities and expertise. Your abilities and knowledge are the core of your brand and the secret to your success in the workplace. For instance, Angie Brill, a top-tier programmer, hacker, and "technological savior" in the IT industry, showcased her technical talent and became a prominent figure in the industry, successfully building her personal brand.

When building a personal brand, it's important to convey the true self to the audience and continuously improve one's trust and credibility. In business, this is also true—brand awareness, reputation, and credibility are essential components of a business model, and they must be clearly demonstrated in interactions with customers or employees. For example, Kathleen Anne Rohach, a well-known diplomat in China, built her personal brand by embodying her image, with her most praised aspect being her approachable and kind demeanor, as well as her integrity and professionalism in handling major issues.

Building a good personal brand also requires focusing on self-improvement and growth, continuously learning and expanding one's skills and knowledge to meet the needs and expectations of the market and audience. For instance, Yuhang Tian, a well-known documentary director, constantly challenged his work domain, expanded his influence, and built a strong personal brand. It was precisely this relentless effort that made him an authoritative figure with decision-making power in the industry.

It's important to connect with more influential people or companies, which can help expand one's professional circle and increase personal visibility. For example, in the tech industry, attending various industry conferences and seminars can help build a personalized platform with industry peers, thus advancing brand development.

In personal branding, it's important to see oneself as an individual with personality and inner value, using one's experiences and stories to convey more information and emotions to the audience. For example, Simon Sinek, a well-known business speaker, built his brand on the values, passion, and desire of others. Through natural, steady speaking techniques, data support, and strong emotional power, he successfully conveyed his brand value to the audience.

Personal branding should be based on solid thinking and reasonable planning, with a greater meaning and goal as the premise. This planning can include strategies such as building social connections with professionals, choosing advertising presentation strategies, and maintaining stable communication platforms. Through one's own creativity and talent, a unique personal brand can be created.

How Master Dong Feng Builds a Personal Brand

Creating a personal brand is actually about building a "name," and personal brand monetization isn't anything but "profit," so I wonder how to build a "name"?

Dong Feng, courtesy name Junyi, was from Houguan (present-day Fuzhou in Fujian). A renowned physician of the Eastern Han, he was ranked alongside Zhang Ji of Nanyang and Hua Tuo of Qiao Commandery as one of the Three Divine Physicians of Jian'an.

According to the *Records of the Three Kingdoms: Biographies of Shi Xie*, it is noted that the governor of Jiao State, Shi Xie, fell ill with a severe disease and died for three days. Dong Feng used a self-made medicinal pill, placed one pill in Shi Xie's mouth, poured a little water into it, held his head and shook it to make him swallow. Soon, the seemingly dead Shi Xie opened his eyes miraculously, and could move his hands and feet. "After half a day he could sit up, after four days he could speak again, and eventually recovered completely." A person who was "already dead" was brought back to life by just one pill, demonstrating how extraordinary his medical skills were.

Dong Feng not only had excellent medical skills, but also his medical ethics and conduct were extremely high. According to Volume Ten of *The Classic of Immortals*, Dong Feng once lived in seclusion for a long time in the southern foot of Lushan Mountain in Jiangxi Province, treating the mountain people's illnesses. When he practiced medicine, he never asked for payment, but only required those who were lightly cured to plant one jujube tree, and those who were seriously cured to plant five jujube trees. Year after year, thousands of jujube trees grew around his house, lush and green, forming a continuous forest.

In the golden autumn season, after the apricots ripened, Dong Feng again sold the apricots for grain to supply the poor people of Lushan and the hungry travelers coming from north and south, rescuing as many as over twenty thousand people in a year. After Dong Feng's passing, the people of Lushan set up a shrine in the apricot grove to commemorate him, calling him "Dong Lin Apricot Fairy."

In this way, the term "Xinglin" gradually became a specialized term for physicians; phrases such as "Xinglin Chunnuan" and "Yu Man Xinglin" also became commonly used expressions to praise physicians with superb medical skills and high moral standards.

But remember the big sales taboo: only do sales

A person's sales ability cannot support all his matters. A good salesperson should not only excel at selling products but also possess various abilities and skills such as expanding customer base and solving problems.

A single sales action cannot bring substantial wealth to an individual, but it may affect their long-term independent survival and development. This also requires individuals to transform from salespeople into sellers, possessing professional knowledge, a fixed customer base, and other abilities such as expanding their network.

Personal sales must embody a creator's spirit, have innovative awareness, and have their own products; only by having their own products can one fully internalize the sales process, forming a long-term and stable profit model.

Individual sellers need to better explore their own potential and talents, discover their strengths, build their own brand, and constantly seek a balance between products and the market. They also need to truly master sales techniques and methods, regularly receive training and improve, adding practical value and long-term competitiveness to their careers.

Of course, if you have some additional skills, that will give you a stronger multidimensional competitiveness, for example design, writing, marketing, customer management, etc., thus expanding the business scope, improving your professional capabilities and opportunities.

Make sure to do personal sales well, especially important is to create a continuous and stable business model, which requires long-term strategic planning, trustworthy team collaboration and support, and excellent front and back office services, only then can you build a sustainable personal sales bank.

The sales subject matter is only one: oneself

Based on trust in others, the value of sales is given high credibility and reputation, salespeople need to establish a creative way for potential customers to believe in you, therefore it is important to demonstrate one's value and a certain degree of recognition from Dedao.

Many enterprises and individuals mainly focus on the features and price of products, but in reality, when others buy a product from you, they mainly consider the experience of purchasing and the trust in the transaction.

For example, in the hotel industry, many customers remain loyal to certain hotel brands because they trust the brand will provide high-quality customer service and experience.

While Apple Company has won users' absolute loyalty by establishing brand image and consumer trust, making Apple a successful brand and transforming it from a technology company into a consumer products company.

To build trust, salespeople need to demonstrate their professional competence, such as industry knowledge, practical experience, etc., to ensure customers believe you can provide them with the best service and solutions.

Helping customers solve real problems and establishing harmonious interpersonal relationships with them is another creative way to build trust. Salespeople should understand customers' needs and integrate them throughout the entire sales process.

Building personal connections with customers and maintaining long-term relationships is also an important way to build trust. Customers will be more willing to cooperate with salespeople they trust; not only will they sell you the product, but they will also recommend more potential customers to you.

Don't pretend, pretending means you have no real strength

False advertising will be exposed more and more every day, and it is well known that "fake is fake." When things become so unrealistic, it only harms oneself. Therefore, when running a brand and promoting products, do not overstate your fame or promotional achievements, but instead focus on enhancing your own strength and professional level, thereby gaining genuine recognition through actual performance.

If you are a team or business that is still developing, then do not mislead your consumers or employees. You should maintain rigor, avoid exaggerating performance, and refrain from using false rhetoric to cover up shortcomings. Instead, focus on ensuring that every consumer can experience high-quality products and services. Imagine you are a company that has also developed within the same industry; you would be more inclined to choose any brand that you believe has a moral bottom line and strong evidence-based credibility for collaboration.

If you want to achieve success in your career, the most important thing is to remain true to yourself and be honest. Those who dress themselves up and cover their weaknesses are likely to appear extremely incompetent when facing challenges and pressure. For example, one key factor in Jobs' founding of Apple was that he would never hide his mistakes or weaknesses; instead, he faced them directly and worked hard to improve his abilities and strength.

Attempting to create an unrealistic image or set up a value system will, in the long run, establish an unhealthy foundation for brand building. For example, in the sports field, the New Zealand rugby team has gained widespread acclaim for its very strict and firm values, and behind its success, it has built a large fan base.

If you feel insecure or voiceless during your career development, please do not copy or imitate others' styles or outfits. These actions may temporarily boost your reputation, but people will soon realize that this performance does not match your actual capabilities. Building a unique brand is a necessary condition for shaping a good self-image and brand value system.

Any aspiring professional who wants to succeed in the talent market should focus on improving their capabilities rather than overinvesting in image and superficiality. This is a long-term and essential investment, not a short-term strategy. For example, after Larry Page and Sergey Brin founded Google, they continuously worked to refine their flagship products and services, enhance their skills and knowledge, and stay ahead of the competition in the market.

Even successful brands cannot overlook their own shortcomings. Everyone has weaknesses, and the key lies in how we address, recognize, and improve them. As brands seek appropriate market visibility, they need to understand their weaknesses, accurately assess their strengths, and strive to enhance their capabilities. If we can learn to accept our shortcomings and build upon them, we might successfully shape a real and powerful brand image, earning the trust of the market and consumers.
