II. Communicate with Your Partner, Reflect on the Past, and Share Your Strengths Examples

During a team design task in university, I could consistently arrive at the office by 7 a.m., eat lunch in under half an hour, and work until 11 p.m. This routine I could maintain for days, and my teammates thought my learning ability was strong, and I was confident in the task, able to stay focused for long periods. I was also very willing to share and help others. Communicating was my favorite thing to do. This led me to pursue a career in education after graduation. The teachers in the team all thought I had strong learning abilities. Whenever there was a problem, they would come to me for discussion. I was open-minded and willing to try various new teaching methods, using diverse and interesting teaching aids, always able to inspire others with deep thinking.

III. Summary

Personal Development Suggestions
● Recognize the value of others' opinions
● Balance your work and personal life
● Seek feedback and suggestions
● Learn to appreciate others
● Learn when to let go of unrealistic ideas
● Pay more attention to the impact your thoughts have on others

The above information was gathered through various tests and conversations with people around me. Overall, I am passionate about learning, and lifelong learning is my lasting motivation. I also enjoy sharing and expressing myself. Tests are only relative references; perhaps the results can change after a few months, so don't take them too seriously. Give yourself more time to understand yourself. Through the course on the Ideation World, I have come to better understand what I truly want. When facing problems, first ask yourself what your inner voice is saying. Be present in the moment, and break down problems using a rationalized or step-by-step approach.
