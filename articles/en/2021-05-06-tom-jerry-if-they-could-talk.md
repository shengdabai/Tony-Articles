> *Tom and Jerry* is an animated series created by producer Fred Quimby, directors William Hanna and Joseph Barbera in 1939. After the first animated short *Tom Gets the Boot* was a huge success, Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer produced over 100 *Tom and Jerry* animated films in the next 25 years. This series is entirely based on slapstick comedy, with very lively plots. It is one of the most famous animated series in the world today. Its creators, William Hanna and Joseph Barbera, are considered legendary figures in American Hollywood animation. William Hanna was born in Melrose, New Mexico, and at the age of seven, he moved with his parents to California. He later studied engineering, journalism, and music. During the Great Depression in the 1930s, he worked part-time at an animation studio as a story editor. His employer quickly recognized his talent, and he was promoted continuously until he became an independent animation director. Joseph Barbera, the cartoonist, was born in New York and had a natural talent for drawing from a young age. He initially worked at a bank but became fascinated with animation in his spare time. His unique talent and diligence quickly led him to find his ideal job at an animation studio. In 1937, Hanna and Barbera first met at Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer's animation studio. In 1938, Hanna worked on the creation of *Looney Tunes* and *Merrie Melodies*, while Barbera worked on *Terrytoons*. Hanna had a comedic talent and was a renowned director with strong organizational skills, while Barbera had an exceptional artistic talent. They both realized they should form a partnership, and thus began a successful collaboration for decades. *Tom and Jerry* is entirely based on slapstick comedy, with very lively plots. Tom is a common house cat with a strong desire to catch Jerry, the mouse living in the same room but always escaping. He constantly tries to drive away these annoying roommates but always fails. In fact, the fun he gets from chasing Jerry far exceeds the satisfaction of catching him, even if he occasionally catches Jerry, he doesn’t know what to do with the mouse! *Tom and Jerry* uses the prototypes of cats and mice, their pranks are very interesting. No matter how intense the game is, Jerry knows he won’t be harmed, while Tom always ends up with some physical pain. Jerry lives with Tuffy, the little gray mouse, in the mouse hole, looking like he is being watched by Tom, but Jerry is very clever, always making Tom’s tricks backfire, and making him suffer the consequences. This story of the clever mouse and the clumsy cat can be compared to the stories of Mickey Mouse and Donald Duck. There is no cruelty in the animal world, only the daily trivialities and quarrels between two neighbors, such as Jerry stealing Tom’s cheese, Tom placing a mouse trap at Jerry’s entrance, and countless pranks and humorous moments in between, giving people a sense of long-lost innocence. Their relationship often changes in an instant—enemies or friends, enemies trying their best to outwit each other, and friends like brothers, never holding grudges.
