Tell a Story #Case Study#

01 Xiao Bai saw WeChat Channels and noticed there wasn't much content. He thought there was an opportunity, so he started making WeChat Channels, updating short videos every day.

02 Xiao Bai kept happily creating content, realizing his material was unique, but the data wasn't great. He was confused and wondered if he should try live streaming?

03 He began live streaming every day, only to see others receiving tips while he got nothing. He started questioning whether WeChat Channels actually had traffic and if it was profitable.

04 After some time, Xiao Bai posted on hisWeChat Moments saying he was switching to Douyin, since his content could be monetized there. But he didn't want to abandon WeChat Channels, so he began distributing the same content across multiple platforms.

05 As he continued, Xiao Bai found himself hitting a growth plateau. Was self-media not suitable for him? He then saw opportunities in cross-border e-commerce...

06 The domestic market was saturated, so going overseas must have some potential. Without further ado, he moved into cross-border e-commerce.

07 Xiao Bai's story might still be continuing. If there are similarities, it might just be a coincidence. I wonder, where did we really spend our time?

I've finished writing. Thank you for being the one who makes things happen!
