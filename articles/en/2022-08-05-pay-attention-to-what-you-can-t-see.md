Note 99

01 The 4P and 4C theories commonly used in marketing, especially the 4P, refer to product, price, promotion, and distribution. Most of the time, we only see the promotion.

02 For example, Jiang Xiao Bai, as a newbaijiu brand, rose rapidly, and most analyses focus on its copywriting, which is the design of the bottle. But this is not the key to its success.

03 All four elements of the 4P theory are important. Promotion is the most visible and widely studied, so people tend to overlook other factors, such as distribution. One of the core reasons for Jiang Xiao Bai's success was its distribution network, covering over 1 million stores nationwide—something most companies can't achieve.

04 WeChat Channels consists of short videos and live streaming, but in addition, it also connects with other WeChat features, such as mini programs, WeChat Pay, and official accounts.

05 Focusing on visible elements often makes us ignore the hidden ones, which are actually very important. WeChat Channels isn't lacking in traffic; what's scarce is high-quality content.

06 Going further, the reason WeChat Channels can sustain investment is due to CLV, or Customer Lifetime Value. Looking at the long term, for example, a street-side snack shop has a higher CLV than a high-priced course, because users tend to visit a snack shop multiple times, even if each visit isn't expensive. High-priced courses are hard to repurchase.

07 Business operations are more important than marketing. Marketing can't solve everything. Understanding this early on is helpful for business.
