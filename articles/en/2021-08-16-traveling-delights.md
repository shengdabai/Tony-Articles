128th Original Piece

Why is traveling always so delightful?

There's a common saying: "At home, you're a noble person; on the road, you're a sinner." Is traveling really that great?

I don't know how many times I've tried to persuade my mom to go on a trip, but she always refuses, sticking to her own belief that staying home is so comfortable and going out is never a good idea!

Perhaps I just wanted to prove to her that traveling is full of endless fun, and I tried to showcase all the benefits of traveling to her. Before I knew it, I had started my own travel mode.

In my memory, my first trip was to Beijing, but it was actually to check my eyesight and nose at the famous Tongren Hospital. Visiting scenic spots became a given, or else it would be a waste of the train ticket money 💰 At the age of ten, I didn't understand what traveling meant; maybe it was just about seeing different things.

In high school, I spent most of my time studying. Of course, I wasn't a top student, but I had time to watch TV and longed for places I hadn't yet visited.

At that time, I thought traveling might just mean going back home for the Spring Festival, since taking a train or driving along the way would allow me to see different scenery. To me, traveling might have been a kind of memory!

The clearest travel memory was during university, which is why university trips have stayed with me for so long—it was the beginning of my solo travels.

I don't know why, but during university, I rarely wanted to travel with others. I just thought, if I want to go somewhere, I'll just go.

One day, she entered my world, speaking in a poetic way. Actually, it was more like I entered her territory. On the road, there was someone to listen to my laughter, someone to share meals with. We walked through bustling streets, we moved through crowded crowds, we climbed rugged mountain paths, and we saw different scenery.

I still remember that was in a beautiful village at the foot of Huangshan Mountain, called Hongcun. Tourists who had heard of it left their footprints there, taking beautiful photos. We met a photographer on a narrow road. I can't remember what he looked like, but I remember he liked how we walked hand in hand, and our figures were captured by his camera.

After work, I traveled even more crazily. What do you mean by "crazy"? Except for work, I was always on the road, traveling as soon as I had a holiday, flying or taking the high-speed rail to my destination.

Is this a form of retaliatory spending? I don't know if it's to escape the troubles of work or to check off cities, but I just really wanted to go somewhere.

What's the purpose of traveling? I think it might be to live a different kind of life!

On the road, I love listening to books and taking classes, having conversations with my favorite authors. Why not listen to music? Maybe music itself is part of the journey, and I prefer to have background music while chatting with people.

There are all sorts of scenery in front of me—some you like, some you don't. What I care about is the people.

Rushing from place to place, checking off spots, using my phone to record travel memories—this is fine. But staying somewhere for a while, eating local delicacies, and chatting with locals is also an interesting experience.

I enjoy communicating with people in the places I visit, experiencing their way of life, starting with food, chatting about culture, and discussing life.

I'm often mistaken for coming from Qingdao, a city known for its seafood. I can't help but introduce myself as from Qinghai, a beautiful place in the northwest of China. Talking about where you're from and sharing stories about your hometown—doesn't that sound great?

Traveling is my hobby, and socializing is my interest. Meeting friends from all over the country, meeting in some corner of the world—how precious this connection is!

Reading a thousand books is not as good as traveling a thousand miles. I want to travel while reading. Reading is inconvenient, but listening to books is also a great choice!

Goodnight, let's all work hard for a new week!
