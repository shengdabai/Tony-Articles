On the Seven Stars Festival, here's a quote from Li Xiaolai:

The most romantic thing I can imagine is growing rich together with you!

Let me try to write my understanding of love using a syllogism:

Major premise: Only by learning to love and understand yourself can you truly understand someone else's needs.

Minor premise: People in love learn to love and understand themselves during their growth.

Conclusion: Lovers grow together, understand each other's needs, and cultivate a beautiful relationship.

There's a book called *How to Make the Person You Love Love You*, written by American authorLeil Lowndes. It deeply moved me. When I saw the title, did it make you feel attracted? Did it make you a little excited? After listening to Fan Deng's interpretation, I believe you'll have some inspiration, just like me.

There are many points to analyze here. Let me talk about some basic principles of love. For instance, when in love, people are not normal. Many people feel a bit excited during the early stages of a relationship, staying up for days and nights, which can be quite thrilling. This is normal, because your body is flooded with phenethylamine, known as Pea. This substance makes you feel extremely excited; it's a neurotransmitter.

Let me explain this with a syllogism:

Major premise: Phenethylamine makes you fall in love with someone.

Minor premise: When you see someone you like, your body produces a lot of phenethylamine.

Conclusion: When you see someone you like, you'll fall in love with her involuntarily.

I think this is quite interesting. If I could, I'd like to inject phenethylamine into her. Of course, this is not appropriate, just a brain teaser.

Where is your partner? According to statistical analysis, the majority of ordinary people's partners live within five blocks of their home. If you draw a circle around your home with a radius of five blocks, your partner is likely within that area.

So let me explain this with a syllogism:

Major premise: A partner appears within five blocks.

Major premise: I often wander around within five blocks.

Conclusion: While wandering, I discovered my partner.

This data is quite interesting, though not very reliable. Still, it gives me a little confidence. So, if I have time, I'll just go out and wander around. Maybe my fate is waiting for me!

Regarding how men and women behave in different situations during a relationship, it's truly fascinating and interesting. I won't go into too much detail here, but I'll try to summarize my understanding:

Major premise: Different behaviors between men and women promote intimacy.

Minor premise: Men who love chatting can resonate with women like female friends, and women who think like men can understand his directness.

Conclusion: Mutual love.

The first principle has taught me to explore the root of things. By using deductive reasoning, I can trace back to the source. Sometimes I'm used to generalizing, which is a product of human evolution. If I try to approach things from a different angle with deductive reasoning, I think it's still quite interesting. In the real world, perhaps deduction and induction coexist, complementing each other. Finally, I hope all the single, chaotic individuals like me—though there are very few—can find happiness. Let's all grow rich together slowly, one day at a time!
