Tony Sheng's Owned Audience Sticky Note

01

Note No. 52

The video collection button is gone, replaced by a like (´-ω-`) #Short Video#

01 In the beginning, short videos had three actions: heart like, share, and comment.

02 A collection button was later introduced, marked with a ★, which was a feature borrowed from other platforms. However, it became clear that not many people used the collection function. From my perspective, what's more important than liking is value; I only collect content that's valuable to me.

03 The collection button was further evolved into a like. Do you feel confused? It seems like there's little difference between collection and liking. Yes, that's exactly the point—collection + like, both the heart and the thumb up are needed.

04 From my data, it's clear that clicking the heart icon usually indicates a strong liking for the video. For example, you might like a video to support a friend emotionally, but whether you collect it is another matter.

05 This latest update weakens the collection button, making people think that clicking the like button is now equivalent to collecting. So next time you make a video, you can say, "Give me a like👍," and that's basically collecting!

06 You must be brave enough to ask your audience for likes, heart icons, and encourage comments. Why? Because if you don't say it, people might forget.

07 Guide your audience to support you, don't miss any opportunity. To some extent, your poor data is because you're too afraid to ask.

I've finished writing. Thank you for being the one who makes things happen!
