Second Note

01 What comes to mind when you see MOT? MOT stands for Moment of Truth, a critical moment. For example, when you want to eat a crispy and chewy egg tart, you can hear the duangduang sound, and this moment makes me choose to go to KFC.

02 Why do we discuss the concept of the Moment of Truth? The reason is simple: you can't be everywhere at once.

03 When does the overall user experience of a restaurant begin? Perhaps it starts the moment you see the restaurant's name. So think about how much you need to cover: promotion, entering the store, purchasing, repurchasing, spreading the word, and so on.

04 There is a Yunnan restaurant that uses Yunnan characteristics throughout the entire experience, but its business isn't great. The owner wants to improve overall, but you know the result might be high costs with little effect.

05 It wasn't until the owner followed his consultant's advice and conducted user research that he found the Moment of Truth. Originally, the core reason customers entered the store was because of the young ladies in Yunnan costumes dancing traditional ethnic dances.

06 The Moment of Truth isn't something we just think of out of the blue. It needs to be identified through user research and analysis. The core isn't to find all the Moments of Truth, but to do the opposite: make cuts and find the top few most important ones.

07 Do you know clearly why your customers buy your product?
