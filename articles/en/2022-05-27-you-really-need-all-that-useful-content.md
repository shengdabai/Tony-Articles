Note 54

The Value of Useful Content Itself Isn't That Great #Plain Language# #Little Secret#

01 You'll hear all sorts of words: useful content, secrets, heart methods... From the bottom of your heart, you might feel a bit of longing for these. If I learn them, will I be different?

02 Knowing and doing are worlds apart. Different people, facing different "useful content," will produce vastly different results. The difference lies in execution.

03 True knowledge comes from practice. You've heard so much—how much have you actually done? Some might think it's because they've learned too little, but even if you know more, what does that matter? The core is how much you've done.

04 Practice in real situations, practice in real situations, practice in real situations. You're more convincing to others or have influence because you've produced results, because you've done something.

05 Clarify one point: Are you trying to be a teacher or a coach? A teacher helps you find problems, while a coach helps you solve them.

06 What's popular now? Training camps, all sorts of training camps. The reason is simple: following along and achieving results is key. Taking more people along, creating case studies, that's what's needed. WeChat Channels are no different.

07 You don't need to know all that. For most people, useful content isn't that valuable. Just take simple truths and practice them to the extreme, do things solidly, and produce results. Just like you believe in WeChat Channels, then do your best to make it happen.

I've finished writing. Thank you for being the one who can make things happen!
