Note 17

01 How to understand owned audience and public domain? Simply put, it's about whether the traffic belongs to you or the platform. If you can manage the traffic you control, it can become your owned audience.

02 I often use an analogy. Users see you on various self-media platforms, can follow you, like your posts, and comment on them. But where should they go? They should go to a place where they can stay.

03 The foundation of building a owned audience starts with finding your basic tools. For example, WeChat. You can use WeChat Channels, Enterprise WeChat, Official Accounts, Mini Shops, personal WeChat groups, and Moments to link them together, allowing users to accumulate within them.

04 A solid foundation is essential, just like a spider web. The nodes are interconnected. WeChat Channels act as the central point of the web, connecting all the tools together.

05 Once the foundation is solid, the next step is user operation. How can you build stronger connections with your users? Delivering products or courses is the way, but it all comes down to content connection.

06 The basic domain is the key part to focus on. Simply put, even without growth, you can still solve your basic needs.

07 Growth begins when you break through the critical point. When that critical point appears, just wait patiently.
