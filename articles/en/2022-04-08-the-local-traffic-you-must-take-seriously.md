Note 11

01 WeChat Channels live streaming is in full swing, with newbies, online celebrities, small businesses, and big companies all eager to get involved—where is your opportunity?

02 First, you need to clarify your positioning. In simple terms, are you focusing on quality or quantity? Do you need to define content and strategy, or do you need to focus on volume and pricing?

03 During my consulting sessions, I often find that users haven't clearly identified their current needs. For example, someone might say they want to make more money—specifically, what do they want? Is it through donations, product sales, course offerings, or collaborations?

04 Next, you need to make choices and sacrifices. You must let go of external factors that aren't as important to you right now. For instance, you don't need to consider the entire internet; for you, the local area is the core.

05 WeChat Channels is a massive billboard. How to make your ad more effective? Naturally, it's by targeting the people around you.

06 Imagine your city, then the district, then the community. If you become the top player in this area, how much value will you gain?

07 Becoming a local leader means connecting to all local resources. You have a loyal local fanbase, and you've already surpassed 90% of ordinary people and businesses!
