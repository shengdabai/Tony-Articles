Note 64

01 What is the purpose of marketing? It could be to sell more, sell better. My understanding is that it's about having influence even when you're not present—earning income even when you're doing nothing. In simple terms, it's "passive income."

02 How to do marketing on WeChat Channels? I drew inspiration from Maoshu's insights on improving marketing skills and have three thoughts to share.

03 Practice in real situations. Create short videos, live stream continuously. Only by doing it can you discover problems, optimize, and iterate. Marketing always comes from details, starting small.

04 Read classic books. Here, I recommend only one book. I believe no book can surpass it. It's worth reading repeatedly and spending a long time on. Li Xiaolai's *WeChat Internet Plain People Entrepreneurship* is a book that Li Xiaolai himself admits is his first book about making money (the content focuses on the WeChat ecosystem, with emphasis on analysis of WeChat Channels). I believe this book has long-term value, and you can always gain something from it at any time.

05 Analyze case studies. Maoshu mentioned analyzing the product detail pages of bestsellers on Taobao, something many of us tend to overlook. For example, in live streaming, look at how the detail pages of best-selling products are structured. Start with imitation, then surpass.

06 WeChat Channels has multiple identities, and its value is so great that nothing can surpass it. Take just one point: the network value brought by WeChat Channels connects resources that may be impossible to measure in monetary terms. For instance, I've gained tens of thousands of connections and a six-figure income through WeChat Channels—this is just the beginning.

07 Improve your marketing skills to expand your influence. You deserve better and more. I recommend you read Maoshu's article thoroughly once again. Thank you, Maoshu, for the deep inspiration!
