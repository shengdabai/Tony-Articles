How to do effective input? When I mention effective input, the first thing people might think of is what I'm about to talk about today—reading. Of course, we have various ways to obtain information, but I hope everyone knows that reading is the best and most direct way to acquire resources and information. It's also the lowest-cost method compared to things like attending classes or communicating with experts. Reading is the simplest and most cost-effective way. I believe reading is an excellent method for us to achieve effective input. In English teaching, we have two main sources of input: listening and reading. Today, reading has become my primary learning method, mainly through reading books, e-books, or articles on websites. For listening, I mainly listen to audiobooks and courses. I think reading and listening are the best input methods at any time. We need to use them efficiently. Today, I'd like to talk about the most effective ways and methods of reading.

Reading is our main source of information, the way we accumulate energy, and more importantly, it helps us upgrade our brains and cognitive systems, making us more capable of doing better and living better. Both Chinese and English reading can be done in any scenario—reading when you can't sleep, when you have nothing to do, or when you need energy. How to build a solid foundation for reading? Both Chinese and English apply: read a lot. You can go to bookstores, read e-books, and read extensively. Recommend reading books you can actually get into and books that provide growth methods, laying a foundation for the future. What if you can't remember what you read or read slowly? In fact, most content doesn't need to be remembered. Remember the days of school? Some things you might not even recall clearly. Don't force yourself to memorize. English reading is the same. Don't believe that memorizing New Concept English will make you capable of anything. Adults learning English should have clear goals. The goal of finishing New Concept is probably something few people achieve. Just read according to your needs, read multiple times, and the purpose is to use it. Only by using it can you read better. During reading, think about how to apply it. Think about what the book says, what value it has for you, and how you can use it. You must use it, practice it, and ideally, make some achievements. Of course, you can also share it with others, which helps you digest and understand the content better.

Reading slowly is inevitable in the early stages, especially for English reading. It's okay. The slower, the better. As your vocabulary grows and your understanding of new books improves, your reading speed will naturally increase. Do you need to take notes while reading? There are some differences between Chinese and English reading. For English reading, you need to look up vocabulary and take notes to better understand the text. But it's best to read first and not get stuck on vocabulary. Some words don't directly affect your understanding of the passage. Read further, and you might understand it later. After finishing the whole passage, go back to check for any misunderstandings. It's recommended to buy an authoritative dictionary instead of using translation software. Online dictionaries are also very convenient. In Chinese reading, you might also encounter similar situations. For example, if you don't fully understand certain meanings, such as some vocabulary, concepts, or models, you might not be able to read quickly. In that case, you can spend some time reading widely. For instance, if you want to study psychology, you can read a few recommended books by famous psychologists and psychological monographs. After reading these three or four books widely, you'll find that other books' content starts to make more sense. For example, during my current graduate studies, I need to read many reviews. You can also try reading reviews to gain a basic understanding of an industry or field, and then read other content. This will significantly improve your comprehension speed.

What are the minimum essential knowledge for English reading? The most important point is that even if you forget everything else, remember this: read. Open a book and read seriously! Integrate reading into our lives, and everything will be much easier. Chinese works from modern times are far from comparable to Western works. After all, world-class scholarship is mostly in English. English reading is our first choice. Reading extensively naturally leads to a sufficient reading speed. If you really want to read faster, read with questions in mind. Follow the questions like a map, and it's not strange to read dozens of books in a day. This is what Professor Wang Dingding calls broadband reading. Everyone reads to solve their own problems. Reading with a purpose is highly efficient. Read with questions and think critically.

Our brains are highly malleable. As long as we stimulate our language areas sufficiently, we can learn a foreign language well at any age. Many people who are fluent in multiple languages are not necessarily immersed in multilingual environments from a young age. They can master these languages because they are interested in language and are willing to learn and practice more. Their brains naturally get activated. Conversely, if you learn a foreign language at the right time but never practice it afterward, you will still lose it. Don't worry too much—just take action. Start now, and it's never too late!

Believing in the power of books is one of the true essences of my educational beliefs. — Sukhomlinsky
