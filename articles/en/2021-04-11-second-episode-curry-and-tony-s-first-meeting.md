Tony and Curry met for the first time in the hospital. That tiny baby in the swaddle was actually Tony's younger brother. Tony felt a kind of love, wanting to hold him, but he was too shy and a bit arrogant, saying, "How come he doesn't have eyes? He's just a lump of meat..." In a sixth grader's eyes, everything is simple and pure, ha ha, and a little bit of a harsh tongue. Sometimes kids just don't get the best treatment. Curry could only make gurgling sounds, trying to express his thoughts, and ended up crying loudly. Everyone would rush over, but what Curry wanted, he could only rely on adults to guess.

After discharge, Curry was treated like a precious gem at home, with all the attention focused on him. His daily life was simple: eat, drink, sleep, maybe cry a bit, laugh a bit. After birth, babies need a lot of nutrition to develop their precious brains and grow their bodies. AI is a product of modern society's development. A few years ago, AI was already in its gestation, but in recent years, it has developed rapidly, thanks to modern technologies like big data, the Internet of Things, and smart devices, which have advanced both software and hardware, thus propelling the progress of artificial intelligence. Why am I talking about AI? The AI we see now is weak AI, and we are more hopeful for strong AI, but that will take a long time. It can be stronger than humans in some areas, but the abilities humans acquire before the age of three are something AI finds hard to develop. For example, how many times do we need to recognize a cat? Once or twice, and by the third time, we definitely know it. What about AI? The Google lab trained AI to recognize cats by learning millions of images. One day, when AI saw a cat's image, it suddenly lit up with a red dot, and it recognized the cat. Think about how lucky we humans are!

During the time at home, Tony and Curry didn't interact much. Why? Because Mom needed to run a restaurant and would take Curry with her every day. Tony, on the other hand, stayed home quietly studying. Honestly, thinking back, Tony doesn't remember this period very clearly.

Before we knew it, Curry started kindergarten. Tony didn't even take him to kindergarten, because the timing didn't match. Tony had already started middle school and was fully focused on preparing for the middle school entrance exam. Curry, along with his cousin, went to kindergarten together, happily learning and having time to play every day. Tony missed out on a lot of Curry's growth during his early years, and didn't have many memories of him. This led Tony to choose to become a teacher in the future, so he could help Curry.
