Note 16

01 The question I'm asked most often in consultations is, "What do I do when I don't have traffic?"

02 I help users analyze and organize their situations, and also assist them in finding practical growth points. But if there's a general method, here's one you can try:

03 The term "group red" was taught to me by Piaohan Cat Uncle Mao. For example, if I have a good reputation in a community, members will add me on WeChat voluntarily, increasing my traffic. I recommend you try not setting up friend verification when adding people.

04 Collaboration. As more people connect with you, you may encounter new collaboration opportunities. Seize these opportunities and try to collaborate. I've always believed that going it alone is difficult, and team-based operations are more suitable. You can start looking for potential collaborators.

05 Promotion. When working with others, you might be pushed forward, which will further open up your traffic channels. For example, if you help someone achieve a result, they might give you a boost, and your traffic will naturally grow.

06 Experimentation. "One look, a lifetime" seems to be something most people can't achieve in one go. If that's the case, then within the limited attention span, try different approaches and find the model that works best for you in terms of traffic.

07 Finally, be mindful in your selection, seize your opportunities, and remember that slow work produces fine results. You'll eventually see people leave, and you'll remain.
