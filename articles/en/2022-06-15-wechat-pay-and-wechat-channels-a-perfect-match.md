Note 66

WeChat Pay and WeChat Channels: A Perfect Match #Short Video#Secrets#Tools#

01 Order a cup of Luckin Coffee and see how WeChat Pay integrates with WeChat Channels. When you place an order and pay, you'll receive a reminder. Look at the bottom—curious about the [Brand Story]? Tap it, and you'll be directed to a WeChat Channel. You'll probably end up following it out of curiosity.

02 The integration between WeChat Pay and WeChat Channels has already been in place for a while. I've been advising users to use this feature, but I didn't realize its value and didn't take the time to learn how to operate it.

03 In the WeChat Pay backend settings, you can bind a WeChat Channel and choose videos or promote live streams. It's incredibly convenient.

04 Going further, as long as there's a WeChat transaction, WeChat Pay is indispensable. That means it naturally integrates with WeChat Channels. For example, when you go to the convenience store downstairs and scan the code to pay, you'll see the store's WeChat Channel. Isn't that a natural form of advertising?

05 As long as there's a WeChat Pay transaction, you can let your WeChat Channel advertise for you. Say goodbye to the limitations of text—video communication is more effective.

06 More and more companies are entering WeChat Channels, showing the platform's value. It also means we need to act quickly.

07 The best way to learn is to use it. Practice leads to understanding.

I've finished writing. Thank you for your support and likes!
