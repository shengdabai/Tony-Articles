01 Content accumulation is a crucial consideration in owned audience operations. I've found a form that suits my expression: Official Account + Knowledge Planet.

02 Someone asked me why I'm not continuing with WeChat Channels. For short videos, I'll start daily updates after re-designing them. As for live streaming, there's currently no particularly suitable model for me to express myself.

03 More importantly, my current focus is on accumulating case studies to help those with a solid foundation achieve success. Choosing one path means giving up others—reduction is the main theme of this phase.

04 The Official Account serves as my owned audience notes, where I promptly organize and share my thoughts on owned audiences. Knowledge Planet focuses on content accumulation, refining my thoughts on WeChat Channels content from more practical and detailed angles. It includes not only my analysis and breakdowns but also column Q&A, with problem-solving at its core.

05 The creation process for WeChat Channels requires perseverance. I hope my accumulated content can give more people confidence.

06 Why am I doing a paid Knowledge Planet? Simply put, it's to thank users' trust with higher-quality content delivery.

07 Paying is a connection. Together, we co-create high-quality WeChat Channels content. What's truly useful is what's useful to you.
