Note 51

01 Live streaming gifts are becoming increasingly diverse, mainly divided into two categories: regular gifts and fan-exclusive gifts.

02 Fan-exclusive gifts are a way to showcase the privileges of a fan club member, and only fans at a certain level can send them. Doesn't that feel cool? For example, sending you a love letter, which is exclusive to fans at level 9 or above.

03 The most expensive gift currently is the Peach Island, which used to cost 888 yuan but now costs 3,000 yuan. The platform takes half, so you end up with 1,500 yuan.

04 Different gifts represent different intentions. For instance, the 520 gift expresses affection and only costs 52 yuan, which caters to the psychology of most people.

05 New and unique gifts keep appearing, such as "Real Good Sound," "Super Pretty," and "Forever and Ever." Gifts are continuously updated and evolved, which is also a form of stimulation that encourages more people to engage here.

06 During official events or major live streams, you'll see custom-designed gifts, such as glow sticks and various exclusive gifts. This makes the live stream stand out as unique and special.

07 What if we could customize gifts in the future? For example, a gift customized by Tony would be a big Tony, exclusive to my live stream. That would be pretty cool. More importantly, the strong association between the gift and the streamer makes it more likely you'll remember me.
