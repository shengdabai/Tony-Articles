Note 23

01 The target audience can be roughly divided into two categories: fans of singers (existing) and non-fans (newcomers). 02 The strategy for dissemination: stimulate the target's performance psychology in social environments. Yes, it's the "performance psychology." 03 For the fan group, provide them with props for their performance. Fans have a strong motivation to perform, and they will actively show off their fan identity. All we need to do is provide them with materials for sharing. 04 For non-fan newcomers, create motivation for their performance. We can launch a music-related public welfare project, as participating in public welfare is something most people enjoy performing and sharing. 05 Do you understand the needs of users during the short video dissemination process? 06 For example, think about what kind of content you like more, and why you like to like certain content. 07 Don't assume that the users who see your videos are all fans. If they are non-fans, what do they need? In simple terms, how can they "convert from strangers to fans"? Feel free to leave your thoughts!
