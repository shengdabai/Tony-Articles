Who is Tony? And who is Curry? Let's go back to 2006, when Tony was just 10 years old, still a primary school student. After his 10th birthday, Tony received a lot of love from the whole family. In his hometown, turning 10 is a big deal for a kid, and he got his first desktop computer, a TQ Tungfang desktop. This brand is probably not used by many people anymore. Tony was really excited, even though he didn't know how to use it, he still felt thrilled, thinking to himself, "I'm now a person with a computer!" What a sudden burst of vanity!

Not long after, another big gift🎁 appeared!

Mom asked Tony, "Do you like your little sister?" This question caught Tony off guard. Why would she ask that? Then, just like today'ssecond-child moms would do, Mom told Tony, "You'll have a companion, someone who loves you more, and the family will have more joy!" But Tony didn't take it well. He ran out crying, not looking back, like a headless fly, not knowing where to go (ー_ー)!! Dad, Mom, and other relatives started searching everywhere. Uncle DaYi ran the fastest, but still couldn't catch up, panting heavily, and finally saw Tony sobbing in the corner. He rushed over, comforted him, and took Tony back home.

This kind of conflict happened several times. Parents took turns trying to reason with Tony, but he remained stubborn. What was going through his mind? He thought having just himself in the family was best. He didn't want a younger brother or sister, because they would take away his parents' love, and family attention would shift to them. Tony just didn't want to accept this reality. Gradually, Tony started to have his own little mood swings, and he still had some reservations about the arrival of his sibling. Ten months passed quickly～

On November 17, 2007, this day Tony never forgot. When Tony came home from school around noon, he found the house empty. He called his dad, who told him Mom had just given birth to a baby and told Tony to take the money himself to buy lunch. Tony went angrily to a duck blood soup restaurant, and don't ask why Tony remembers this so clearly. On this day, Curry was born. You might have guessed it—Curry was a little cutie, with his eyes half-closed, happily saying hello to the world, "Umm～"

The next day was a weekend, and Tony and his dad went to the hospital together. It was their first time meeting Curry～～～
