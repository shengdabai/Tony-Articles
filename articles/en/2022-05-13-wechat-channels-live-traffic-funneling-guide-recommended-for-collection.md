WeChat Channels Live Traffic Funneling Guide #Live #Secrets#

Operations Strategy:

01 Pull Live Reservation: Reservation data has a decisive impact on traffic inflow. The reasoning is that many mobile app notifications and live reservation prompts are currently the most effective way to reach users. Private domain can also drive public domain. (Tip: For reservation testing, do it one hour before the reservation, usually using a small account for testing.)

02 Reservation Wizard: Reservation Ranking Use the reservation promotion feature to let more people participate in increasing reservation data. You can set up benefits to encourage users to help us pull reservations, which is also a form of interaction and owned audience operation. Specific operations can be done through the WeChat Channels Assistant.

03 In the long run, owned audience operations and WeChat Channels Live will become more closely integrated. After all, an increasing number of brands are already exploring this. Meanwhile, the live channel should remain in the [Shopping] category.

Traffic Rewards:

04 Service Provider Traffic Packages for Cold Start and Daily Support: If there are WeChat Channels service providers around you, consider joining to receive corresponding traffic support. Of course, service providers also get traffic based on the data of signed-up streamers, mainly the live room transaction amount, which is what we often refer to as GMV.

05 The leverage effect of owned audience traffic: Improve related data, such as audience size, dwell time, comments, clicks, and purchase behaviors. The better the data, the higher the base weight on WeChat Channels, and the better the future traffic support will be. In comparison, GMV performance is explicit and short-term. In the future, the platform will provide corresponding support based on your model, so focus on owned audience accumulation.

Traffic Accumulation:

06 Push traffic to Enterprise WeChat. I often say, give users a place to go, because they will become the lever for the next live stream in your owned audience, driving traffic growth and forming a snowball effect, rolling in a positive cycle.

07 This year, the most frequently heard point is 1:1 traffic recommendation. This point can be explored further: if 50 people enter the owned audience, they can be rewarded with public domain traffic, and this threshold will rise. The key stage of owned audience traffic funneling before the live stream starts is the first 20 minutes, which helps with account weight. After two hours of the live stream, GMV may achieve a 1:1 ratio with public domain traffic. For example, if you sell products worth 10,000, you might be able to leverage 10,000 public domain traffic.

The above content is a summary and observation from multiple live streams. As the platform develops, some changes may occur. Focus on owned audience, make good use of it, and keep going (´-ω-`)
