Tesla, Like a Virus

001 The Founder Who Makes Big Promises Come True

Elon Musk's bold statements and unconventional behavior have kept him in the spotlight. When a founder deeply thinks about the product from first principles, the impossible gene spreads rapidly.

002 Why It Became a Sensation

Tesla pioneered the electric vehicle market, optimized the car experience, offered high cost-effectiveness, better automation, and social sharing—multiple factors made Tesla rapidly popular.

003 Disrupting the Traditional to Build Reputation

By stepping away from past path dependencies and returning to the most basic conditions of things, Tesla rethought the optimal way to achieve its goals. By eliminating the assembly process and adopting one-piece casting technology, it reduced car purchase costs and quickly built a reputation.

004 Open Technology to Drive the Industry

In eight years, production capacity expanded 20 times. By openly sharing electric vehicle battery technology, Tesla advanced the electric car industry. As more companies joined the electric vehicle sector, the possibility for environmental protection grew.

005 Social Currency Attribute

It's not unusual for Tesla cars to be gifted, but users naturally see them as representing technology and value, making them a new kind of social currency. Additionally, Tesla cars can be purchased using Bitcoin, a digital currency.

006 Triggering Behavior Through Incentives

The market aims to uncover user needs and promote purchasing behavior. Tesla's car design has won the appreciation of the younger generation and tech enthusiasts, continuously expanding the pool of potential customers.

007 Awakening Emotions

Tesla's advertising integrates multiple elements like technology and fashion, and professional filming teams create user experiences that instantly resonate with emotions, leading to natural purchasing behavior.

008 Creating Public Attributes

Tesla's three driving forces: scale effect, network effect, and Moore's Law. More users and investors are optimistic about Tesla's future. It's not just a new energy company focused on energy storage batteries, but also a tech company working on autonomous driving technology.

009 Be a Rabbit

In a marathon, there are pacemakers, who might be the rabbit in thethe tortoise and the hare (hare and tortoise race). Tesla's existence is like a "rabbit" in the new energy industry, not interested in building a moat, but just wanting to accelerate the world's transition to new energy.

010 The Idea of Viral Spread

Elon Musk always keeps the ultimate goal in mind: to make people's lives better, serving needs people themselves haven't even thought of.
