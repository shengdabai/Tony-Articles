Note 85

The "Apple" That Strikes First

001 Attention Determines Identity

Apple has consistently adhered to the principle of putting product excellence first. Attention remains focused on the product itself, and where attention is directed determines the company's identity and clarifies its strategic positioning.

002 Privileged Moments Are Hard to Resist

When Apple integrates multiple core technologies and achieves one-click operation, connecting a phone with a finger, the attention at the product launch event immediately stunned the world with the iPhone.

003 Leading the User's Focus

The mobile phone market is highly competitive, yet Apple has always led the way. By maintaining a firm grip on user attention, even with other brands constantly innovating in features, Apple continues to uphold its core strengths.

004 Safety and Stability Are Unmatched

The security and stability of Apple's phones have been tested over time. There's even a case in the U.S. where federal police could not disclose suspect information from Apple's data, providing solid support for marketing and promotion.

005 Health Protection Brings Warmth

Apple embedded health monitoring functions during the early stages of product development, laying the groundwork for future smart wearables and health digitization. Users, in turn, unknowingly help Apple refine its products.

006 Red, Yellow, and Green Super Symbols

The red, yellow, and green buttons on Apple computers, compared to Windows settings, spark associations, giving the super symbol new attributes. This application naturally supports brand promotion.

007 Scenario Simulation

What do the startup videos of Apple's devices make you think of? Does the Apple logo remind you of anything else? These associations give the brand different meanings and leave a deep impression.

008 Unique Operational Style

Other companies first explore and make mistakes, then, after the market stabilizes and users develop habits, Apple enters the scene. This unique operational style allows for low-cost operations but unmatched influence.

009 Alliance of Attack and Defense

Apple has its own product alliance, with devices forming an alliance internally and cross-industry alliances externally. More importantly, there's an alliance based on user identity, allowing Apple to move freely.

010 Works by Jobs

Jobs created the company Apple. The company's early influence can be seen from multiple angles. A few words can't cover everything. I look forward to this apple.
