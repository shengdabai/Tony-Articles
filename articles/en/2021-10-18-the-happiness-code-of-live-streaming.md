# The Happiness Code of Live Streaming

> Published: 2021-10-18 · [中文](../zh/2021-10-18-%E7%9B%B4%E6%92%AD%E7%9A%84%E5%B9%B8%E7%A6%8F%E5%AF%86%E7%A0%81.md) | [English](../en/2021-10-18-the-happiness-code-of-live-streaming.md)

When I figure something out, I keep doing it because I clearly know the value behind it. I also clearly know I might regret it in the future, so I have to do it today.

This is the first day of my continuous live streaming. Before this, I had already tried live streaming on Moments three times and had dozens of live sessions with others. Do I have experience? Not really. I just like it.

I stopped live streaming for a while because I didn’t fully understand what I wanted to keep streaming. Like everyone else, I had the same concerns: Will anyone watch? I’m so ordinary, will anyone listen to what I say? What if I can’t keep going? What if the data isn’t good?...

Before I even started, I was already overwhelmed by a pile of questions.

Live streaming for ordinary people, documenting life, spreading influence—that’s it. It’s your influence.

You have a product, sell it. You have a community, sell it. Anything you can offer value for can be sold.

Like me, an ordinary person, how can I connect with more people, make more friends? How do I know where the next lucky break is?

What are Moments and live streaming? They are like a huge billboard in the center of a commercial plaza. Not just for a few seconds, but something that can keep running, rolling out continuously.

Today is such a wonderful era. Why wouldn’t we show ourselves? Show the most authentic version of you.

I started creating short videos on Moments from my 2.6 birthday. I like the sincerity and warm social interactions here. I’ve met so many good friends, something that’s unimaginable offline.

What have I gained along the way? Anxiety? Worry? Monetization? Selling things?... I’m a realmotivational-advice expert, but the important thing is that I have strong meaning behind it.

Moments is the last chance for ordinary people to create video content.

This is too important. Knowing is one thing, but doing is what matters. So let’s start continuous creation.

Short videos and live streaming are two paths, each with its own logic. They can be done separately or integrated together.

How to do it well? There are many ideas, lots of expectations. What I want to say is to keep doing it. Only by doing it will you know what suits you and what is valuable to others.

What we are building is a real, multidimensional self, product, or company. You can do it. Only by doing it day after day will the dawn come.

Live streaming has many unclear aspects and many areas to improve. That’s okay. I’ll keep getting better, carrying my thoughts and carving out my own path.

If you haven’t done Moments yet, go do it now. You must do it. Just do it.

Set and invest in White, set and invest in my live stream, set and invest in me. Why?

Because the future is limitless!

Thank you for your support. Good night, see you live tomorrow!
