Common Questions:

1. How to get incentives?

1) Choose the "Shopping" category for live streaming + hang a shopping cart (teaching live streamers who regularly stream and hang carts can also get rewards by selecting the "Teaching" category)

2) The number of viewers from outside WeChat Channels in the live stream should be at least 50 people during the live stream

2. From which scenarios can users be guided into the live stream and be counted as outside WeChat Channels viewers?

1) Share the live stream to one-on-one chats, group chats, andWeChat Moments (WeChat Moments)

2) Guide users to book the live stream, and users enter the live stream from the "Service Notification - Booking" push message

3) Guide users to follow the WeChat Channels, and users enter the live stream from the "Service Notification - Follow" push message

4) Guide users to enter the live stream from scenarios such as the subscription box, chat, or official account articles

5) Guide users to enter the live stream from a mini program
