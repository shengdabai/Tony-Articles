03 When playing movies, considering copyright and related risks, old films are usually played, which perfectly aligns with the needs of most platform users, and you can feel this from the interaction area.

04 Two movies are played for a day, or even several days, hoping to convey the message repeatedly, allowing the live stream content to keep reappearing, regardless of whether users watch or not, the live stream continues, for example, if you live stream every day, constantly reappearing, letting users remember you.
