Note 68

Stay Active #Plain Talk# #Little Secret#

01 Have you ever seen a public account where, after following, you find the latest article was written in 2020? Or when you scan a QR code to add the author, you find it's already invalid...

02 Continuously maintaining an active presence so users can find you is especially important. This is something I often mention: give users a place to go, make sure they can find you.

03 WeChat Channels can connect with the WeChat ecosystem, allowing users to reach you through different channels. You need to test these channels to ensure they're working and keep them updated at all times.

04 Your personal introduction and product details should be updated in a timely manner so users can get the latest information. But you might worry: if what I'm doing now is very different from what I did before, even completely opposite, will that have an impact? That's just part of the growth process.

05 Stay active so your expression remains open to the public, but you don't need to post every day. Maintaining a certain rhythm is necessary.

06 A high-quality self-media account can help users find what they need and stay updated. Pay attention to every detail, and you can do even better.

07 Finally, a little addition: valuing your active presence can lead to commercial success. Sales represent the market, and continuous sales mean staying active.
