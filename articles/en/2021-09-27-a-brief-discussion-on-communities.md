A community is a group of people coming together because of shared values, but more importantly, it's the connection that creates a unique space!

Below are some of my simple notes and insights, all covered in the 2W1H framework.

What: What is a community?

Broadly speaking, it refers to all social relationships that function within certain boundaries, regions, or fields. It can refer to an actual geographic area or the social relationships within a region, or it can refer to more abstract, conceptual relationships.

In simpler terms, it's a group of like-minded people.

Li Xiaolai's teacher elaborates on the value of communities in his book *The Internet Entrepreneurship for the Common People on WeChat*.
