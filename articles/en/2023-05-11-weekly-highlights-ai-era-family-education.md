Many people are getting increasingly exhausted,

and the underlying reason is that they are too "performing."

Those who haven't developed a genuine skill,

can only rely on "performing" to maintain a superficial "perfection."

Live by your strengths, and those with truly strong abilities,

don't need to "perform" to cover up their weaknesses,

because their strengths are enough to overshadow the less important flaws.

Don't always envy others who start with a great hand,

in this infinite game of life,

no one knows what cards they'll get in the next round.

In the world of long-termists,

making the best of the good cards you have in the short term,

and doing well with the temporary bad cards,

is the right way to play.

When you want to grow and improve together with your partner,

it's possible that they aren't willing to take action at first.

But when people see those around them change dramatically, they naturally imitate.

However, even after starting to change, it will definitely take time to truly improve.

It could be five years, or even ten.

Listen to the story of Li Xiaolai and his wife supporting each other:

Children easily get addicted to electronic devices, online games, and short videos,

which is definitely a problem with the "cohesion" of the family.

Children who are easily addicted and hooked,

usually have no other interests or hobbies,

and spend very little time communicating with family members.

So the solution to a child's addiction to games,

lies in the parents' sincere communication.

Besides being communicative,

provide children with better alternatives than gaming,

take them to do more interesting and meaningful things,

and the problem of gaming addiction will be resolved naturally.

School ≠ learning. Everyone who values family education,

can and should learn on their own to improve their knowledge,

solve life's problems, and help their family grow better together.
