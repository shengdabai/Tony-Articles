Have you seen Master Cai's series of comics?

Listen to what Master Cai has to say!

> Cai Zhizhong, male, born in 1948 in Changhua, Taiwan. He is a renowned cartoonist and the honorary president of the Cartoon College at Jilin Animation Institute. He began his career as a professional cartoonist at the age of 15, joined Guangqi Press as a graphic designer in late 1971, and self-taught cartoon drawing techniques. In 1976, he founded Long Cartoon Company.
>
>
> Cai Zhizhong has directed several long-form animated films such as *Old Master* and *Wulongyuan*. Starting in 1983, he began creating four-panel comics, with over 100 works such as *Zhuangzi Says*, *Laozi Says*, *Liezi Says*, *The Drunken Hero*, and *The Bald Detective* published in over 30 countries and regions in multiple languages, with sales exceeding 30 million copies. In September 2013, the People's Education Press will release the first set of "Comic Edition" traditional culture curriculum materials for primary and secondary schools, selecting 260 stories from Cai Zhizhong's comics. In 2015, Cai Zhizhong directed the animated film *Classic of Mountains and Seas: The Legend of Tianyan*, set for release in October. In 1981, Cai Zhizhong won the Golden Horse Award for Best Cartoon Film. In 1985, he was selected as one of Taiwan's Top Ten Outstanding Youth. In 1999, he received an award from the Netherlands' Klaus Prince Foundation, recognizing his "unprecedented recreation of traditional Chinese philosophy and literature through comics." In 2011, he received the Lifetime Achievement Award at the Golden Comic Awards. On November 17, 2020, Cai Zhizhong "retired" to Shaolin Temple, receiving the Buddhist name "Yan Yi."
>
>
>

I was fortunate to attend Cai Zhizhong's talk at the Chaos University, **the teacher used his entire life to complete his beautiful works**! Here are a few comic analyses for you.

> After the class, Master Cai left us with a question: What is the one thing you most want to do in your life? How do you plan to achieve it?

Cai's talk made me feel the wisdom of a master. Especially the last part, which deeply moved me. **If Newton or Einstein could leave behind an hour-long video, how much impact would it have on us?** I still remember Li Shanyou Professor's offline class in Nanjing, where he shared a video focusing on the moments of beauty, like Van Gogh's creation and the dedicated processes of Japanese masters. The visual impact on us is immense. Master Cai also hopes to condense his life and thoughts into this video. Thank you for this opportunity, which connected me with Master Cai and taught me a lot of wisdom. Everyone can create their own beautiful works; our potential is underestimated.

Speaking of this topic, it's really about three words: **What, Why, How**. Who do we want to become? Why do we want to become that person? How do we become that person? These three words have been something I've constantly asked myself. When doing anything, these three words are very effective. Questioning is an art, and it's also the key to open the door to our thinking.

Let me share my thoughts. My greatest wish is to become an **educator**, because I hope to use the fire of education to **light up people's lives**. I don't want to see education as an end in itself, nor equate it with knowledge. We've already learned various kinds of knowledge and taken various exams during our student days. That's not education. What we need to learn is beyond knowledge—thought, logic, expression, etc. **Use education as a pair of glasses to see the world.** I hope I can use my own strength to help more people change their lives, to keep growing, and to make their own choices.

How do I start? **Deepen my expertise in the education field**. Currently, I'm pursuing a Master's degree in Educational Leadership at the University of Manchester. Through studying education, I deeply reflect on the changes in education, and build my thinking model from the perspective of leadership. Combining theory with practice, I continuously improve my professional competence, attend large forums, learn from experts, and internalize my personal reflections. At the same time, I'm also participating in an international English trainer program to refine my teaching skills and accumulate experience. Next, I need to create my own works—articles, books, products—continuously optimize them, and create the education I envision, helping more partners!

The above question asks what I most want to do. Now, thinking about it, what I most want to do is my own school. It doesn't need to be very big—just a micro or small school—where I can put my ideas and educational thoughts into practice. How to operate it? It needs to be accumulated gradually. First, I need to improve my earning ability to save enough money to build and operate the school. Second, I need to recruit excellent partners who share the same vision to empower the school's development. Try educational innovation with students, gradually accumulate, and provide more diversified choices for them. I also need to keep learning, expand my network, and lay the foundation for the things I want to do in the future. This is currently very difficult, but I won't give up. I'll accumulate bit by bit, starting with daily writing, daily sharing on WeChat Channels, and daily reading, using my actions to build my personal brand. Perhaps I can attract like-minded people, and then work together, waiting for the flowers to bloom. Keep going, 😊
