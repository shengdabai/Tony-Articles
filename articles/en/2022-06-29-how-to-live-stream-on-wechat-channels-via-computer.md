How to Live Stream on WeChat Channels via Computer #LiveStreaming #Tools #CaseStudy

01 Whether to live stream via computer or phone has always been a question that troubles many people. My suggestion is to decide based on your habit and convenience. As long as it's easy to operate and sustainable, it's fine.

02 What's the difference between live streaming via computer and push-streaming? Generally speaking, push-streaming refers to live streaming from non-phone devices, such as computers or cameras. When live streaming via computer, you can use push-streaming software like OBS, or you can directly use the WeChat desktop app to live stream.

03 The image below shows how to live stream via the WeChat desktop app. This entry is located in the WeChat settings page, with a dedicated button: WeChat Channels Live Streaming. Once entered, the functions are straightforward, with five main features: Source, Hot Ranking, Comments, Voice Chat, and Product Management.

04 There are many options for choosing the source. It can be the computer desktop, other windows, or other devices, including phones. It can connect up to 20 sources, and you can adjust the position and ratio of each source as you like. This is much better than using a phone.

05 Regular live streaming operations, such as heat, comments, and voice chat, have recently been updated with a new voice chat feature. Previously, computers couldn't support voice chat, but the recent optimization has further enriched the live stream experience.

06 During the live stream, you can also open the phone app to view the live stream and see effects like gifts that the computer doesn't show. Product management can be operated on the phone or synchronized via the computer web page: WeChat Channels Assistant. I usually do both.

07 Live streaming via computer offers a more stable image, avoiding issues like unstable internet, phone overheating, and unclear video. It has certain requirements for users, so you can try it out and decide later.

The above is my hope for your inspiration. At the beginning, you don't need to consider too much—just start with a phone. I look forward to your live stream.
