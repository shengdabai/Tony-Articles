A friend once asked why Tony's younger brother was named Curry. The reason is that the boy wanted to change his name for his WeChat Official Account, and he liked the basketball star Curry, so he chose his English name. Kids really name things carelessly. By the way, curry can also mean curry, and I love it 😍

In the previous episode, we talked about how Tony's memories of Curry's kindergarten days were a bit hazy. At that time, he was busy preparing for the college entrance exam and didn't have deep feelings for it. It felt like nothing special—just a mischievous younger brother. The most interesting game between Curry and Tony was training. Before Tony entered junior high, he trained in martial arts every weekend. With the arrival of his younger brother, it seemed he had a target to practice on. Tony would sometimes invert Curry and take him through splits, flips, and various fun training exercises. But Curry didn't think that way—he felt his brother was bullying him. Was that really the case? Seems like it, haha. Better to be honest.

They would quarrel over each other's cartoons and snacks, and those years of brotherly play were like a dream from which they woke up. Laughter and tears, they quickly reached the time of parting. Tony was going to university in Hefei. Why Hefei? As an Anhui native, he couldn't go to USTC, so Hefei University of Technology was the choice. Honestly, Tony didn't know much about Hefei University—how good it was, or how strong it was. To be honest, Tony still doesn't know. Just kidding. Tony got to study civil engineering, which is among the top in the country and a national first-class discipline.

Tony and his father packed up the luggage and set off for Hefei. It's unclear whether Curry was happy or not after Tony left. Once, Tony asked Curry, "Do you miss your brother?" The answer was, "I don't." That seemed a bit awkward. Tony really missed Curry. This was his first time living away from home. He had never stayed in a dormitory, let alone be so far from his family. Several times, during the midnight hours, he would think about continuing to quarrel with Curry and play happily at home.

Curry started elementary school, and now thinking back, it still made Tony feel a bit sad. He missed out on all the firsts of his younger brother's elementary school years. After graduation, he chose to become a teacher, partly because of Curry. To make up for it, Tony became a children's English education teacher, surrounded by cute kids aged 6 to 12, the so-called divine beasts. A figure of Tony Sheng would often move among the children!
