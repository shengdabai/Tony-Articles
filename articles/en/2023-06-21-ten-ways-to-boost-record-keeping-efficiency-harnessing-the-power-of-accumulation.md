Today, I want to share a method that is very easy to start with, free, and absolutely personalized. That method is—**recording**.

That is, the timer we all know. There are timers everywhere in my home—bathroom, kitchen, bedroom, living room. Different timers have different functions. Some are loud, and some have vibration. Their purpose is to count down, creating a strong push.

For example, when I watch short videos, I start a timer like this. I **place a loud timer in an inconvenient place** so that when it rings, I have to go get it and turn it off. If I don't, it will keep ringing. This bell helps me step away from the world of short videos and successfully overcome the bad habit of mindlessly scrolling through my phone.

There are also timers that are quiet and don't vibrate. I mostly use them for forward counting, **and I also use them for my child to help them understand time. At the same time, this is also a form of early math and numeracy education.**

This method helps us clearly and visually see the accumulation process of habit formation. It's simple, but effective. **Persisting in repeating simple and effective things is the best way to befriend time.**
