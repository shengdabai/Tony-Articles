A journey into the sounds of words, starting with Text-to-Speech (TTS) technology.

Text-to-Speech (TTS) is a remarkable technology that transforms written words into spoken language.

Once a concept from science fiction, this technology has now become a reality, offering not only convenience but also fun and innovation.

The principle behind TTS is not overly complex. It uses speech synthesis engines to convert text into sound. These engines employ advanced artificial intelligence algorithms and speech processing technologies to simulate human voices and intonations, producing realistic audio output.

With technological advancements, the difference between synthetic speech and real human voices has become nearly indistinguishable, opening up more possibilities for TTS applications.

For instance, you can now use the built-in reading function in WeChat to listen to this article and see if it feels different from before. The stiffness and stutters of machine-generated speech are indeed less noticeable now.

TTS technology has become widely used across various fields, bringing us more convenience and enjoyment.

The most common application is voice assistants, such as the well-known Siri and Xiao Ai. You can use simple voice commands to have these assistants answer questions, send messages, and play music.

This not only enhances work efficiency but also adds a new level of interaction with devices.

Additionally, TTS technology can be applied to audiobooks, voice navigation, and voice broadcasting, allowing information to be conveyed in a more vivid and engaging way.

TTS technology continues to innovate, offering us more surprises and convenience.

Beyond synthesizing voices in common languages, TTS can also simulate the voices of specific individuals.

Imagine being able to read an article in the voice of your favorite celebrity or superhero—how much more interesting that would be!

Researchers are also striving to integrate emotion into synthesized speech, making it more expressive.

In the future, TTS technology is expected to expand further into new application areas, meeting people's growing demand for diverse and personalized voices.

The emergence of TTS has made words more vivid and engaging, no longer confined to the page.

Whether it's improving work efficiency or adding entertainment value, TTS has brought us many conveniences and innovations.

Let words in our lives resonate with beautiful sounds—this has become easier than ever.

Why bring up TTS technology? Is it offering modern, busy people a space to freely listen to whatever they want?

Various studies show that sound is one of the most effective learning tools for the brain.

From hunting to modern society, hearing has always been one of the most important senses for humans, playing a crucial role in human evolution and survival.

When people hear sounds, their brains instinctively respond, converting audio information into semantic knowledge.

In contrast, reading relies solely on visual input, requiring more complex cognitive processes to convert information into meaning.

Listening is easier and more efficient than reading.

Listening can bring higher efficiency and better learning outcomes. For example, you can use audio learning to multitask, distributing your mental resources across multiple tasks.

Additionally, listening helps maintain attention and avoid distractions.

Have you ever experienced difficulty concentrating while reading or watching screens, only to find your mind naturally focused on the sound when listening? Listening is more efficient and enduring than reading.

Audio learning has become a popular learning method, gaining increasing application. Audiobooks and lectures allow people to gain useful information while driving, walking, or doing chores, without needing extra time.

Audio explanations and voice guidance have become common methods in travel, language learning, and language teaching. Listening can effectively meet people's learning needs, allowing them to gain knowledge at any moment in life.

Through TTS technology, our efficiency in learning and living has been further enhanced. WeChat has flexibly applied the reading function, making me feel like WeChat has become a personal radio—our own radio.

WeChat, Your Personal Radio

By converting all information within WeChat into speech through text-to-speech technology, we can access knowledge anytime and anywhere. No longer do we need to constantly check WeChat chats and news official accounts.

Text-to-speech technology greatly benefits those who don’t have much time for reading. By listening to WeChat messages, they can stay updated with the latest information during business trips or commutes.

Retaining information becomes more vivid. Listening to messages while driving or walking is often much easier than looking at a screen.

Listening to long text messages can lead to new insights and experiences. It allows people to fully immerse themselves in the information, creating deeper memory responses.

Using voice services, you can gain more than just information. Interestingly, whether it's dubbing, storytelling, legal programs, or daily stories, they can all become the voice of text-to-speech reading.

This transforms information that was once stored on computers into a portable radio you can carry with you.

WeChat, Building a Rich and Interesting Community Culture and Space

WeChat Groups, a Paradise for Sharing Interests and Knowledge

WeChat groups are excellent social tools that can gather people with shared interests.

Whether it's a book club, a music group, or a travel team, WeChat groups provide members with an interactive and communicative platform.

In group chats, people can share their preferences, exchange insights, and offer help. Such shared spaces not only enrich our lives but also spark more creativity.

Official Accounts, a Stage for Personal Expression and Talent

Official Accounts are open platforms where individuals or organizations can share their insights, stories, and expertise.

By creating your own official account, you can showcase your talents and attract and gather readers with similar interests.

Readers can also engage with authors through comments and interactions, forming a truly interactive community.

On such platforms, talents are displayed, ideas are exchanged, and more inspiration and creativity are sparked.

My younger brother created his own official account. He likes to express his thoughts there, even if they are immature reflections from a middle school student. These thoughts are valuable to both him and me.

Mini Programs, Personalized Social Experiences

Through mini programs, we can customize our own social apps or interactive applications.

For example, we can create a mini program for shared yoga classes, allowing yoga enthusiasts to communicate, share experiences, and participate in activities.

Such personalized spaces not only meet our needs but also give us more autonomy and room for innovation.

In the vast social platform of WeChat, each user plays the role of a network node.

I thought of Metcalfe's Law, where only a few nodes have significant influence. These nodes' ability to spread information makes them highly valuable within WeChat.

According to Metcalfe's Law, only a few nodes in a social network have massive spreading power, and these nodes are called "network nodes."

In WeChat, network nodes are often those with a large number of followers and fans. Their content can spread exponentially and spark widespread discussions.

The value of these network nodes lies in their influence. They can drive more users to participate and spread, thus forming a large social network.

In WeChat, network nodes have various ways to increase their value. First, they can attract more users by publishing eye-catching content, such as creative videos, compelling articles, or interesting life updates. These contents can spread rapidly and gain a lot of attention and interaction through the network nodes' influence.

Second, network nodes can collaborate with brands using their influence and popularity. By partnering with well-known brands, network nodes can provide significant exposure and influence for the brands, while also earning economic returns from the collaborations.

User engagement is an essential part of how network nodes demonstrate their value. Network nodes need to maintain close interaction with their followers and fans by responding to comments, liking posts, and leaving messages. Through such interactions, network nodes can further strengthen their relationships with fans and increase user loyalty and engagement.

Should we actively support and participate in these network nodes, striving to enhance our own social value and become influential network nodes, contributing to building a rich and interesting WeChat social network?

Using WeChat to build your world, creating a rich and interesting community culture and space, is becoming an increasingly popular choice for more people.

Through WeChat groups, official accounts, and mini programs, we can communicate and interact with like-minded people, share knowledge and experiences, and grow together.
