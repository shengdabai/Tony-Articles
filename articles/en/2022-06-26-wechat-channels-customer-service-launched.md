01 The WeChat Channels Creator Center should be checked frequently, as there are often updates you might not be aware of, such as the violation query. In fact, the platform regulations are quite detailed. Many people ignore them or only check when a problem arises.

02 The violation query includes detailed explanations. If you're unclear about specific rules, you can check the rule learning section. It's worth spending more time on this.

03 After a violation, you'll receive a safety notice. Be sure to read the explanation carefully. Each deduction should be taken seriously. Take your account seriously. Some creators don't care, and only realize their account is being restricted for a long time, so they choose to start a new one.

04 A new official customer service has been added. This can provide you with better WeChat Channels service. Please note that Tencent customer service and WeChat Channels customer service are separate. Similarly, for mini programs (including mini shops) and Enterprise WeChat, you should find the corresponding official customer service to better resolve issues.

05 Common violation cases can also be found with examples. Take the time to read through them. When you watch other WeChat Channels content, take notes if you spot any issues. Don't have acounting on luck mindset. You often hear people say, "So-and-so did that and it was fine, why can't I?" Are your account and theirs the same? Is there a possibility it wasn't detected during monitoring? There are many possibilities, but being penalized unnecessarily is not acceptable.

06 If you really do violate the rules, don't panic. Stay calm and handle the situation step by step. Your anxiety won't help solve the problem. After all, the platform is still in development, and some functions and regulations are still being improved gradually.

07 The best platform utility is to grow together with the platform.
