Choosing Investment or Consumption #Plain Language# #Live Streaming#

01 Your choice determines your direction moving forward. Amplifying value is your choice to enter the WeChat Channels.

02 When entering the WeChat Channels, most people are acting as consumers—consuming time, attention, and naturally, money, such as live streaming donations.

03 The law of value exchange remains unchanged. What we need to consider clearly is which one offers you greater value and lasts longer—it's an investment.

04 The process of doing a WeChat Channels is like running a company. You need to believe in it, invest time and effort, and also spend some money. The true investment yield maximization lies in whether it's a "bull market" where everyone benefits, or a seemingly bleak "bear market"?

05 Frequently, people consult me and report that their accounts have been running for a long time, yet they have no traffic or income, and they're unsure if they should continue investing. In other words, should we still invest during a "bear market"?

06 Essentially, our long-term gains come from the long "bear market," because most people leave, and many don't see immediate returns, choosing to shift to other areas. The same thing will happen again. By choosing to keep investing during a period that seems to offer nothing, you will eventually reap long-term gains that last for a long time.

07 Compared to consumption, we should place more emphasis on investment. This is long-termism. The core of long-term investment is continuous commitment. Why? Because you see its value and the massive potential of the WeChat Channels. So there's no need to think about anything else—just do it, do it seriously, and keep doing it...
