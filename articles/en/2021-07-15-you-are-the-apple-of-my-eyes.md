# You Are the Apple of My Eye!

Puppy Love refers to romantic feelings or relationships formed during adolescence, or the act of having affection for someone during that period.

Seeing the title and then the word "puppy love," did you think I was about to reveal my love story? Maybe I will!

Apple is the fruit, but it can also be used metaphorically to refer to the most important or precious person or thing. Why? Because it was the apple that made Newton discover gravity, or maybe it's because Steve Jobs made Apple revolutionize mobile devices, or perhaps it's simply indispensable, something we need every day to stay healthy and live long. As for the truth, I might not care to investigate—it's a line from a movie. When I first heard it, I wondered, to whom should I say this?

I do have a puppy love story that I'll never forget, but the main character is someone I've always felt I've wronged. It wasn't until after I left my job in 2020 that our relationship became closer. I wanted to give her a new name, Platane, but she didn't seem to like it much.

Time flies, and I've always thought I was especially lucky. I never considered that we could continue being friends. Now, I dare to think we might be more than friends. I remember what Platane once said to me. Perhaps we were still very young and naive back then, and our feelings weren't as deep. Now, maybe we can still be friends. If love is too deep, maybe seeing each other again can't be friends. I don't know. I just want to cherish this moment.

When was the last time your heart raced, your cheeks flushed, and you felt nervous speaking? It was when I saw Platane. Even though I tried to stay calm, I knew I was flustered. Why? Because she was important! She was the apple, is the apple, and will always be the apple in my eyes!

Sometimes I have to admit I'm a lucky person. Why? Because yesterday, Platane and I had a casual chat, and we discussed some topics. At first, I didn't know how to respond, and I wasn't sure where the problem was. I was just confused.

Today, a book appeared in front of me. It was a book thatFan Deng teacher talked about. I opened it by accident, and suddenly I understood the problem. The author of this book is Mr. Jin Weichun, and the book is called *Life Is Only One Thing*. What is this one thing?

**_Life is simple, simple enough to be reduced to one thing! — Cultivate yourself._**

**_See myself._**

The person who doesn't understand themselves is themselves. I used to think I accepted myself and believed I was open, but the truth was not like that.

In our conversations, I could feel Platane's tolerance for me. She took care of my feelings, and sometimes I would unintentionally express my hidden thoughts. Maybe I didn't think that way, but she could sense something different. I think the problem was with me.

I'm not good at expressing myself. I'm quite introverted, and I speak quickly, partly to mask my nervousness. If my friends who know me read this, they might think I'm being modest or just making things up. I want to appear extroverted and confident, to be a person who is at ease, but I haven't achieved that yet.

The words from Jin Mr. made me realize that some of my behaviors were actually manifestations of self-aggrandizement, while I minimized the people and events around me. This created an image of me being cold, but it was actually arrogance.

I never thought of myself as arrogant, but I can't deny that I have been. I judged others carelessly, spoke without thinking, and said things I wasn't responsible for. For example, I showed disdain for the civil service, but I hadn't really thought about it. Was it because I resented civil service work? Or was it because my friends' choices made me question it? I just didn't like some people's behavior, but I developed a prejudice against the whole group and treated others with arrogance.

Any profession, any choice, requires a cost that those who haven't experienced it can't imagine.

I want to apologize to the people around me. My arrogance hurt you, and I'm sorry!

Jin Mr. mentioned the value of apologizing, no matter how big or small the issue, we should learn to apologize and take responsibility. This is something I need to learn. Some mistakes I'm willing to admit, but others I'm not. Why? Because I don't accept myself, and I don't acknowledge that I'm like this. These are things I can't accept, while minor mistakes I'm willing to admit. The most fundamental mistakes are the ones I need to acknowledge, and sometimes I'm stubborn, refusing to accept them.

**True remorse is passionate, powerful, and joyful. True remorse is eager to change, eager to be different. That's what it means to feel ashamed.**

The shortcut in life is to admit mistakes. I don't know if I've taken this path, but I know I'll try to walk it in the future.

When you are a person who fully accepts yourself and is genuine, you have charm. People will want to be with you. Improve yourself, and others will want to be with you. Essentially, most of the problems we face don't come from the outside, but from within. Have I improved myself?

I really enjoy being the teacher, thinking I'm all-knowing and always wanting to add my two cents. I hate this version of myself. I don't like being verbose, and I don't want to hear long-winded advice, yet I've become that person myself.

Know yourself better, be willing to change, be willing to face every situation around you, and be willing to start with small things. For example, be willing to listen instead of just talking. I apologize for all the inappropriate things I've said. Listening carefully and learning to sense things deeply is really important. I know this, but I haven't done it. I need to reflect deeply, and I ask you, my friends, to supervise me.

It seems I haven't told you about my story with Platane. Sorry, I couldn't satisfy your curiosity. This article came from our conversation while we were walking. After I returned, I thought carefully about the issues we discussed, and this is what I came up with.

A phrase that made me think for a long time was, she said, "If I had the chance to choose again, I might not have chosen you. Sometimes I make the other person feel tired."

After thinking it over, I realize I was definitely self-centered. My behavior reflected my thoughts, and my thoughts reflected my core values. I used to be confused by this sentence—was I bad? What did I do wrong? How could I make it up?

Today, I accept myself. I do need to cultivate myself, and I believe I will be better. I can make Platane more willing to talk to me, to confide in me, and to complain to me.

Cultivation is what I need to do, growth is what I expect. I'm willing to do anything, and doing more is my ability. I want to do it with heart!

**_I like you, my puppy love!_**
