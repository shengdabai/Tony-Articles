Big News: WeChat Channels Assistant Upgrade #Live Streaming #Tools #Plain Language#

01 WeChat Channels Assistant has been significantly upgraded, further enhancing commercial operations. Streamers must study this carefully and do thorough post-live reviews.

02 The operation assistant's permissions have been upgraded, allowing the addition and promotion of links (live stream previews, red envelope covers, enterprise WeChat business cards). The WeChat Channels Assistant backend can now view real-time data dashboards, as shown in the figure below.

Supplement: Functions such as hanging red envelopes, live interaction, comment wall, and prohibited content handling remain the same as before.

03 The data dashboard has been optimized and upgraded on the original basis, with richer content and more comprehensive information. The visualization of live stream data is more refined.

04 Left-side data: Transaction data — displaying sales amount, orders, and number of people during different time periods along with charts. Traffic data — details of traffic during different time periods, traffic sources, and traffic interaction. This provides a clearer understanding of user sources, such as specific data from public and owned audiences.

05 Middle data: Core data — traffic indicators, interaction indicators, and transaction indicators. At least one item is displayed, up to a maximum of eight items. The font size of the data is larger, highlighting key metrics for live stream analysis. Below the product list, each item has specific data, which can be monitored in conjunction with data from the small shop or other platforms.

06 Right-side data: Conversion analysis — traffic conversion and product conversion. Adjust the live stream rhythm in time based on conversion data. User profiles and channel distribution are more intuitively displayed, such as user profiles for successful transactions, which can be used to tailor content to attract more potential customers. You can also see the current audience demographics, age, and regional distribution in the live stream, such as male, 20-30 years old, Northeast China. You can then adjust your live stream script accordingly based on this group.

07 Overall, this upgrade to the WeChat Channels Assistant allows streamers and operators to more clearly and intuitively understand their audience, laying a solid foundation for future commercial operations. It also emphasizes data analysis and user modeling. Each review and operational iteration will make the live stream better.
