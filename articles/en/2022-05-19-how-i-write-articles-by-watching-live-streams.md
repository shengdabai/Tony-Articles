How I Write Articles by Watching Live Streams #Plain Talk #Little Secret#

01 Without realizing it, I've written over 80 articles. I want to tell you that writing isn't hard at all. The core secret is to watch a lot and think deeply. 02 Why do live streams give me so many inspirations? Because I watch them with a purpose. I'm studying the account, not just consuming (spending money and time are both forms of consumption). 03 Are there any live streams that make me forget my purpose? The answer is yes. That's even more reason for me to write and analyze them. For example, Liu Run's "Opening Dumpling Series" live streams can keep you and others engaged. 04 Don't ignore any small idea you have. Even if it's a sudden flash of inspiration, write it down. After some time, you'll find these are all treasures. 05 Live stream rooms are varied. You can set aside a specific time to observe them. If you don't find anything interesting, I have two views: one, you watch too little; two, if there's nothing you're interested in, that means you have potential. 06 Companies create users, and video channels meet user needs. Dig deeper, and you might find business opportunities. 07 Being knowledgeable about video channels isn't hard, and making money through them isn't hard either. The hard part is being someone who puts in hard work and consistently invests time here, because video channels really do have your potential.

I'm done. Thank you for being the one who makes things happen!
