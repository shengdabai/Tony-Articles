Logic is a tool that helps us think clearly and make reasonable judgments. If we consider logic as a tool, then in itself, logic is meaningless. The results we gain by using our tools are infinitely more important and meaningful than the tools themselves. Saying that logic is the ultimate meaning of life is as absurd as saying that a hammer is the ultimate meaning of a carpenter's life, or that using a brush is the ultimate meaning of a painter's life. For a carpenter, a hammer is very important, but creating things people can use by using a hammer is far more meaningful than simply using a hammer for the sake of using it. Similarly, a brush is also very important for an artist. But saying that using a brush is the ultimate meaning of an artist's life is absurd. For an artist, using a brush to create paintings, for the purpose of using a brush, is far more meaningful than merely using a brush. Finding things that are meaningful to our personal lives, based on our talents and abilities, everyone will discover something meaningful to them, and everyone can contribute to humanity. When we make the most of the benefits nature provides, bringing benefits to those around us and fun to ourselves, life becomes meaningful.

> Independent thinking is a pursuit, patience with doubt, a love for contemplation, a slow assertion, a readiness to consider, a careful desire for arrangement and planning; and a hatred for every form of dogma. — Francis Bacon

As parents, our task is to instill a wide range of values in our children. Although some parents around the world choose to instill valueless ideas in their children, those of us who do not want our children to grow up to be criminals or engage in various inappropriate behaviors will work harder. Values and morality are part of this, but there are other important aspects of life to focus on. It starts with finding two ways to cross the street, either go from there or stop. If we stop explaining the world to our children after they learn how to cross the street, then perhaps you should stop reading and go back to surfing the internet for funny cat pictures. Through critical thinking, we gain a method to evaluate and improve our judgment. It enables us to enter almost any situation and figure out the logic behind what is happening in that situation. It provides us with a way to learn new experiences through a process of constant self-assessment. Therefore, critical thinking allows us to form reasonable beliefs and judgments, and on this basis, to lay the foundation for a "rational and reasonable" emotional life.

This is the direct lesson we need to pass on to our children: do not accept visible logic at face value. Although not all problems are complex enough to require a scientific approach, some do require some inference to determine whether they are correct. Take the example above—how many children would immediately accept the wrong conclusion? Perhaps I'm being paranoid or thinking about the end of the world, but I think it's a pandemic. Children become lazy and unable to be self-sufficient because their parents tell three-year-olds to take off their jeans after they cry, or ask them to put down their toys. These are the parents who are responsible for their children's science projects while they are playing video games. These children grow up lacking simple problem-solving skills that make life easier. Do you remember the plastic stacking toys you had when you were growing up? Well, parents didn't have such early developmental toys, but instead left their children in front of the TV. Although there are some educational programs on TV, they are not the same as hands-on experiences.

Correctly applying reasoning knowledge not only improves our way of thinking and the accuracy of handling things, but also brings a lot of convenience to our lives. Reasoning also involves writing papers, distinguishing different viewpoints, and analyzing and researching issues. Correctly applying reasoning can help us solve many problems in life. Logic helps us synthesize a large amount of material in a shorter time, process a lot of information, and improve work and study efficiency. Its fundamental meaning is to train and improve people's logical thinking abilities, promote their conscious use of logical knowledge, and improve the quality of learning and work. It not only helps us correctly understand things, moving from the known to the unknown; it also helps us accurately and rigorously express and argue our ideas; it also helps us expose fallacies and refute sophistry; and finally, it helps us cultivate an analytical rational spirit and a sense of innovation.
