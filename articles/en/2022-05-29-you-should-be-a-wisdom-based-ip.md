Knowledge and Wisdom, what kind of IP should you build? #PlainTalk #LiveStreaming #ShortVideo#

01 No matter if you're doing knowledge-based content or anything else, you're essentially building a persona and establishing your influence. Let's clarify what an IP is. IP (Intellectual Property) literally translates to intellectual property, but in reality, it refers to any work you own. In short, an IP is a product that can generate effects.

02 To put it simply, your persona, positioning, product, service, works, and everything you want to present to the outside world are all your IP. So, are you aiming to be a knowledge-based or a wisdom-based IP?

03 The founder of Dedao, Luo Pan, shared a point: knowledge usually describes a law in a positive way, while wisdom is responsible for questioning that law.

04 From my understanding, conveying a law is about knowledge, while critical thinking is about wisdom. It sounds like wisdom is more powerful, and we also hope to become wisdom-based IPs. But if we don't have a certain foundation or understanding of the law, how can we analyze and think critically?

05 In the long run, becoming a wisdom-based IP is more rewarding. Because knowledge can be copied, it can become outdated, and it can be retrieved by machines. But when we possess wisdom and share our thinking, this can last for a long time—perhaps even be passed down for thousands of years like Confucius.

06 WeChat Channels is a tool, and through it, you can amplify your IP. What do you hope it will present in the future? If it rolls out within the WeChat ecosystem, what do you most want it to introduce about you? Here's a quick note: if we leave this world, what would be your epitaph?

07 Marketing is about making a deal without you being present. Isn't WeChat Channels a great marketing tool for you? From my perspective, tools will change, methods will change, but what never changes is that you never stop thinking.

May we both find our own wisdom-based IP characteristics.

I'm done writing. Thank you for being the one who makes things happen!
