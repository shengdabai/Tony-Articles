During the May Day holiday, while reviewing each Tea Talk session, we noticed that there's one issue that almost every time comes up in the free open mic section:

> **How should we motivate our partner?**

Why is this question so important?

Li Xiaolai's system of family education points this out bluntly:

> In family building, what is most important?
>
> **The relationship between the couple.**

Why?

> Because, in all probability, children are the replication of their parents.
>
> More likely than not, children from unhappy couples often face a problem in their ability to interact with others.
>
> Family building is, in fact, an activity of creating an organization. And whether one is creating or participating in an organization, the ability to interact with others is essential—it's the foundation of everything good happening. Without this ability, it's nearly impossible to move forward.
>
> People raised in families with unhappy couples often struggle to build genuine trust with others.
>
> **Therefore, everything a parent tries to teach their child, or any effort made for the child, is ultimately less important than a harmonious marital relationship.**

This perspective, along with the subsequent chain of reasoning, is logical and enlightening.
