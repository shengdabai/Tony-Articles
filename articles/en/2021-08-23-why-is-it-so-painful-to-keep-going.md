# Why Is It So Painful to Keep Going?

134th Original Piece

When you have the idea of sticking with something, most of the time, you won't actually keep doing it.

Sticking with something means you're assuming it's painful. When would you say you're sticking with something?

Sticking with the gym, sticking with memorizing words every day, sticking with reading a certain number of pages…

This is like your to-do list or sticky notes—full of all sorts of information. You can take out a pen and see how many of these things you actually need to stick with to complete.

Every time I receive a message telling me to keep going and keep working hard, I don’t thank the sender at all. It has nothing to do with gratitude. At the very least, it shows the sender doesn’t understand that I don’t need to stick with anything.

You think everything is important, and it seems like you can’t give up on anything, that you have to do everything. But you ignore individuality and don’t understand what differentiation means.

Why do we habitually think that important things must be stuck with?

Because we never seriously consider the meaning of the thing. We don’t think clearly about why we’re doing it. What others think is important, what experts say is valuable, the burdens society places on us…

Let go of all that. They don’t belong to you. What truly belongs to you is what you think for yourself.

Think clearly, and then act. Compared to how long or how many times, it’s more important to do it consistently. Once, twice, three times… keep accumulating, do it enough, and this thing becomes a habit.

For example, I post daily on WeChat Channels and on my WeChat Official Account. Video creation and writing are things I do every day. Do I find it difficult? Actually, no. I’m accumulating, and every piece is part of my practice.

When I set too high an expectation for myself, I fall into unrealistic fantasies. My content is still in its early stages. What I can do is try to express clearly, and every encouragement means a lot to me.

Avoid falling into your comfort zone. Everything looks wonderful, you become overly confident, but then you need to cool down and refine your content more.

The motivation to keep going: find meaning, see value, look to the future. Of course, there’s also a more direct and important reason why most people keep going: tasting the sweetness!

I can earn a little income from my creations, and that makes me happy. My creative motivation is further strengthened. What if you don’t get any income?

Lower the threshold for income. A like, a comment, a share, a follower—these can all be your income. After all, we’re looking at the long term.

One final reminder: do more, do more, do it well. Never let long-termism become just long-term talk. If you can think it through, do it. If you can’t, do it anyway!

Monday, give yourself a goal that doesn’t require you to stick with it!
