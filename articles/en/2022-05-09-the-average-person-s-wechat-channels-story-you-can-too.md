The Average Person's WeChat Channels Story
#Plain Talk# #Little Secret#

01 Telling my WeChat Channels story for the first time, maybe it can inspire you a bit!

02 Starting from reading Li Xiaolai's book *WeChat Internet Plain People Entrepreneurship*, I decided to post short videos every day, and over time, I created more than 500 videos, documenting my life.

03 During the creation process, I tried different formats, but the followers didn't grow, no interaction, content creation plateau? I didn't know if I should continue.

04 Joining various training camps, buying all sorts of courses, I was fortunate to become an operator for Xiao Daye, later becoming an operations manager, on the WeChat Channels creation path, I was not only doing it myself, but also helping more people.

05 Nurturing influencers, creating and sharing, accumulating experience, half of my WeChat contacts are WeChat Channels creators, helping more people start their WeChat Channels journey.

06 I entered a state of freelance work, and also received help from kind people, projects and consultations, one by one, things started to take off, I got some results, some credibility, and gradually became known to more people.

07 On WeChat Channels, we're not novices, we're just newbies, accepting our "clumsiness," and we'll get better!
