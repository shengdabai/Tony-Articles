Private Notes from Tony Sheng

Note No. 30

Live Stream Review #Live Stream #Case Sharing#

01 Review is about finding problems and optimizing the process. Never forget the original purpose. 02 Don’t talk without data—it’s just being irresponsible. You can check relevant data through the WeChat Channels assistant tool or your phone’s background. Speak with evidence, analyze with references, and always look at the data. (The data dashboard in the backend includes six dimensions: basic data, interaction data, gift data, e-commerce data, detailed gift data, and product-selling data.) 03 For example, new streamers should not only pay attention to basic and interaction data but also focus on gift data. From this dimension, you can see how much the audience acknowledges the streamer and the content, helping newcomers quickly find their positioning. However, for product-selling streamers, product-selling data is the key dimension to focus on during a review. 04 Pay special attention to the five key elements: pre-warming, streamer, visuals, scene control, and interaction. (You can combine live stream replay to do the review. Still, remember to base your analysis on evidence, not just feelings or memory.) 05 Keep your eyes and ears open; being a streamer means being a multi-talented person. If one person isn’t enough, the team can cover it. Clear division of labor ensures a solid foundation for the review. 06 Propose practical plans, not just problems. A good review should be able to solve problems. 07 The core secret of live streaming is to watch the live stream a lot. Watch a lot, always watch. Don’t isolate yourself and try to create things in a vacuum!
