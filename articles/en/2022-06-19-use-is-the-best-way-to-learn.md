Note 70

Use is the best way to learn—what does that have to do with you?

01 Friend's Question: There's so much to learn on WeChat Channels, so much I don't know. What should I do?

   My Answer: My suggestion is to first do what you can. Don't overthink it.

02 Often people tell me we should learn more. Maybe we can learn more, without limits on what we learn. But when we learn, please choose the focus well, practice seriously, and put it into action.

03 Most things are unrelated to us. On WeChat Channels, there's never-ending noise, and all kinds of people and events will appear. Instead of commenting or criticizing, focus on what you can do.

04 The best way to practice live streaming is to use live streaming. Use it well, get familiar with every function, and understand it thoroughly.

05 Sometimes you'll hear hosts talk about so-called live streaming logic or break down the live streaming process. In my understanding, it has little to do with you. What you need to understand is that live streaming helps you filter and find users who like you.

06 A commercial loop must exist. That is, users are attracted, then they buy, then they experience the product, then they become loyal users, and then they repurchase, which helps us recommend the product. Think clearly about how live streaming can connect this commercial loop.

07 Pay attention to what you should focus on, find the key points, and ignore what's unnecessary and not relevant to you. Keep using it, keep practicing, and you can definitely do it.

I'm done. Thank you for your support if you're able to make things happen!
