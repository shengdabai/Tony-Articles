# September Greeting

> Published: 2021-09-02 · [中文](../zh/2021-09-02-9%E6%9C%88%E4%BD%A0%E5%A5%BD.md) | [English](../en/2021-09-02-september-greeting.md)

---

Seeing some friends reflecting on August and planning for September, it seems I should write something too. Let's say seven points, so you can have a clearer impression. Eight points are too much, after all, seven is just right!

1. I experienced a spontaneous road trip, thanks to two lovely ladies who drove me, which gave me a deeper understanding of Qinghai. The most beautiful sky, changing weather, pleasant environment, and breathtaking starry nights—there's so much memory, and even more reverence for nature. Welcome to the beautiful Qinghai! Friends must be welcomed, with good food, drink, and fun. Yeah, that's pretty good👌

2. I had deep conversations with friends, each day practicing the principle of providing a little value. Sometimes I wonder if I've really helped a lot of friends this year, and it's especially satisfying to have people willing to listen to my thoughts and give me feedback. Let's chat when you have time!

3. The second unit of my article for the University of Manchester was successfully submitted. With 4,000 words of academic requirements, extensive literature reading, and multi-angle thinking, I revised and revised, and finally settled my anxious heart yesterday. I still have many shortcomings and need to improve my academic research skills, but I'm glad I'm not excellent—because that means there's more room to grow!

4. The DipTesol International Trainer Program is moving faster, and my thinking about English teaching has become broader. Being a trainer means considering more than just being a teacher. With weekly courses and tasks, discussions, and live sessions, I'm grateful for the care from all the teachers. Keep accumulating and asking questions, and I'm lucky. Oh, by the way, the exam is approaching too. Am I nervous? You tell me!

5. My younger brother's plan to travel with me was also restricted by pandemic controls, which left him a bit disappointed. I wanted to take him to experience different lives, visit places he hasn't been to, and try different cuisines. Of course, I'll make up for it during the National Day holiday. My parents' favoritism seems to want me to earn money at home. Making money online is also pretty good, so I'll try it. Haha, let's see!

6. Health issues—this leg suddenly started hurting. After a check-up, I found out my uric acid levels were high, which made me a bit uneasy, but I quickly calmed down. From now on, I'll have this pain reminding me to eat healthily, avoid alcohol and sugar, rest well, and not stay up late. Most importantly, I need to exercise more. You tell me, isn't uric acid just my health manager? Haha, let's listen and follow the advice!

7. This point is because I got carried away writing and it was already past midnight. I secretly made it look like one article equals two days off. Maybe I should save more articles in the future so I always have something to work with!

Thank you for your time. Goodnight or good morning, what matters is that September is here, and we're here!
