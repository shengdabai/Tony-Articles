This isn’t scaremongering. Just because we don’t pay attention to artificial intelligence doesn’t mean it isn’t changing the world.

Reports mentioning artificial intelligence often pair it with the keyword “unemployment.” Media still tend to focus on the negative, but if we think carefully, are the industries that artificial intelligence is set to disrupt only those that are easily replaceable?

Take the teaching profession, for example. There are claims that teachers are extremely vulnerable to being replaced by artificial intelligence. Compared to the all-knowing AI, a teacher’s professional knowledge seems insignificant. Wait a minute—this kind of argument seems to have already existed when the internet rose to prominence...

Another side argues that the emotional value and counseling support provided by teachers are something AI cannot replace. Just like a lot of online courses, from both research and effectiveness perspectives, online education hasn’t replaced offline learning—it’s just a helpful supplement. Teaching involves interacting with people, and things that involve human relationships are hard to replace.

Different viewpoints, relative perspectives, and varying attitudes toward artificial intelligence—so will artificial intelligence really disrupt the education industry?

What about the legal profession? For tasks like searching legal documents and acting as a private legal advisor, AI clearly has advantages in efficiency and cost. But when it comes to defending a client’s interests, arguing on their behalf in court, or debating with opposing counsel, AI probably doesn’t have much of an edge.

In fact, whether we support or oppose it, artificial intelligence has already been changing the working models of many industries. To put it bluntly, it’s already disrupting all industries and jobs.

The concept of artificial intelligence can be traced back to the 1950s. At that time, researchers began to think about how to make computers possess the thinking and decision-making abilities similar to humans.

However, under the hardware and algorithm conditions of that era, the development of artificial intelligence was very limited. With the advancement of computing power and algorithms, artificial intelligence entered a new stage of development.

During the pandemic, artificial intelligence made tremendous breakthroughs, thanks to the growth of big data, the improvement of computing power, and the development of key technologies such as machine learning and deep learning. These technologies allow computers to automatically learn from big data and improve their performance, enabling them to carry out tasks more accurately and intelligently.

Artificial intelligence technology has been widely applied in image recognition, speech recognition, natural language processing, machine translation, and intelligent driving.

In the field of image recognition, artificial intelligence can now accurately identify objects, faces, and scenes. This technology has been widely used in security surveillance, medical diagnosis, and traffic management. In speech recognition and natural language processing, artificial intelligence can understand and interpret the meaning and emotion of human language, making intelligent assistants and voice interaction a reality, and providing convenience for daily life.

In the medical field, artificial intelligence is also becoming increasingly widespread. It can help doctors with disease diagnosis and prediction, develop treatment plans, and assist in surgery. In education, artificial intelligence technology has been widely used in personalized teaching, learning support, and intelligent assessment, improving learning outcomes and students’ personal development.

Artificial intelligence is also showing great potential in many other fields, such as transportation, finance, and manufacturing. Intelligent transportation systems can improve traffic efficiency and vehicle safety. Robots and automation systems can enhance the efficiency and quality of industrial production. Intelligent financial systems can better manage financial risks and provide personalized services.

In the past, traffic congestion and traffic accidents often caused headaches. However, with the development of artificial intelligence technology, traffic management has seen significant improvements. Intelligent transportation systems can monitor traffic conditions, optimize traffic signals, and even coordinate traffic flow through communication between intelligent vehicles. This has made urban traffic more efficient, safe, and environmentally friendly.

Decades ago, teaching methods in the education field mainly relied on traditional classrooms and printed textbooks. Today, learning has become more personalized, interactive, and global. Artificial intelligence technology allows individual students to gain better learning outcomes through online courses, intelligent teaching tools, and personalized learning plans. Education has shifted from catering to the masses to addressing the needs of each student, stimulating their interest and potential in learning.

The art world has not been ignored by artificial intelligence either. Artists have started to combine artificial intelligence with the creative process, producing stunning artistic works. Artificial intelligence can learn and imitate the styles of artists to generate entirely new artworks. This new form of artistic creation not only enriches the art world but also sparks people’s thinking about creativity and innovation.

However, in the development of artificial intelligence, we also face some challenges and issues. As the application scope of artificial intelligence expands, we need to think about and solve a series of ethical, privacy, and security issues.

In addition, the replacement of human jobs by artificial intelligence has also raised concerns, as we discussed earlier.

We need to find appropriate solutions to ensure that artificial intelligence technology brings more benefits and fairness to society.

Especially in the areas of privacy and data security, there are currently no mature solutions. We need to place greater emphasis on data protection and avoid leaking personal information carelessly.

Perhaps the “God platforms” understand us best, knowing our preferences better than we do, and we can’t help but get drawn into the content they recommend.

Interestingly, netizens have their own methods too. By registering new accounts and naming them something like “so-and-so,” they avoid providing real information, allowing fake data to be collected by the platform. In this way, it’s also a kind of solution.

Society, the government, and internet companies are also investing more in regulating AI-generated content, using various means to strengthen security protection.
