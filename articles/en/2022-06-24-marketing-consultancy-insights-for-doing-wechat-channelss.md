Note 75

Insights from Marketing Consultancy for Doing WeChat Channels #Plain Language #Case Study #Tools

01 Marketing consultancy helps clients develop strategies, such as positioning analysis, needs analysis, and customer analysis. It's not about what we think—advertising or malicious sales.

02 A key part of marketing consultancy is conducting research. Without research, there's no authority to speak. How do you conduct research? Simply go into the customer's world, which is field research. For example, to research a restaurant, becoming a server is one approach a marketing consultant might take, and it's over a period of time.

03 We also need a marketing mindset in the process of creating video content. How to better discover user needs, provide products users need, and understand users is especially important.

04 For example, testing some accounts, we found that a video channel featuring a list of traditional Chinese medicine books performs well on WeChat Channels. Editing lectures from national scholars and nutrition experts, and attaching health-related books, makes it easy to sell.

05 A relatively popular product, black sesame balls, why do so many people have a demand? Health, black hair, rich nutrition, and the benefits of black sesame are all reasons that encourage users to make a purchase.

06 Using a marketing mindset helps us better uncover needs and find users' essential needs. Even if it's what users think is essential, as long as we can find it and the product quality is good, there's a big market. (Why do we say it's what users think is essential? For example, learning English, is a vocabulary book the core of English? Obviously not, yet most people still buy vocabulary books. If you try to promote writing materials instead, it's very difficult.)

07 One thing to add: advertising and marketing are different. Advertising focuses more on creative execution, such as organizing an event with specific requirements, creating an advertising plan, and then bidding. Marketing focuses on needs, doesn't require too much creativity, and solid research is more useful.
