Technological innovation can help the world advance more quickly and address the most pressing challenges we face today. Achieving this goal requires pooling global wisdom, experience, and expertise. The Gates Foundation is working with Chinese partners to help build a future in which everyone has the opportunity to live a healthy and productive life. *This speech was prepared by Bill Gates for a special event hosted by the Global Health Drug Research and Development Center on June 15, and I would like to thank the moderator for their introduction, as well...*
