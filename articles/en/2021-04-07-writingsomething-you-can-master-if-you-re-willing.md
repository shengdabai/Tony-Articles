**Why Join Li Xiaolai's Writing Training Camp**

The reason is simple—**Li Xiaolai**. I joined the Dedao column *The Path to Financial Freedom* quite early on, but didn't take it seriously until 2020, when I started to study it seriously. It really made me regret not doing so earlier—I should have done it sooner, missing out on such a valuable resource. When I saw Li Xiaolai's writing training camp, I felt a lot of motivation. How does Li Xiaolai teach writing? After all, we're both English teachers, and I seemed to have fallen into a logical trap. English teachers' writing skills should be... that's strong, and the facts confirm it. Plus, such an incredible one-year course—what reason do I have to say no? No one recommended it, but just following and learning and learning again on the WeChat official account, I decided to sign up without hesitation!

What's the biggest challenge in writing?

Thinking back, the biggest challenge I used to face was **lack of motivation**! I never thought about writing, let alone writing consistently. Even though I knew writing was important, I thought it would help me a lot. At the very least, it would allow me to express more, and the more I expressed, the higher my input requirements would become. That's the gap between thinking and doing. To change my lack of writing, joining the writing training camp was a great opportunity.

Has it been solved now?

Definitely, it has! How could there be no motivation? With the **support and help of the 261 members in the 102nd class of the Li Xiaolai Writing Training Camp**, along with the guidance from the assistants and group leaders, and their encouraging words, how could I not have motivation? Plus, everyone around me is excellent, and once I start writing, I can't stop. **Excellence is a habit**—this habit is long-term value. Since the camp started, I've written every day, and I've written more and more. This feeling is amazing, and I've also received a lot of support from my fellow members, which is very uplifting 😁

The Top Three Biggest Gains

In the past two months, I've written over 200,000 words and 111 articles—unbelievable!

1. **Writing consistently, regardless of the number of words**—even copying and paraphrasing the original text is a form of practice. This habit might be hard to break!

2. **Learning to appreciate and praise**—surrounded by excellent people, I'm somewhat introverted, and sometimes I don't know how to express myself. But when I received so much encouragement and even some embarrassing praise from my fellow members, I wanted to give back to more people. I started as a group reviewer from the beginning, and reviewing is also a form of writing practice. Plus, it's an opportunity to connect with my fellow members, gaining growth and a lot of love (⑉°з°)-♡

3. **Writing has become more methodical**—using techniques like highlighting, perception, analysis, analogy, and rhetoric. There are practical and effective methods that are easy to implement. With all these tools at my disposal, I've come to understand writing better. After all, this isn't a high barrier—**if you want to do it, you can**!

What Learning and Writing Habits Will I Continue?

**Maybe I don't need to stick to them anymore.** I remember Li Xiaolai said that in his understanding, there's no such thing as **effort or persistence**. This saying has stayed with me. Why not use effort and persistence? Because of passion, and because I feel inspired. Before, I needed to persist with writing because I thought it was difficult and had to endure and keep going. Now, for me, writing doesn't seem to require much effort or persistence anymore. Just **write**, sincerely. After the training camp, I'll write every day, **New Work Column: Tony Learning Perception**, recording my daily learning insights, internalizing the knowledge I've gained from various platforms, forming my own understanding, and producing better outputs. I also plan to complete my two columns: one is about my story with my younger brother, documenting the growth of two little pigs, and the other is about my experiences with martial arts and taekwondo, sharing my journey and understanding of these disciplines. My next plan is to launch my WeChat official account, though I've already registered the account, but I haven't decided on the specific content yet. Now I've already started, and I hope to find a suitable direction, and to work on it carefully every week!
