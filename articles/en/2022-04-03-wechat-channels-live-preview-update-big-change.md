Sticky Note Background

【Function Update Synchronization】

WeChat Channels Live now supports creating multiple live previews at the same time and adding more preview display scenarios.

Please update WeChat to the latest version for details as follows:

1. How many previews can you create?
Up to 100, you can manage them via your phone or the WeChat Channels Assistant dashboard.

2. Can you modify the theme and live time of existing previews?
No, you can only cancel them and recreate them. When you cancel a preview, the audience who had reserved will receive a notice.

Preview Display

3. Where will the live previews be displayed?
Including but not limited to the author's profile page, the live end page, the author's video detail page (only the most recent one is displayed), the live room link (requires the host to push it), the associated Official Account homepage (requires setting the Official Account identity to display), etc. You can also save the preview QR code.

4. After having multiple previews, can you choose which one to display?
No, most scenarios default to displaying the preview closest to the live time.

Live Reminder

5. When will the audience receive a reminder for the live stream?
If the live stream starts within one hour before or after the preview time, all reserved audience will receive a reminder. For example, if the preview time is 19:00, the live stream must start between 18:00 and 20:00.

6. Will the reminder be sent if the live stream starts earlier or later than the preview time by more than one hour?
If the live stream starts more than one hour later, the reminder will not be sent, and the reservation will expire. If the live stream starts more than one hour earlier, the reminder will be sent at the original reservation time. For example, if the preview time is 19:00 and the live stream starts at 16:00, the reminder will be sent at 19:00.

7. If there are multiple previews, and the live times cover multiple events, what happens?
Only the most recent preview will trigger a reminder. For example, if the preview times are 19:00 and 20:00, and the live stream starts at 19:00, only the 19:00 reminder will be sent.

8. Is there a relationship between the live reminder for a followed streamer and the reminder for a reserved live stream?
No. The follow reminder may be sent after following the streamer, while the reservation reminder is sent after reserving. If there is a conflict, the reservation reminder takes priority.

Sixth Note

04 Friend's question: There are so many changes in live streaming, and I don't know how to operate. Is it not suitable for me to live stream?

   Tony says: Do your best to practice. First, watch other streamers to learn, second, study each live stream button repeatedly. Don't just listen to others; know exactly what to do.

05 Live stream previews must be used. If enough people come in when you start live, more platform traffic will be directed your way.

06 Preview settings should be attractive. What does it mean to be attractive? Ask yourself what content would make people reserve a live stream.

07 Never treat free live streams and reservations as cheap things. They are excellent places to showcase your value.
