Tony Sheng's Owned Audience Notes

Note 18

01 Whether live streaming is suitable for you depends on whether you've provided a good exit for your users.

02 Some people say they do live streaming just for the fun of it. I think that's not sustainable. If you're doing live streaming, do it seriously, do it solidly, and do it consistently—because you want the platform to see your effort.

03 I understand this as the power of repetition. There are big names who give valuable content, but ordinary people aren't suited for that. What we can do consistently is repetition—polish your content or product, refine it, and refine it again, maybe 100 times.

04 Offline stores: imagine how many customers you'd get in one day of opening. During the pandemic, you couldn't even open the store. But WeChat Channels live streaming gives you an excellent window. All you need to do is go live.

05 Live streaming should have a plan, it's easy and pleasant, and most importantly, it should have commercial value. It allows you to connect with your audience. After all, saying you're great is different from giving you money and making a transaction with you.

06 How do you keep going? For example, publicly announce your live stream schedule. If someone has booked a spot, you wouldn't want to cancel, would you?

07 The value of live streaming depends on what you're doing. Welcome to connect and chat about it!
