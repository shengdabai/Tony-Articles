Note 82

Everyone has their own Disney in their heart

001 Name It Right

The direct English translation of this book helps us better understand how to win friends and influence people, and how to stop worrying and start living. These two sentences are the core of the book.

002 Strong IP

Care for users and plant a seed in each user's heart. Disney's rich IP characters each have different experiences, and through them, users gain strength and find friends.

003 Fun Topics

Diverse and interesting content topics, such as film, entertainment, and merchandise, all influence users' growth to some extent, because praise and joy are everywhere.

004 Releasing Again

The same content is released by Disney at intervals. New children are always born, and brand value keeps growing, accompanying generations of users.

005 Live Textbook

Disney's rich content products vividly demonstrate various ways of social relationships. From each character, we can see what made them, and they also influence more users.

006 Buy, Buy, Buy

Continuous acquisitions are part of Disney's strategy. Value the other party, cooperate sincerely. A single company's power is limited, and achieving win-win cooperation is a long-term approach.

007 Risk Resistance

Over 90 years of development, Disney has had ups and downs. At any time, it starts by thinking about itself, remains positive and optimistic towards the outside world and users, and takes the initiative to take responsibility when problems arise.

008 Empathy

Understanding users and bringing them joy, countless classic content has resonated with many people. Disney deeply understands the importance of empathy, helping more people find themselves.

009 Smile Power

One of the core points of Disney's hiring is smiling at users. In fact, they are not trained to do so, but they genuinely enjoy their work, even when doing repetitive and simple tasks, their faces still shine with joy.

010 People First

Whether it's a company, organization, or school, wherever there are people, understanding human nature is the key.
