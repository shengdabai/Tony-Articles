Mr. Yu Minhong says: When dealing with urgent matters, slow down—this is 100 times more important than rushing.

Doing urgent matters slowly is a vital attitude toward life, capable of yielding better results across all areas. It helps us break free from the restless pace of modern life and allows us to accurately seize every opportunity.

In the self-media industry, many people chase popular topics or traffic blindly, neglecting quality, which often leads to low-quality content and poor reception.

Compared to chasing speed and fleeting pleasure, we should slow down, work diligently on each article or video, and continuously improve our professional skills and quality.

Take Eastern Selection as an example. Under the team's and his own belief, Yu Minhong firmly advanced his vision, eventually creating the seemingly impossible "cultural selling" IP.

A mindset of seeking quick success only leads us into short-term anxiety and confusion over missed opportunities. However, by adhering to the principle of "doing urgent matters slowly, and choosing the right path over speed," we can steadily enhance our abilities, deepen our creative depth, and elevate the distinctiveness and value of our work.

In the self-media industry, chasing traffic and trends is often a high-risk endeavor. However, by slowing down and focusing on consistent accumulation and refinement, we can gain a stronger competitive edge and better development opportunities.

The principle of "doing urgent matters slowly" holds even greater significance in the self-media industry. It truly helps us understand the essence of "seeking victory over speed," grasp its underlying value, and find better paths for self-improvement, achieving continuous self-realization.

Choosing the right path means simply walking it consistently. We just need to consistently invest in important matters.

Consistent investment improves content quality and engagement.

Consistent investment allows content creators to focus more on their work, continuously producing high-quality, high-value content and releasing it at fixed intervals. This ongoing creation and release helps users develop good reading habits and increases their engagement.

For example, Lei Jun's blog is a great case in point. His regularly published content is rich and highly appreciated by users.

Consistent investment enhances the spread of content.

Consistent investment helps content creators build a stable readership and attention, increasing the visibility and reach of their content. Regularly publishing also allows users to develop expectations and trust in your content, thereby boosting sharing and spread.

For instance, Xue ZhaoFeng's courses have achieved excellent spread through regular publication of effective course content and consistent promotion via WeChat official accounts.

Consistent investment brings more opportunities to content creators.

Consistent investment allows content creators to gain more exposure, promotion, and collaboration opportunities, thus enhancing their influence and credibility.

For example, Teacher Zhang Xuefeng has attracted a large number of readers and corporate attention by consistently publishing high-quality IT-related content. He also regularly promotes his work on social media, leading to numerous business collaborations and commercial opportunities.

Consistent investment helps content creators accumulate brand equity.

Consistent investment allows content creators to present themselves as persistent and reliable, gradually building brand equity. By publishing content with a distinct personal style or higher professional quality, creators can further attract attention and positive feedback, laying a solid foundation for future work.

For example, the political commentator Qian Mu's blog, which consistently publishes in-depth analysis and commentary on current events, has built up a high level of brand equity.

Consistent investment enhances the professionalism of content creators.

Consistent investment allows creators to maintain learning and deepen their research on the subject, improving their professional depth and expertise.

For example, Quantum Bit regularly interviews multiple IT industry leaders to share insights on building technical systems and career paths, including Zhou Yuan and Lü Yi. This targeted professional analysis gradually builds trust among fans, leading to sustained attention.

Consistent investment brings higher economic benefits.

Consistent investment allows content creators to attract new audiences and clients through the release of high-quality, high-value content, ultimately achieving greater economic returns.

For instance, creators can attract more business collaborations and advertising revenue by regularly publishing content across multiple fields such as education, industry, social media, and news, thereby increasing their creative capital.

The greatest advantage of consistent investment is its continuity and accumulation. Through continuous publication and long-term accumulation, more people will become aware of you and your ideas, and more people will recognize your value and capabilities. Accumulated content continues to create value over time, even if the initial release had little impact. Future improvements can still be leveraged, turning seemingly lifeless content into something renewed.

Take the American musician Trevor James, for example. Initially, his buyers only purchased low-quality printed sheet music, but by regularly releasing new sheet music, he encouraged buyers to purchase more sonatas through his website.

A simple mindset allows people to focus more on their goals, such as Pony Ma, who consistently worked to build Tencent into a social media giant.

Don't easily be distracted by others; your inner thoughts are the most valuable, as with Jack Ma, who remained committed to his e-commerce vision and eventually founded JD.com.

Being willing to take risks and try new things can lead to more opportunities for success, as seen with Mark Zuckerberg, who dedicated himself to developing Facebook and became one of the world's top IT entrepreneurs.

Don't give up on your goals because of mistakes; instead, learn from failure and use it as an opportunity to do better. For example, Jeff Bezos values Amazon's management but also learns from failures to continuously improve.

Patience is needed to achieve your goals, as seen with Yi Jianlian, who remained dedicated to basketball and eventually became a famous professional player.

Dare to imagine the future, as Elon Musk has focused on sustainable energy development and founded SpaceX and Tesla.

Enjoy the process and stay happy, as Li Ning always sticks to the slogan "Anything is possible," enjoying the success brought by sports and entrepreneurship.
