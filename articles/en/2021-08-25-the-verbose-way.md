Why can't you help but ramble?

For me, I ramble basically because I care about the other person. It sounds a bit like "for your own good."

Why does caring about someone lead me to ramble? It might be because I'm not sure, or I have concerns, or maybe I'm afraid I won't explain myself clearly...

It seems there are many reasons that make me ramble, but in the end, it's because I don't trust the other person.

I had a conversation with my younger brother about what he gained over the holiday. After some reflection, besides finishing homework and reading a few books, he didn't really do much else.

From my above statement, you can see I had a pre-set expectation in my mind. When he didn't meet my expectations, I started rambling, entering the state of being verbose.

I ignored his progress and growth, focusing only on whether he completed various skill learning, whether he broadened his horizons, whether he...

I made a big mistake, falling into greed. We expect to accomplish many things at once or achieve multiple goals in a short time. But when we calm down and think about it, it's unrealistic.

Instead of spending more time with my brother playing and exploring new areas, I gave him some suggestions. It looked like advice, but in reality, it was my expectations of him.

Our conversation lasted over an hour, no, actually, it was me talking the whole time, occasionally asking a question because I felt his replies were too slow...

It's like a student being asked a question by a teacher. Before even knowing what the question is, they're already nervous and anxious. I thought I wasn't scary at all, I wasn't a teacher, so why not just say what I thought?

My brother has his own characteristics. I ignored his feelings, thinking he was timid and cowardly. Thinking about it now, it's really not right. Calm down, take a deep breath, wait a moment.

Rambling made me say a lot of things, both what I should say and what I shouldn't. Actually, I should have left the scene, taken a deep breath, and calmed down.

Never go back, because you'll fall into rambling again.

I originally wanted to write this article to explore the content of my conversation with my brother. But after thinking about it, I've actually forgotten the specific details. I was just rambling to myself.

What's the most terrifying thing about rambling? It turns into complaining. Don't go to the grassland, it's useless, and it only leads you into a vicious cycle.

Do what you should do, be there for them well. Rambling, hold back. Complaints, don't let them linger!

Good night, rest early, be quiet...
