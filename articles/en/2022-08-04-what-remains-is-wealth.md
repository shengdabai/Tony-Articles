How to Create and Accumulate Wealth #In Plain Language#

01 Li Xiaolai has shared three points that help us create and accumulate wealth: production, sales, and networks.

02 Production means products. On the Video platform, we can be producers, creating physical products, courses, or communities. Doing good content, doing good products, having inventory—this is important.

03 Sales means the market. There's a lot of content to sell on Video. Sell what you have, or sell others' if you don't have your own. Sell high-quality products. For example, I made a pure profit of five figures by selling Li Xiaolai's courses.

04 Networks mean value. If you value networks, you'll find that a large portion of wealth comes from the internet.

05 One representative of networks is community economy. Why are online communities so appealing? What value does a network formed by connecting people hold? For example, Fan Deng's Book Club has developed in part due to its community growth.

06 Video naturally helps us build a network. This network can be as large as the entire WeChat internet or as small as your local offline circle. Use the network built by Video well—would wealth be less?

07 Wealth creation and accumulation basically come from these three aspects, especially the third: networks. By using the network built by Video, we can create more value and bring greater returns. Keep going!
