# My Top Ten Practice List

In September, I joined the "PiaoHan Wealth Action Camp" run by PiaoHan YiZiMao, connected with fellow participants, and embarked on a magical journey. After reflecting on this period of learning, I've summarized the following practice list for the future!

001 Share Nonstop Every Day

Keep sharing, and you'll reap a fortune. Share a little value every day—friends, communities, videos, and writing are all my platforms for sharing. Accumulate momentum and wait for the flowers to bloom!

002 Keep Practicing Through Reading

Reading is a daily essential. Combine it with the practice list, find valuable points, and list specific steps for practice. Learn, and then apply it.

003 Be a Friend to Time

Time is fair to everyone. It can't be managed, but what we can do is focus our attention and do what needs to be done. Be a friend to time.

004 Value High-Quality Social Connections

Categorize my social network, focus on key people, actively connect, and maintain relationships with care. Seize every opportunity to exchange with experts.

005 Keep Praise in Mind

Frequently praise those around me. Be sincere in your praise and discover their strengths. If I can't find a point to praise, it's definitely because I haven't been genuine.

006 Helping Others Is Helping Oneself

Do more things that benefit others. Helping others is helping yourself. When others succeed, I will too. But don't meddle in others' affairs or act like a know-it-all.

007 Let Your Work Speak

Always do your best work. How to build trust with others? Your work is the best medium.

008 Needs Are the Best Teacher

Find real needs, solve real problems, and wealth will naturally come. Never isolate yourself and assume you know everything.

009 Be an Extreme Practitioner

Do it first, then talk about it. Use results and examples to explain. Do it with all your might, and silently tell yourself, "I'm doing something big."

010 Keep Growing

Improving yourself is the solution to everything. Keep growing, keep growing, keep striving to grow.

My Golden Ten, posted on my desk, reminding me constantly. Let's go, I am a hero in the PiaoHan world!
