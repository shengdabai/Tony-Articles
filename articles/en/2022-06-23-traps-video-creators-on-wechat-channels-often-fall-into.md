Note 74

Traps Video Creators on WeChat Channels Often Fall Into #Plain Language #Secrets #Case Sharing#

01 The trap of knowledge, the curse of knowledge—how you call it is up to you. What it actually means is that the more someone knows, the harder it is for them to develop empathy.

02 For example, in a place where English letters dominate, do you know what 3F means? Third floor? Third level? From which level does it start? And which is the basement, B1 or LG? It seems like it's something you should naturally know just by being in a mall.

03 In the process of spreading information, spoken language is easier to remember than written language. If you hear this ad once, you might not be able to visualize the words or the images in your mind, and you might even wonder if it's the right word to use. Is that really appropriate?

04 In the early days of WeChat Channels, some streamers would talk about various knowledge and tips on how to do WeChat Channels, speaking with great confidence, as if the users really understood. This attracted a group of people into WeChat Channels, but they eventually left.

05 Having empathy is important for creators. Don't get stuck in your own world, assuming users can understand, assuming the other person can see it, assuming—this is unreliable.

06 The techniques of expression, the creation and operation strategies of videos and live streams—these, in my opinion, are easy to learn. The hard part is how to get users into the environment you've created. You need to know that what you don't understand, users also don't understand, and what you do understand, users might not. Think from the perspective of your target audience: what do they want?

07 Keep refining your content, starting from the smallest details. Sometimes, changing just one word can make your content hit the soul directly.
