Note 101

Lessons from the Past Can Be Used Wisely #Live Streaming# #Short Videos#

01 Here, "lessons from the past" refers to a positive meaning—following the paths that others have successfully walked, which is a way to achieve rapid growth.

02 I've consulted with some users who always want to be original, to take a different route, believing that because they were successful before, they will also succeed with WeChat Channels. But then, what did those who entered the internet industry early do?

03 Don't jump to conclusions, nor look down on others' approaches. Start from the basics. During this process, mistakes will happen, and there will be costs—these are all part of the accumulation.

04 For example, a mother who runs a parenting product account through live streaming has made several thousand yuan a month. Perhaps you think it's not much, but the key is that if you can clearly understand this process and the business thinking behind it, you can do it and do it bigger.

05 If others have already succeeded with a certain model, it means it's doable. Often, it's the unknown that stops us from taking action. Now that someone has already tested it, why shouldn't we follow?

06 You can follow along, or even build a matrix—multiple accounts, operated by multiple people, which naturally expands the scale and returns.

07 Thinking is not enough. Please take real action, do the review and summary, and if you can, do it.
