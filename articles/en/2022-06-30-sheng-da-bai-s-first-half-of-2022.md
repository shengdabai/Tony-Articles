Summary of Tony Sheng's First Half of 2022

[**100 Tips for WeChat Channels**](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzkxMTIxMzM1NQ==&mid=2247488342&idx=2&sn=a1c019f3a26a814f9779f5d2cb4bcee0&chksm=c11ec512f6694c046445b11fa2b2fcd9dbf43383cf478d42b1335368a38fcf4376830ab31ab9&scene=21#wechat_redirect)

7 Keywords

Today is June 30, 2022, the last day of the first half of the year. Over the past six months, I have six keywords I want to share with you, each representing my experiences and growth in each month. The last keyword is a summary of the first half of the year.

001 Build the Foundation

A solid foundation is the basis for everything. I have conducted over 200 professional consultations daily, covering topics related to WeChat Channels and owned audiences. I write daily, update my WeChat Official Account regularly, continuously output content on Knowledge Planet, and post at least one update on my Moments every day. I also record my perceptions anytime and anywhere. I read marketing and biographical books, combine them with classic works from both domestic and international sources, and focus on case studies. Professional knowledge is just the baseline; my goal is to stand out.

002 Regular Assessments

To me, exams are the best way to test myself. More importantly, they provide systematic and objective feedback. The theoretical exam for the International Trainer Program, practical training assessments, and paper evaluations for educational leadership have further strengthened my training abilities. Plus, the feedback and suggestions from my mentors have given me a clearer direction for my efforts.

003 Cultivate Mental Strength

Mental state is crucial. Over the past six months, I've reduced my carbohydrate intake and made comprehensive dietary adjustments. I've burned nearly 500 calories daily through exercise, and my sleep quality has improved. In less than two months, I've lost nearly 10 kilograms and am still steadily losing weight.

004 Good Friends

I have a few small circles for reading, exercise, and life, where I share and exchange ideas. I've met several friends in person, which has given me a lot of energy. Online, my virtual coffee sessions have also yielded many insights. Thanks to the support and encouragement from my good friends, my anxiety has eased quite a bit. I especially want to thank one friend for their support—thank you, Little Prince, you're with us.

005 Project Work

From operating communities to managing WeChat Channels, Knowledge Planet, and community product launches, I've gained more hands-on experience in managing projects and owned audience marketing. The total sales of the projects exceeded 10 million yuan, achieving milestone events for several well-known IPs, and I've iterated better with my team through practical experience.

006 Eliminate Distractions

The strategy of elimination has made me more aware of the importance of goals. Within limited time, I focus on what truly matters. Although I sometimes hesitate and doubt, sacrificing some opportunity costs has actually been more valuable for my growth. I direct my attention to the right people and the right things. Eliminating distractions and focusing on myself.

007 Improvement

In 2022, I've experienced a lot—job changes, project bottlenecks, pandemic isolation, emotional fluctuations, and I've felt down and confused at times. I've also had many doubts about my choices. Fortunately, I've been continuously accumulating, and good things have been increasing. In a complex era, it's hard to know what is certain. In a time when everyone is striving, it doesn't seem easy to live well. Improvement is my choice. Continuous growth and constant improvement, we are full of hope for the future, moving forward step by step, becoming better one by one. Let's keep up the good work in the second half of 2022!

I've finished writing. Thank you for your likes and support!

END
