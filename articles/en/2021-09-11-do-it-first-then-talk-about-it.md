# Do It First, Then Talk About It

150th Original Piece

Using a PPT to organize my thoughts makes the structure clearer. My understanding is that tools and forms are all meant to serve the content. Whether it's mind maps, PPTs, Word documents, or images, we should express our thoughts in the way that feels most natural to us and most conducive to thinking. The best way to present our thoughts is in our own unique way!

The distance between knowing and doing is something that's quite heart-wrenching to talk about, yet it's something that truly happens in our lives. There's a saying I've heard countless times: "I understand the principles, yet I still can't live a good life!" This piece makes me think: do we really understand these principles? Are they ones we can grasp just by reading them, or by hearing them, or by being able to recite them?

My thought is that in most cases, we haven't even thought it through properly—we just repeat the phrase. Have we really thought about it seriously? And after thinking, have we done anything about it?

When reading, it's normal to feel confused about the content or have unclear thoughts. We need to read repeatedly, reading word for word, and thinking as we go. This is critical thinking and critical reading, more accurately speaking, independent thinking—the result of personal understanding and logical analysis.

During the reading process, we should reflect, combining our own experiences and values to think critically. Reading alone isn't enough; we need to read with reflection and logical reasoning. After reading, we should write, which is critical writing—expressing our understanding and analysis through thought.

Although we don't need academic critical thinking in most cases, the training of logic is fundamental and core to this kind of thinking!

Once we truly understand and clarify things, we should then take it seriously and work hard at it, repeatedly and earnestly, practicing what we believe is right. The most important thing is our values, and we should take them seriously. So this path is long, and there's a huge gap between knowing and doing. What do we do? We have role models, we have direction, and we have fellow travelers! Good night 😊
