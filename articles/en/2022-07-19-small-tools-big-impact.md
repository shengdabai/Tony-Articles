Note 94

Small Tools, Big Impact #Tools#

01 Pay attention to your phone's built-in draft box, notes, or other record-keeping tools and use them whenever possible—they can surprise you in unexpected ways.

02 For example, I often record observations about video channel updates, cases, interesting points, and all sorts of information that catches my attention.

03 Surprisingly, these pieces of information might have started off as mere data, but when combined with your goals, new value can be discovered. That's how I write most of my articles on the Knowledge Planet.

04 As a video channel creator, being observant, integrating information, and staying focused on your goals are essential. Let information serve you and create greater value.

05 For instance, can you analyze the phenomena you observe during a live stream? Which of them can help you, and how can you apply them to bring you what you need? A few of my clients have managed to sell their products better this way.

06 Don't be too subjective in your judgment. What you observe has a reason for existing. If you reject it from the start, you might miss out on new opportunities.

07 Use tools that suit you and value small accumulations and continuous improvements.

I've finished writing. Thank you for your support and likes!
