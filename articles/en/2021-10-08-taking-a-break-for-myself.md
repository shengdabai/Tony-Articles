# Taking a Break for Myself

> Published: 2021-10-08 · [中文](../zh/2021-10-08-%E7%BB%99%E8%87%AA%E5%B7%B1%E6%94%BE%E4%BA%86%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E5%81%87.md) | [English](../en/2021-10-08-taking-a-break-for-myself.md)

---

It's been a while since my last post, but writing hasn't stopped—I've been keeping a daily record, though sharing on the public account needs more time to mature.

In late September, I attended a three-day academic conference, and then took a full week off for the National Day holiday. These two weeks flew by really fast.

I've always thought I had no regrets, living seriously every day. I used to believe it was because I didn't leave myself any room, pushing myself forward. Now I realize, I was just looking at my future, always moving forward. If you ask me, what does my future look like?

I might not be able to give you a specific answer. What I can say is, everything is wonderful!

In recent days, I've thought a lot and felt a bit anxious. I've noticed more and more outstanding people in my community achieving amazing results. I've been wondering about myself—there's so much I still need to improve.

But it also shows that my circle is getting better. After all, the people I used to know were already great, and now the surrounding environment is full of brilliant people, outstanding individuals, and unique characters—everywhere you look, there's someone impressive.

I'm happy to see my social circle evolving. Still, I'm not satisfied with my own growth, so I've started to think deeply about it.

I know my abilities can be better, and I believe I can achieve it. But right now, I can't do it, and the reason is still my own laziness.

Why am I lazy? Maybe I feel busy and need a break, or perhaps I want to avoid challenges. That's not my usual style at all!

I've taken a break for myself, to figure out what I should do. Honestly, I don't have a clear answer, but I know I need to let go. I have to cancel some things and focus on my clear goals.

Friends who know me well know that I do a daily three-item review. But as I write, it's not just three things anymore—that's my "greed."

Letting go is necessary, and especially important for me right now. There are countless things to read, many courses to learn, and plenty of networking with brilliant people. There's so much I want to do, but I don't have enough energy.

I'm a graduate student, and my first priority is my studies. I'm committed to finishing both master's programs thoroughly, more accurately, by devoting enough energy to research.

I'm also a creator, and video and writing are my daily routines. I refine them repeatedly and express myself with care. Creating is my entrepreneurial opportunity as an ordinary person, and I enjoy it deeply.

I'm a lifelong learner, doing what I should do, practicing my five core values: reading, exercise, investing, spending time with family, and helping friends.

Three identities, multiple roles, knowing my path clearly, and moving forward with thought.

Take a break for myself, put down the burdens on my shoulders, travel light, and explore the world!

I'm your growth companion. Feel free to reach out. I'm great, and so are you. Good night, and sweet dreams!
