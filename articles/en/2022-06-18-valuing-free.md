Note 69

Valuing Free #Short Videos #Live Streaming #Little Secrets #Plain Talk#

01 You didn't see that wrong. You need to value free, because free represents your level!

02 The cost of spreading information today is almost zero. Content is the key element, yet most people think free means just doing it casually.

03 Study short videos and live streams. Big names share their best insights without holding back, and this is free. Then, what is expensive?

04 What's expensive is your attention and your ability to act.

05 For example, Teacher Cai Zhizhong's first live stream on Douyin openly shared his thoughts and years of creative insights. How much would you need to pay for that? The issue is, you listened, understood, thought, and acted…

06 Another example is advertising slogans. Teacher Hua Shan created many well-known slogans, like ["Love Cleanliness, Stay at HanTing"], six characters, four words, worth millions. You saw it, but would you use it?

07 Share the best things for free. Believe me, it will definitely attract people willing to pay for you.
