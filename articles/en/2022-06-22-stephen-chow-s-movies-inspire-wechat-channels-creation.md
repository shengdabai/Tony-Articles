Note 73

Reflections on WeChat Channels from Stephen Chow's Movies #Short Videos #Plain Language#

01 In your work, I saw my own reflection.

02 Stephen Chow is an actor I deeply admire. His movies have created many classic characters, each one unforgettable to me. More importantly, in his films, I saw myself.

03 It's crucial to make users relate to you. We may not be able to create classic movies, but can we make users see themselves in our content?

04 For example, I like a video channel called "Xiao Yuan Came Oh," where Xiao Yuan documents real stories from rural areas of Anhui. He visits elderly households under the pretense of eating free meals. You can search for it yourself.

05 It moved me deeply, reminding me of the time I spent with my grandparents. It evokes a sense of resonance with users, blending the story with their own experiences.

06 Great content must come from life. If it can move you, it can also move a group of people. Use authenticity to record life.

07 Love yourself, create with sincerity. Quality content is always a rare commodity—you can definitely succeed!

I'm done. Thank you for your likes and support if you're able to make things happen!
