01 There are many surprises waiting for you in the WeChat Channels Creator Center: Video Activities (find an activity that suits you, making it easier to be seen), Creator Classroom (official learning videos, you can gain something from them at any stage), Creator Traffic Package (traffic rewards and traffic purchase, do a good job promoting your videos and live streams), Help and Feedback (Creator Incentives).

02 There are two types of Creator Incentives: the Incentive Program and the Rights Guide. In summary, having traffic and income are the 2022 goals. It's recommended to choose the Shopping Channel for live streams, and participate in official activities more for short videos. The Rights Guide provides different permissions for creators at different stages, corresponding to different numbers of followers.

03 You can still earn income without followers. We should focus more on monetizable traffic rather than being overly concerned with the number of followers. Of course, having more followers means you get more permissions.

04 In the Creator Center, there is an area called Growth Incentive. Here you can find the New Creator Program, Recruitment Program, and Live Stream Tasks. Participate in what you can, complete tasks on time, and you will receive more income rewards. Two indicators: live stream for 40 hours a month, and post 20 videos a month.

05 The more you participate, the more you gain. When you bring greater value to the platform, the platform will take better care of you. This value is reflected in bringing more money to the platform and investing more time on it.

06 Tap more, look more, it doesn't take much time. Read through these information, and it will definitely help you.

07 Be a thoughtful person, you'll never lose out.
