# Who Took Tony's Cheese?

" What would you do if you had no fear?" This is my favorite line from *Who Moved My Cheese?* Because it's precisely this fear that has led our lives into such or such a maze.

Although this book tells a short fable, it vividly explains the truth of life: "Change is the only constant." It also deepened my understanding and insights into life.

The four main characters in the book — the mice Sniff and Scurry, and the little people Hem and Jee — live in a maze, constantly running around searching for cheese. Although their visions of cheese differ, they share the same belief: to persistently pursue their goals. However, the complex structure of the maze posed significant obstacles to their actions, with getting lost and bumping into walls being routine. But for the cheese they longed for, they kept on chasing, just to obtain the most desired thing in their lives.

This reminds me of our real lives. The "maze" is like today's complicated society, and we are like the little characters running around in it, chasing our original dreams, just to obtain the most desired food — cheese. But life never runs smoothly; setbacks, obstacles, sadness, and disappointment are inevitable. But when our hearts are filled with longing, what are these things really? So, we keep falling, getting up, falling again, getting up again, but always moving forward.

However, in many cases, especially after experiencing the joy of success, when we face life's tests, we often lack the courage to change. We fear change, because it brings us the unknown. When this fear of the unknown takes over our minds, our steps of progress will stop.

Just like the little characters in our story, their persistent pursuit eventually paid off on a sunny day. Yes, they found their dream land — Cheese C Station. Mountains of various cheeses piled up, shining with an alluring light. They joyfully rushed to the cheese pile, fully enjoying the deliciousness, immersed in the great satisfaction and joy of success. They cheered and celebrated this momentous occasion. So, for a long time, they went to Cheese C Station every day to enjoy the delicious cheese, while dreaming of a more prosperous and fulfilling life. Unknowingly, when they took their success for granted, the test of life had just begun.

Faced with success, the two mice and two little people had completely different reactions. Hem and Jee, while enjoying their success, saw their confidence swell to an unprecedented level, becoming arrogant. In this comfortable life, they never noticed the changes happening around them. However, Sniff and Scurry did not let the temporary success cloud their vision. They kept observing Cheese C Station every day, so they were fully aware of the changes there.

One day, Cheese C Station had no cheese left. Sniff and Scurry, having anticipated this, were not surprised, but instead made the decision immediately to look for new cheese elsewhere. When Hem and Jee arrived later and discovered this change, they were stunned. After a brief silence, they erupted in anger, shouting accusations at the sudden change. It was precisely because they had looked forward to Cheese C Station with great anticipation that when all their hopes turned into illusions, the source of happiness shattered. The painful reality hit them hard, and they couldn't understand what had happened. From then on, they were trapped in the web they had woven themselves, living aimlessly in a life no longer filled with hope.

It's clear that when life's tests came, the two little people chose to avoid the pain rather than face it. So why, when facing the same test, did the two mice quickly find new paths, while the two little people remained trapped in pain for a long time? The answer is simple: the two mice never saw this as their permanent reliance. They knew that after a brief rest, a new journey would begin. However, the two little people were clearly different from the mice. They had placed too much hope on Cheese C Station, even seeing it as the only way to achieve happiness. Therefore, when all the beauty turned into nothing, they couldn't accept it, and they kept asking meaningless questions about the current situation, seeking comfort for their minds.

Often, the more we value something, the more we want to hold on to it. When it disappears one day, the pain is greater. Therefore, we need to understand how to balance the weight in our hearts, knowing what is more important. This way, we won't fall into the depths of pain and be unable to let go. Once we lose the correct measure, we become irrational, starting to deceive ourselves, telling ourselves maybe... maybe... But where do all these "maybes" come from? Repeating the same meaningless question, even if we find an answer, what difference does it make? It still won't change the current situation.

"Being too attached" makes us lose the courage and determination to change. We fear change because it may bring another disaster. Fear suppresses our ability to overcome ourselves, making happiness seem out of reach. Often, we are the ones who defeat ourselves. Life's pressure forces us to make changes, but when we decide to act, we are deeply shadowed by the fear of failure. Again, we can't see the path ahead.

Long-term suppression and countless inner struggles made Jee incredibly painful. He wanted to start over many times, seeking new hope, but he kept giving up because of the fear of failure. However, being in a completely passive state made Jee tired of waiting, and he gradually realized some things. Finally, he faced failure and himself. He finally understood that once something changes, it can never return to what it was. Life changes, days move forward, and we should change with them, not stay in place.

So, Jee began his new journey. Every time he wanted to retreat, he would ask himself repeatedly, "What would you do if you had no fear?" to motivate himself to move forward bravely. At the same time, he realized that things weren't as scary as imagined when you're in the middle of them.

"Without change, you will be eliminated." Isn't this also the truth of life? The times are changing, and we, living in this rapidly developing society, need to find our place and create new hope for ourselves. Change may not bring the expected results, but staying the same will surely be eliminated by society. Progress requires fresh blood, not rigid adherence to old ways. We are still young, and young lives should never fear failure. Without trying, how can we know if we will succeed?

In the process of chasing dreams, we inevitably doubt ourselves, constantly asking ourselves, "Is what I'm doing worth it? If I really lose everything because of this, how will I face it? Can I really do it?" Yes, it's precisely because we fear failure that we doubt ourselves repeatedly and hesitate. When we fall into such a situation, we need the spirit of fearlessness. Without fear, we can move forward confidently and boldly chase our inner paradise. Often, when we miss out on opportunities, looking back, we should ask ourselves, "What would I be like today if I had been fearless back then?" Instead of regretting afterwards, we should just go for it from the start. Success will be much closer to us.

Jee was able to continue his journey and eventually reach his dream place — Cheese N Station, because of this belief. At this moment, Jee had learned how to face both success and failure in life. He enjoyed the joy while keeping a calm mind, calmly facing this significant success in his life.

Jee also taught us through his experience that we must be good at noticing the subtle changes that occur at the beginning, so that we can be prepared for the bigger changes that are coming. At the same time, we should know that some fears need to be taken seriously, as they can help us avoid real dangers. However, most fears are unwise, and they only make us avoid change when it's needed.

We should also understand that life's tests are not as simple as we imagine. We must always be ready for new changes. Even if we can't perfectly solve the difficulties we face, at least we can face life's disappointments with a fearless heart. In that case, even if we fail, it's still worth celebrating because we've surpassed ourselves and overcome ourselves!

Remember, when you feel powerless, don't forget to ask yourself:

"What would you do if you had no fear?"
