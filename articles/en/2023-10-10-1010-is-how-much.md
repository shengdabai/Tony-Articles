At the birth of computers, people discovered two magical digits: 0 and 1. They are so simple, yet so powerful.

They can represent binary "on" or "off," or decimal "zero" or "one." They are the most fundamental elements in the world, the foundation of all complex systems.

However, these two digits weren't understood by humans at first. Their existence was once seen as mysterious magic, misunderstood and misused. But through the wisdom and relentless efforts of generations of scientists, their secrets gradually came to light.

0 represents emptiness and infinite possibilities. It symbolizes both the beginning and the end. It is the symbol of nothingness, yet also the origin of everything. The existence of 0 allows the world to be full of change and innovation.

(According to historical records, 0 was invented by ancient Indians. In the 3rd century BCE, Indians had developed numerals 1 through 9, but there was no numeral for 0. They used empty spaces to represent it. Over a thousand years later, during the Gupta Dynasty in India, the concept of 0 emerged, represented by a dot "▪". In 1881, an ancient mathematical manuscript called the Bakhshali Manuscript was discovered in the village of Bakhshali, India. This precious manuscript shows a relatively complete decimal system, with 0 represented by the dot "▪".)

1 represents existence and certainty. It is the symbol of the tangible and the mark of truth. The existence of 1 brings certainty and stability to the world.

Though 0 and 1 seem entirely different, they are deeply connected. They are an inseparable pair, eternal companions. Without 0, there would be no 1; without 1, there would be no value in 0. They are interdependent and influence each other.

In the world of numbers, the story of 0 and 1 continues. They will continue to guide humanity in exploring the unknown and creating the future, becoming essential tools for understanding and changing the world.

In the world of computers, 0 and 1 are the most basic elements, representing the two states of information: on (1) or off (0). It is precisely the combination of these two digits that has created the digital world we encounter everywhere in our lives. Just like human language, text, music, and images are all expressed through the combination of 0 and 1.

In our digital age, computers have become indispensable companions in our lives. They play many different roles, from business to home entertainment.

However, computers are built from the most basic elements—0 and 1. This binary language stores all the data of a computer. Compared to the decimal system we humans use, binary is a more efficient way of storing and processing information, and it is also more direct and simple.

0 and 1 mean on and off. These two digits are used in almost all computer systems and applications. They are considered the most basic and smallest units in the field of computing, known as bits. They can be combined with other bits to form more complex data types, such as integers, characters, and images.
