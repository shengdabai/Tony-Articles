I Love Yemian

001 Peak Experience in 16 Characters

Once you see it, you enter; once you enter, you buy; once you buy, you buy again; once you buy, it spreads like wildfire.

002 Brand Power Connects the World

Xibei Yemiancun's brand slogan is inspired by "I Love New York," which incorporates international elements and activates collective unconsciousness, instantly creating resonance with users.

003 Mass Communication Without Fear of Style

The Northwest style, minimalist layout, red and white checkered fabric, and large red square logo create a series of visual impacts that make users want to enter the store. This style and pursuit of uniqueness make it hard to mass promote, and inevitably, some people will dislike it.

004 Carefully Selected Dishes with Quality Assurance

Close your eyes and order—every dish is delicious. This level of quality makes it hard for users to resist. The restaurant selects signature dishes to ensure flavor and quality, making every meal a peak experience.

005 Kissing Discounts Spread by Word of Mouth

Valentine's Day discounts with a "kissing" theme, year after year, not only attract new customers but also keep old ones loyal. Consistently doing one thing leads to continuous growth in user conversion.

006 The Hourglass Promise

Many restaurants use an hourglass countdown, but they only serve their signature dishes. Xibei ensures that all dishes are served within the set time, with a standardized process and precise control, naturally increasing user return rates.

007 Marketing Calendar with Universal Approval

Xibei regularly schedules activities, unlike some restaurants that constantly introduce new things. Xibei sticks to the same marketing calendar, doing what it does at each stage. The brand grows within the cultural context, making it easier for users to recommend and return.

008 What Others Don't Have, I Have; What Others Have, I Don't

You don't necessarily need to be better than others. For example, compared to Hot Pot King's service, Xibei doesn't emphasize service. The peak experience comes from one point that deeply resonates with users. At Xibei, you can always find your own special moment.

009 Small Activities with Big Fun

Waiters will actively suggest playing "Rock, Paper, Scissors" with you. If you win a round, you get a 5 yuan discount. If you win ten rounds in a row, you get 50 yuan off. A simple activity can still create a peak experience for users.

010 Online Momentum to Capture the Mind

Leverage new media to capture the young consumer group, do brand promotion well, gather resources from first-tier cities, and consolidate small KOLs, keeping up with the trend.
