The 100th Note

 _**Have you thought about how to make money through WeChat Channels?**_

01 This title is too direct—it's making us think seriously about what path we should take to make money on WeChat Channels? Or are you aiming for business, or just doing WeChat Channels for the sake of it?

02 There's one term that easily confuses people—traffic monetization? Traffic is traffic, monetization is monetization; there's no inherent connection between them. Essentially, what we want is traffic that can be monetized.

03 Possible three approaches: One is to attract traffic on WeChat Channels and then connect it to business, converting traffic into revenue. Two is to use other traffic within the WeChat ecosystem, building up owned audience traffic to form a closed-loop commercial system within WeChat. Three is to use external platforms to convert traffic into WeChat Channels and then commercialize it.

04 I've encountered many users who started with WeChat Channels without clearly thinking through their business path. They're likely just going to be frustrated, because they've missed the point entirely.

05 There are many big accounts on WeChat Channels that can't monetize, or users just don't buy their products. How can they sustain themselves in the long run?

06 Traffic and people are different. We need to carefully analyze our target audience. Often, we treat traffic as simple data, not understanding user needs, which is basically self-delusion.

07 The earlier you clarify your thoughts, the easier it is to start monetizing WeChat Channels. WeChat Channels is unlikely to have very large accounts, but that's our opportunity as ordinary people. Make the most of it.

I've finished writing. Thank you for your support if you're someone who can make things happen!
