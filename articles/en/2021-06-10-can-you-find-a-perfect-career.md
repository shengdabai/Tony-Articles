A perfect career has three components—passion, strength, and value; I love doing it, I can do it, there's demand, and the return is decent. If a job can't satisfy all three, designing a diversified career strategy is also a good approach. There's a concept called the "Three Rings Theory of Career," which suggests the most perfect career lies at the intersection of competence, opportunity, and passion. It's the same idea as what Classical Teacher shared. Although we may be competent, have relevant skills and experience, and even identified industry opportunities, the most important thing is passion. A scholar interviewed dozens of entrepreneurs, and they all agreed that passion is the most important. I deeply agree. When we have enough passion, what others see as hard work becomes natural for us.

I think I've found the perfect industry for me—education. I love education, and helping every individual grow is my honor. The education industry takes time to show its value, and nurturing people is a huge project. Education has always played an important role in human societal development, regardless of the form or method, there are many areas in education that need deep exploration. Am I competent? I can only say I have some experience. I'll keep working hard and take it step by step. As for what I can do in the education industry, I don't mind. Using my personal influence to affect a group of people, and then that group affecting another group—let's go! 😊
