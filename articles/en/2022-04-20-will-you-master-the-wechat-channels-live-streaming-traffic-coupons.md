Note 22

01 At the WeChat Public Class in January this year, the WeChat Channels team launched the Merchant Incentive Program, stating that **"for every owned audience user a merchant guides into the live stream, the platform will at least incentivize one public domain user to support the merchant."** From April 12 to December 31, 2022, **WeChat Channels live streams that choose the "shopping" category and have a shopping cart will automatically participate in the Merchant Incentive Program. If you want to receive traffic coupons, the number of non-WeChat Channels imported viewers in a single live stream must be at least 50.** After meeting these requirements, WeChat Channels merchants can receive traffic coupons valid for seven days after the live stream ends.

02 Besides the traffic from the WeChat Channels Live Streaming Plaza or algorithm recommendations, it's basically all your owned audience traffic, which falls under the reward category. So what you need to do is spread it as much as possible through all available channels. Here's a note: viewers in the live stream who help share and promote the stream are also indirect recommendations and can count toward the traffic reward.

03 After the live stream, you'll receive a reminder from the WeChat Channels team indicating how much owned audience traffic was imported, and you'll be given traffic coupons as a reward. One point of traffic coupon equals two UVs, where UV refers to the number of users, not the number of visits.

04 There are two ways to use the traffic coupons: system intelligent recommendation or custom targeted recommendation. In simple terms, this is about being broad or precise. You need to consider the effectiveness—large traffic will be faster, while precise traffic will take more time. The final results will require more testing.

05 Compared to other platforms, the current traffic coupons are still a relatively simple version, and some functions are not yet complete. At present, the focus is on how to get more traffic coupons and obtain official traffic rewards. This might be the top priority right now.

06 The traffic coupons for short videos are also in an internal test phase. In the future, traffic coupons may also be purchasable. Commercial operations are definitely part of the platform's future plans. For us, finding a better business model and making good use of the WeChat Channels tool will make its value even greater.

07 Private domain driving public domain is becoming increasingly evident. Encouraging live-streaming e-commerce, the future of live streaming will definitely need to bring commercial value. Live streaming has already changed the traditional shopping model. Before the next disruptive product comes out, how can live streaming help our business development is a topic worth thinking about and researching.
