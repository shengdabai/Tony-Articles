**Two Lives Later**

In the east of England, there is a county called Norfolk, and within it, a small town called Congham. Though the town is small in population, it is quite interesting because it hosts an international race every year. What makes this race unique is that the competitors are not humans, but snails—animals considered by humans to be among the slowest. The race has been held since 1960 and continues to this day.

How do sticky snails race? The organizers place the snails at the center of a circle with a diameter of 33 centimeters, allowing them to face any direction. As long as a snail crawls out of the circle, it is considered to have "completed the race," and the first snail to escape the circle is declared the champion. In 1995, a snail named Archie set a record of 2 minutes to escape the circle—equivalent to a speed of 9.9 meters per hour, a record that has never been broken!
