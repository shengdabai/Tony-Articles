100 Tips for WeChat Channels

Over the past two years of getting involved with WeChat Channels, I've helped some businesses and individuals achieve performance growth through WeChat Channels, opening up new possibilities. 002 Everything is economic. As ordinary people, WeChat is our tool for making money.

003 WeChat provides you, the one who can achieve things, with a free yet long-term buildable and accumulative advertising screen — the Moments. Now, you have a much larger advertising screen — WeChat Channels.

004 WeChat Channels is your personal advertising company. Start your entrepreneurial journey with your creation.

005 Moments > print media, short videos > mini TV stations, live streaming > TV shopping programs. Now you have a media and advertising company.

006 WeChat Channels doesn't lack traffic; it lacks content — your content.

007 WeChat is the only platform among all those providing short video services that absolutely doesn't lack users.

008 For ordinary people, the last possible way to gain traffic and commercial value on the internet is WeChat Channels.

009 Your brand value is not to be underestimated. WeChat Channels helps amplify your value.

010 Marketing is to make sales surplus. For you, WeChat Channels is an excellent free marketing channel.

011 In the era of short videos, being good-looking may give you some advantage, but it may also bring users who are only interested in your looks. These users may not be valuable. The users you really need are those attracted by your content.

012 WeChat Channels has two legs to walk on. Start by taking one step — short videos or live streaming. Both can be done well, but it will take you a long time.

013 WeChat is a tool for familiar social interactions, but WeChat Channels helps you connect everything on WeChat.

014 Choice is more important than effort. Continuously operating WeChat Channels gives you a bigger chance of success from the start.

015 How long is long? Give yourself at least a year to cultivate WeChat Channels. Right now, it's still not too late.

016 Those who can last are the ones with the most traffic, the most income. They are growing together with the platform. WeChat Channels is still growing.

017 Since WeChat Channels launched over two years ago, some people have come, some have left. Few truly understand the value behind it. I suggest you check what the experts have chosen.

018 Don't challenge the platform. If you're here on WeChat Channels, please read the rules carefully and follow them. After all, you're just one in a billion registered users.

019 You don't need to worry. Obviously, most registered WeChat Channels users don't update or create content regularly, and many WeChat Channels accounts have only names and profile pictures.

020 There are plenty of business opportunities on the WeChat Channels platform, but one thing you don't need to consider is advertising, as it's the main source of income for the platform.

021 Accounts that can continuously bring value to WeChat Channels will receive more traffic. For example, if you have a lot of tips or high sales performance in live streaming, you'll get more attention.

022 Every action you take on WeChat Channels is part of the process of the platform's algorithm getting to know you. If you present yourself as a "model worker," the platform won't disappoint you.

023 You might hear the term "account weight." In simple terms, the WeChat Channels algorithm creates a model for each account, and a lot of data forms this model. The better the data, the higher the weight, and the more likely you'll get what you want.

024 Don't try to figure out the algorithm or ask about internal information. After all, the algorithm you understand will change, and a new one will appear. Essentially, the algorithm shapes the user.

025 Thinking from the end goal is the core mindset for WeChat Channels creators. Think clearly about what you ultimately want, and then work backward to figure out how to get there.

026 Focus on long-term value, and you won't get bogged down by short-term gains. This is the best time for us to grow together with WeChat Channels.

027 Three ways to monetize on WeChat Channels: tips, selling courses, and selling products. For most people, selling products is the most direct way. If you don't have your own product, selling others' high-quality products is a good path.

028 Only when something has commercial value can it last. Have you ever seen a viral video that didn't make money? Have you ever seen a live stream with hundreds of thousands of views that didn't bring in revenue? This is normal. Think clearly about how you can achieve sustainable commercial value on WeChat Channels. This is important.

029 Traffic is traffic, monetization is monetization. Don't equate them, because your traffic may not be able to be monetized. It's crucial to distinguish between them.

030 The traffic you should focus on is the one that can be monetized. Where does it come from? Start by understanding user needs. Initially, you can gather users who have paid for your content.

031 WeChat Channels can connect the entire WeChat ecosystem, such as personal WeChat, WeChat groups, Moments, official accounts, enterprise WeChat, and mini programs (small stores). I recommend the model of WeChat groups + WeChat Channels.

032 The value of a community economy lies in concentrated business. You can directly interact with users and use WeChat Channels to consolidate users into your community. This is what you should do.

033 Pay attention to the user profile on WeChat Channels. According to big data statistics, users are mostly between 30 and 50 years old. The content they are interested in is something creators must consider.

034 The best categories to do within the WeChat ecosystem are baby products, food, and cosmetics. You can choose the direction of WeChat Channels based on your strengths. If you don't have one, consider combining with baby products.

035 WeChat Channels can choose its own platform products and earn commissions. If you have a supply chain or can provide services or courses, you can redirect traffic through the enterprise mini store.

036 WeChat mini store is a type of mini program. It is automatically linked with WeChat Channels. However, now it requires enterprise qualifications, which is a step toward standardization. The operation is not difficult, and it's relatively convenient to use.

037 If you already have other mini programs or platforms, such as Xiaoe Tong, you can directly integrate them with WeChat Channels. Make sure to let the monetizable traffic enter the service entry point and consolidate users.

038 Always give your traffic a place to go, such as a live stream fan club, a live stream fan group, enterprise WeChat users, or personal WeChat friends.

040 Live streaming is placed separately on the Discover page, like a TV program. Whether you watch it or not, it's always there.
