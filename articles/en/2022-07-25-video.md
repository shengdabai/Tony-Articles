Recently, I've been asked the most questions about product promotion, and this piece aims to clarify the situation regarding product promotion.

01 WeChat Mini Shop and WeChat Channels (product promotion) are two different things. WeChat Mini Shop is developed based on the mini program, making it convenient for individuals and businesses to conduct transactions within WeChat. It has no management fees, but tax is mandatory since it is backed by WeChat Pay. WeChat Channels is a separate system, and while it can choose to use WeChat Mini Shop for product promotion, it can also use platforms like JD.com, Aichang, and Xiaoge Tong. Essentially, WeChat Channels can promote products because it is integrated with the mini program, and both the Mini Shop and various platforms conduct transactions through the mini program.

02 Clarify the first point, and the following diagram will be easier to understand. WeChat will phase out the WeChat Mini Shop, as product promotion will now go through WeChat Channels Shop. This change will be fully implemented on December 1st of this year. For reference, there's the Douyin Shop, which is the official store within the video ecosystem. This is a normal operation for WeChat Channels to enter e-commerce commercialization.

03 After this rule is implemented, it will affect two parts. On the merchant side, merchants must provide the required documents and open shops for product promotion according to the requirements of WeChat Channels Shop. On the personal side, there are two channels for product promotion: selecting products from the merchant side or the platform. Therefore, merchants will need more adjustments. Here, the merchants refer to those who can provide course or product information with a business license. For personal users, it's important to note that previously, it was also mentioned that only one merchant can be bound, and there are no restrictions on the products from the platform.

04 How to understand that personal users can only bind one shop? The platform offers a variety of product sources. Among them, there is a WeChat Channels Merchant Alliance. Once you join the alliance, there is no such restriction. For example, if Tony has a Mini Shop and wants everyone to help sell my planet, if you have already bound another shop, you cannot help promote it unless I join the alliance, meaning my planet becomes a platform product.

05 How do you think about such rules? In my opinion, it's good news. The platform is becoming more standardized, which is good for both merchants and users. It helps identify those who violate rules, making the WeChat Channels platform better and better, which is in the interest of those of us who plan to develop long-term here.

06 During the platform's development, I've also seen some projects that aim to make quick money, charging in various ways. This has led some users to criticize WeChat Channels. Cleaning up the platform's illegal content and products will help WeChat Channels grow healthily.

07 Follow the platform, and when there are rules, follow them closely. Adjust in time and don't complain. The reason is simple: if we want to stay here for a while, we should just be honest and follow the rules.

Let's work hard and make money honestly.
