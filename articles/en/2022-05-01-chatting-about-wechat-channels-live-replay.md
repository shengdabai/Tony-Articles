Note 29

How to Play with Live Replay?

01 To start playing with live replay, you need to be certified—whether it's enterprise certification or personal certification. It's necessary to screen the streamers. (Public and private streams are both acceptable.) 02 If you stream via your phone without joining a call, the backend of a certified WeChat Channels will automatically generate a replay, which you can download directly. All videos within 24 hours are available. 03 If you stream via your phone and your WeChat Channels is certified (whether or not you're joining a call), there will be a button on your homepage after the stream ends. Open this button, and your WeChat Channels homepage will show the replay. However, this replay cannot be downloaded, but you can record it separately. 04 Live replay solves a pain point: I didn't watch the whole thing, and I want to see what I missed. Now you can do that. 05 Looking back at all the live streams you've encountered so far, solving the replay issue has always been a major challenge for platforms. WeChat has truly achieved this. Now you can watch replays, which opens up more business opportunities, though it might also bring challenges. 06 For example, if you're reading a book, users want to see mind maps; if you're learning English, users want various resources. Now that live replay is available, you no longer have to worry about missing out on anything exciting. 07 This feature raises the standard for live content. Since what you say will be recorded and viewed by users, you need to seriously consider whether you're truly providing value. 08 Live streaming is about atmosphere. Users gain more value by following your rhythm. But when replays are available, is the reason for mandatory real-time participation still valid? Regardless, it's here now. You can try it out. Final tip: Consider paid live streams, so your replays will also be paid.
