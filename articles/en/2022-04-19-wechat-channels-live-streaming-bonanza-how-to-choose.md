Note 21

01 In 2021, WeChat Channels introduced an incentive policy to reward merchants with traffic. The participation conditions were two-fold: 1. Choose shopping live streams, 2. Bring in more than 50 people from non-WeChat Channels entry points.

02 Non-WeChat Channels entry points include sharing the live stream to one-on-one chats, group chats, Moments, reminders for live stream reservations, follow reminders for WeChat Channels, official accounts, mini programs, etc. Any traffic that doesn't come from the WeChat Channels Live Stream Square counts as non-WeChat Channels entry.

03 The platform will give you traffic based on the number of people guided into the live stream. For example, if 100 people come from your owned audience, the platform will give you another 100, making it 200 in total, and so on.

04 Why shopping live streams? Because they bring value and profit, which creates a more sustainable business relationship. From my perspective, live selling and live teaching both work. This is a model that benefits the platform, merchants, and users alike.

05 During the live streaming boom, there's traffic and money to be made. Are we ready for it? Do we need more people to see it, or more income? Sometimes we want both, and that requires accumulation.

06 Bonanza, trend,comeback... Words like these. For me, it's more important to understand why I'm doing it. If it's yours, it's bound to succeed.

07 Live streaming has given us new possibilities and brought new business models. So, have you thought through how to proceed? Feel free to share your thoughts.
