2022 Tony First Live Stream Review #LiveStream #CaseStudy #PlainLanguage

01 Maintain a consistent frequency and rhythm in your live streams, keep the exposure going, so the platform can see your effort. Every live stream data you generate helps the platform better understand who you are, what you have, and whether you deserve traffic...

02 Activate your owned audience, get your friends and family to watch your live stream. Although I didn't promote it, I just posted it on my Moments, and people started to like, comment, and support me. Then, the number of viewers went from under 100 to a huge number rapidly. The traffic is enough, the key is whether you can keep it.

03 Keep observing excellent live streams, learn and take notes. Don't always think it's a trick, or that the other person is trying to cut you off. In most cases, paying for content is the fastest way to grow. Impulsive spending is fine, as long as you learn how to use it for yourself.

04 How to better showcase yourself? Uncle Mao's article shares this with you. Show a constantly improving version of yourself, a very professional version, a principled version, and an energetic version. I thought about it, and I did some of these, which made more people interested in me.

05 Mutual success. You need people to support you. Thank the Transmission Zhuge team, Jin Teacher as the host, who gave me a great boost and opened up more possibilities for me. Mo Zhen Teacher gave me a lot of confidence. If you really don't know how to stream, stream with your good friend or someone with experience.

06 Clarify the value of live streaming for you. This live stream was my recent public appearance. The promotion wasn't thorough enough, but the content and presentation were okay. You might ask if I'll continue streaming. Probably not, because my priority is to serve more of my friends, like the ones who supported me with donations. Their support comes from our relationship, and it's my responsibility to do better for them.

07 Don't believe in overnight wealth, don't expect overnight fame. Most of us don't have the ability to rely on luck. It's better to work hard, make steady progress. Yes, be grounded.
