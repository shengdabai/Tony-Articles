We must learn to write.

Writing is more about communication than expression. It's similar to how an excellent teacher can anticipate a student's questions and answer them, which is the essence of communication.

Writing is not just an individual's expression; it's also a conversation with the reader. It involves understanding the reader's thoughts, answering their doubts, giving them energy, and instilling confidence.

Whether writing news reports, commentary, or creating novels and poetry, one must possess good writing skills. Only with excellent language can one capture the audience's attention and convey one's intent effectively.

The quality of writing directly affects the reader's experience and their ability to receive information.

A well-known **self-media person**, "Parallel Horizon," is a great example. Through beautiful and expressive writing, he conveyed his insights and thoughts, earning the recognition and support of a wide readership.

Writing skills also help us better define our content style and positioning. Only by mastering writing techniques can we more accurately express our thoughts and emotions.

Although writing skills require continuous learning and practice to improve, self-media people should not ignore the excellent works of other platforms and news organizations. By reading more and learning more, we can draw on others' excellent writing experiences and ideas, further enhancing our own writing abilities.

Writing skills are not just a technical issue; they are a reflection of thinking and expression. Mastering writing techniques allows us to more accurately express our views and let more people hear our voice.

Mr. Lu Xun once shared 12 words on writing:

**Have sincerity, avoid embellishment, be natural, and don’t show off.**

"Have sincerity" means that creation must emphasize authenticity and cultural depth, without neglecting deeper emotions and thoughts.

Content creators should have sincerity. They can do so by sharing personal experiences and reflections or by discussing trending topics and social phenomena, helping the audience better understand their perspectives and attitudes.

"Avoid embellishment" means writing should be genuine, direct, and clear. It should not chase for flashy forms but focus on conveying the essence of the content.

Authors can adopt this method, explore the essence of content, and use simple language and real examples to provide readers with valuable information.

"Be natural" means the writing style should be effortless and unadorned.

Creators should be natural, expressing their views with a relaxed and authentic style to attract readers.

"Don’t show off" means not overemphasizing oneself, letting the content itself be the focus.

By sharing personal experiences and thoughts, expressing inner feelings, but not overemphasizing oneself, the focus should be more on the content itself.

Lu Xun's writing method, refined over time, has been widely applied in self-media content creation. As a self-media author, having sincerity, avoiding embellishment, being natural, and not showing off are principles that not only make content more valuable and appealing but also give the self-media account a more personal charm, making it more appealing to the audience. Believing in these principles, creators can write content that is more profound and meaningful, deeply loved and guiding readers.

**Three Steps to Writing**

**Opinion - Example - Summary**

An opinion is the soul of self-media content creation, the foundation for expressing one's thoughts and conveying information.

An opinion should highlight its strengths to increase the audience's attention. For example, the self-media account "Marketing Master" shares some uncommon marketing strategies, which can attract more audience attention and increase the account's followers.

An example is the concrete embodiment of an opinion. It can support and prove the opinion, making it more persuasive.

Autoblog authors can use examples to help the audience better understand the opinion. For instance, the food self-media account "Foodie" pairs each step with a video and explanation, making it easier for the audience to learn.

A summary is a crucial step in the content creation process. It leaves a deep impression on the reader and effectively improves the quality of the content.

Autoblog authors should ensure that the summary is clear and concise. For example, the travel self-media account "Travel Daily News" summarizes the theme, itinerary, and highlights of the trip at the end, making it convenient for the audience to understand and share.

The three-step approach to writing is the key to successful self-media content creation. Clear, concise, and precise opinions; real, interesting, and practical examples; and clear, concise, and accurate summaries can all highlight the professional image of the self-media author and win the favor and trust of more audiences.

Only by accurately grasping the opinion, selecting the right example, and drawing a clear summary can one's content stand out among numerous self-media accounts. Believing that following this three-step process will help autoblog authors create more outstanding and high-quality content, achieving better responses and accomplishments.

**Creating Tutorials Is a Good Business**

Tutorials are the most in-demand content in the "use" aspect. Even though there are various tutorials online, those who are good at learning will think that just watching tutorials and following them is enough. However, some people don't watch tutorials carefully, so there is a demand for tutorials, and it's also a business that can be quickly started.

Tutorials can take many forms, such as videos, images, text, or live-streaming teaching. The same content can be used continuously, as new users will always exist. For example, Wikipedia has a dedicated section for tutorials, covering everything from tying shoelaces to various professional fields.

**Games Change Creation**

The impact of games on content creation is profound and significant. They can inspire our thinking, expand our horizons, and improve our writing and content creation abilities.

Games can give us infinite creativity and inspiration. For example, the story-based game Fortnite can stimulate our imagination and creativity, offering more sources of writing inspiration and helping us master more content.

Games can cultivate logical thinking and strategic application, which is crucial for creating high-quality content. For instance, StarCraft can enhance our decision-making and strategic thinking abilities, which are helpful for planning, organizing, and structuring written content.

Games can broaden our knowledge and cultural background. Historical and cultural-themed games like Total War and Civilization can help us understand more historical and cultural knowledge, expanding our horizons and thinking.

Games also develop our observational and descriptive abilities. During gameplay, we need to observe details of scenes, characters, and props, which can be applied to our writing to better portray characters and set scenes.

Through the development of plots and character creation in games, we can better understand human nature, society, and history. This has important implications for the development of plots, character personalities, and emotional expression in our writing.

The music, art, and level design in games can inspire our sense of beauty and aesthetic appreciation, allowing us to better understand beauty and art and integrate these elements into our writing, thus improving our creative level and expressive abilities.

Games can also enhance our cooperation and communication skills, which are important for our interactions with readers and peers. For example, multiplayer competitive games require communication to better coordinate strategies and achieve common goals.

The impact of games on content creation goes far beyond this, including our imagination, judgment, cultural literacy, and emotional expression. Creators should deeply experience the charm of games and apply it to content creation.

**Writing Is a Dialogue with Our Future Self.**

It represents the original intention of our writing. We are not just simply expressing our thoughts and views; more importantly, we want our words to transcend time and influence our future self.

Writing helps us better summarize and organize our thoughts. For everyone, thinking is a complex process. Our thoughts are often disrupted by chaotic and disordered information, and writing helps us organize our thoughts, allowing us to clearly see our ideas and their strengths and weaknesses. This will greatly assist our future self-development.

Writing also helps us improve our expression and communication abilities. Writing is not just about putting our thoughts on paper; it's also about making others understand our thoughts and intentions. This requires us to strengthen our logical thinking and expression abilities during the writing process, making our articles clearer and more coherent. Such good writing skills are very helpful for our future self in communication, negotiation, and collaboration.

Writing also allows us to reflect on and review our growth in the future. The process has encountered many difficulties and challenges, but every piece of writing is a testament to our intellectual growth. Our future self can also see the difficulties and challenges we've faced through these articles, better understanding our growth journey and providing a solid foundation for our future development.

Writing is a dialogue with our future self. It provides support for our future development. Striving to improve our writing skills, making our words increasingly valuable, and greatly influencing our future self-development.
