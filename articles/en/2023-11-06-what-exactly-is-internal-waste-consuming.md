# What Does Internal Friction Actually Consume?

> Published: 2023-11-06 · [中文](../zh/2023-11-06-%E5%86%85%E8%80%97%E5%88%B0%E5%BA%95%E5%9C%A8%E8%80%97%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88.md) | [English](../en/2023-11-06-what-exactly-is-internal-waste-consuming.md)

---

Within any organization—whether it's a company, a team, or any other form—internal waste is a common and dangerous phenomenon.
Internal waste refers to the mutual attacks, consumption, and destruction that occur within an organization due to various factors. It severely affects the development and achievement of organizational goals, making it an urgent issue to resolve.
Internal waste is typically caused by factors such as interests, power, and interpersonal relationships. For example, power struggles between factions, personal interest competition, and some workplace habits and vices can all lead to internal waste.
Internal waste not only consumes organizational resources and energy but also severely undermines internal cohesion and teamwork, leading to a deteriorating organizational atmosphere and an inability to achieve common goals.
Resolving internal waste requires the participation of all members. It is essential to fully recognize and focus on addressing the following aspects. First, establish good communication channels and mechanisms, allowing all parties to express themselves freely and resolve issues promptly. Second, strengthen cooperation and team building, cultivating a positive team culture that enhances team members' sense of cohesion and belonging.
In addition, establish fair and transparent systems and norms to ensure equitable distribution of interests and avoid conflicts of interest. Most importantly, foster the right values and a culture of trust, building harmonious and amicable interpersonal relationships and cultivating a mindset of win-win outcomes.
Creating a harmonious organizational atmosphere is crucial for long-term development. A harmonious atmosphere promotes trust and collaboration among employees, enhancing team cohesion and combat effectiveness.
Organizations with a harmonious atmosphere attract talent, boost employee motivation and creativity, and thus achieve better performance and competitiveness.
Moreover, a harmonious atmosphere can effectively reduce employee stress and tension, improving their sense of happiness and job satisfaction.
Personal internal waste is a widespread issue in both the workplace and daily life, yet its causes are often overlooked.

The emergence of personal internal waste is closely related to one's mindset. When facing setbacks and difficulties, people often fall into negative emotions, developing a passive attitude and behavior.
These negative mindsets and behaviors lead to the generation and development of personal internal waste. Therefore, maintaining a positive mindset—such as optimism, confidence, resilience, and initiative—is key to avoiding personal internal waste. This requires proactive mental adjustment to reduce the occurrence of internal waste.
The emergence of personal internal waste is also closely tied to the limitations of self-awareness. Personal internal waste often distorts one's self-perception and evaluation, leading to narrow and negative thinking patterns that result in self-doubt and a state of depression.
To reduce personal internal waste, one must start from self-evaluation, strengthen positive self-assessment, and overcome the limitations of self-evaluation bias. Only then can one better understand oneself and realize personal value.
The emergence of personal internal waste is also closely related to environmental influences. Work environment pressure, relationships with colleagues, and personal development space all affect one's attitude and behavior. When environmental pressure exceeds one's capacity to handle, internal waste is likely to occur.
To reduce personal internal waste, it is necessary to create a positive, harmonious, supportive, and open work environment that encourages free communication, potential development, shared learning, and growth.
The causes of personal internal waste are multifaceted, not only related to mindset and self-awareness but also closely tied to environmental influences. Recognizing these causes and taking corresponding measures is crucial for reducing personal internal waste.
By adjusting personal mindset and behavior, recognizing one's strengths and weaknesses, reducing the impact of self-evaluation bias, and creating a good work environment, one can better reduce and avoid personal internal waste, becoming an excellent individual and gradually achieving personal success and growth.

Internal waste is a common issue in both organizations and individuals, but outstanding people never fall into the trap of internal waste. They deal with it in a proactive and wise manner, maintaining high team cohesion and personal development.

Outstanding individuals understand that open and transparent communication is essential for preventing and resolving internal waste. They actively listen to others' opinions and concerns and respond promptly.
At the same time, they encourage team members to communicate and share their ideas, avoiding information asymmetry and misunderstandings. Through effective communication, the team can better understand each other, reducing internal waste and improving work efficiency.

Outstanding individuals know that building a culture of cooperation and trust is crucial for preventing internal waste. They encourage team members to share experiences of success and failure, and to learn and grow together.
They also value teamwork, encouraging collaboration and recognizing each other's value. This cultural atmosphere enhances team cohesion, improves work efficiency, and reduces internal waste.

Outstanding individuals understand that setting common goals and values is essential for uniting the team and preventing internal waste. They work with team members to set clear goals and define each person's responsibilities and contributions.
At the same time, they strive together with the team, embodying the team's values and culture. The consistency of team members' goals and values enhances team cohesion and reduces internal waste.

Outstanding individuals never fall into the vortex of internal waste. They deal with it through open and transparent communication, a culture of cooperation and trust, and shared goals and values.
These methods not only help them succeed in their careers but also provide the team with a harmonious and positive work environment. We should learn from outstanding individuals, using wisdom and a proactive attitude to deal with internal waste, achieving personal and team progress together.

Personal strategies for dealing with internal waste are diverse. Below are some common and effective approaches:
1. **Self-reflection and Cognitive Adjustment**: First, recognize that you are experiencing internal waste and examine the reasons behind it. Through self-reflection, understand your strengths and achievements, build confidence, and approach challenges and difficulties with a positive attitude. At the same time, change negative self-evaluations and views of yourself, focusing on discovering your strengths and value.
2. **Goal Planning and Prioritization**: Set clear goals and break them down into achievable sub-goals, which can improve work efficiency and reduce internal waste. Create reasonable plans and schedules, and prioritize tasks to complete them in an orderly manner, avoiding endless busyness that leads to internal waste.
3. **Seeking Support and Collaboration**: Communicate and share with family, friends, or colleagues, seeking support and advice, which can alleviate inner pressure and increase motivation and enthusiasm. At the same time, build good working relationships, share responsibilities, and collaborate to solve problems, reducing the occurrence of internal waste.
4. **Health Regulation and Relaxation**: Maintain a healthy lifestyle with adequate sleep, balanced nutrition, and moderate exercise, which can enhance mental and physical resilience and reduce internal waste. Additionally, find relaxing activities that suit you, such as listening to music, reading, or traveling, which can relieve stress and maintain mental and physical balance.
5. **Seeking Professional Support**: If you find it difficult to overcome internal waste on your own, it is recommended to seek help from a professional counselor or mental health expert. They can provide professional support and guidance, helping you explore the causes of internal waste and offering practical solutions.
Everyone's experience with internal waste and the methods to deal with it may differ, so it is important to choose strategies that suit your personal situation and needs.
Trying different methods and exchanging experiences with others can gradually help you find effective ways to deal with internal waste, achieving better work efficiency and quality of life.
Finally, it is emphasized repeatedly: never waste internally, never waste internally.
