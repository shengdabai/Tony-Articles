Li Xiaolai's course *The Truth About Coaching* systematically explains all aspects of coaching, and I highly recommend it. There are three key points I'd like to share.

1. Coaches focus more on practice than teaching.

2. The core of coaching is genuine love for students.

3. The secret to being a good coach: a mix of persuasion and trickery, without hesitation.

In our learning and growth journey, besides teachers, there are other roles that play important parts, such as coaches, mentors, and trainers.

They have clear differences, but among them, becoming a coach is the most valuable choice.

Compared to traditional teachers, coaches focus more on practice than teaching. Teachers usually aim to transmit knowledge, emphasizing information delivery and talent development in the classroom.

Coaches, on the other hand, pay more attention to students' practical skills and operational abilities, emphasizing skill improvement through repeated practice and guidance.

Coaches often use specific cases and examples to demonstrate correct methods, and provide personalized guidance to help students continuously improve and refine their skills.

This focus on practice helps us apply what we learn in real life and rapidly enhance our abilities.

Becoming a coach requires not only rich professional knowledge but also good interpersonal and communication skills. Coaches need to understand students' needs and goals and build close partnerships with them.

Through interaction and communication with students, coaches can better understand their problems and provide personalized solutions.

In contrast, teachers usually focus on collective instruction and lack personalized attention and guidance. Therefore, becoming a coach makes us more likely to become lifelong mentors and friends to students, helping them achieve their dreams.

According to survey and research data, there is evidence that coaches have a more positive impact on students' learning and development.

A study found that coaches' guidance and support significantly affect students' performance in academic competitions, helping them improve competitiveness and achieve better results.

Additionally, compared to teachers, becoming a coach can better cultivate students' leadership and teamwork skills, laying a solid foundation for their future careers.

Teachers and coaches both play important roles in cultivating students, yet they differ significantly in methods and goals.

Teachers' professionalism and knowledge transmission are their greatest strengths. They undergo systematic training, possessing solid subject knowledge and teaching skills.

Teachers focus on classroom instruction, helping students master theoretical knowledge and foundational skills. They are skilled at organizing and delivering knowledge, and assess students through exams and assignments.

If you pursue academic achievement and need systematic learning and mastery of various subjects, choosing a teacher as your mentor is a good choice.

In contrast, coaches focus more on cultivating practical and operational skills. Their goal is to develop students' hands-on abilities, emphasizing skill improvement through repeated practice and guidance.

Coaches typically teach knowledge through practice and case analysis, presenting real-life problems and challenges to help students solve them.

They emphasize personalized guidance and active participation, guiding students' learning through observation and hands-on experience. If you are more focused on improving practical skills and need to enhance your abilities through actual practice and guidance, choosing a coach as your mentor is more suitable.

Beyond these differences, teachers and coaches also differ in their goals and focus areas. Teachers usually emphasize students' academic performance and knowledge mastery, aiming to help students achieve excellent results in the academic field.

Coaches, on the other hand, focus more on students' personal development and career planning, aiming to help students discover their interests and potential and achieve success in both personal and professional domains. Therefore, you can choose the mentor role that best suits your needs and goals.

The best coach on a child's growth journey: why parents are more likely to fulfill this great mission?

As a coach, genuine love for students is key to achieving outstanding educational results. However, compared to other educators, parents are more likely to become a child's true golden coach. Why?

Parents naturally have a sincere love for their children, which is the first condition for becoming a golden coach. Because parents and children are connected by blood ties, they pour their love and care into their children's education without reservation.

No matter whether the child faces success or failure, parents are always by their side, sharing in their joys and sorrows. This sincere love and affection make parents more focused on the child's individual development and overall growth.

When my son lost a basketball game, he was deeply affected emotionally and physically. His father waited quietly for him to come home, without criticism, just silently offering encouragement and a hug. This sincere love made the son feel supported regardless of winning or losing, and after that, he was full of courage and motivation to face challenges again and again, making steady progress.

The close communication and trust relationship between parents and children is one of the keys to a golden coach's success. Parents can make full use of these advantages, allowing them to communicate more freely and flexibly with their children, share experiences, and build deep emotional connections, which helps children better accept and understand their parents' teachings.

Research shows that building a good parent-child relationship can significantly improve children's mental health and learning outcomes. And parent-child communication is one of the key methods for building such relationships. Direct and close communication between parents and children can strengthen mutual understanding and trust, an advantage that other educators find hard to match.

Parents, in the process of paying attention to their children's growth, can provide more thoughtful and detailed care and guidance. They deeply understand their children's interests and needs, and can offer targeted learning opportunities and resources. This personalized care can inspire children's learning motivation, helping them achieve better results and confidence in their growth.

Research has found that the home education environment has a significant impact on children's academic performance. As the closest people in their lives, parents play the role of a guiding principle, being able to flexibly adjust teaching methods and approaches to meet their children's individual needs.

Parents as coaches have unparalleled advantages, which stem from their sincere love and are closely tied to the establishment of emotional bonds and trust. They can provide children with meticulous care and guidance, nurturing their personality, interests, and potential.

Therefore, as parents, we should make full use of our strengths and tap into our potential as the best coaches, helping our children achieve greater success on their growth journey.

Lead, educate, and nurture your children with sincere love, laying a solid foundation for their future brilliance.

Unveiling the key to becoming an exceptional coach: Li Xiaolai's "no-holds-barred, a mix of persuasion and trickery."

Coaches are crucial in shaping the next generation, and how to become an exceptional coach is something we all strive for. In this process, Li Xiaolai proposed an intriguing and thought-provoking idea: no-holds-barred, a mix of persuasion and trickery.

No-holds-barred means exploring non-traditional teaching methods. Breaking the importance of traditional teaching, encouraging coaches to adopt innovative and radical approaches to achieve better educational outcomes. This means that in the educational process, we need to break free from constraints and try different teaching methods.

Research shows that introducing novel and interesting teaching methods can stimulate students' learning interest and enthusiasm, better promoting the understanding and mastery of knowledge. For example, using gamified teaching and fun-based learning methods can increase student engagement and autonomy, improving educational effectiveness.

A mix of persuasion and trickery means inspiring students' intrinsic motivation. The importance of motivating students to take an active role in their learning, and using some means to guide them to accept and understand knowledge.

Students' active participation is one of the key factors in improving learning outcomes. Through experiential learning, case analysis, and other methods, coaches can embed knowledge into contexts that interest students, making them feel immersed and enhancing the appeal and effectiveness of learning.

Customizing personalized teaching plans, focusing on students' needs. Li Xiaolai emphasized the importance of tailoring teaching plans according to students' actual needs, thereby increasing the relevance and effectiveness of teaching.

Personalized teaching has been proven to be an effective way to improve students' learning outcomes and self-development. Through regular communication and feedback, coaches can understand students' learning needs and create personalized educational plans to meet their actual needs.

The strategy of no-holds-barred and a mix of persuasion and trickery allows coaches to break away from traditional models, try more innovative teaching methods, and inspire students' learning motivation.

However, in the implementation process, coaches also need to maintain ethical boundaries, ensuring the authenticity and sustainability of educational goals. As coaches with a unique role, we can flexibly apply the strategy of no-holds-barred and a mix of persuasion and trickery, guiding students to take an active role in learning and making positive contributions to their growth and development.
