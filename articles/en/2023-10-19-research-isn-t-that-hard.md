What's the English word for research? *Research*—split it into *search* and *re*. It means searching repeatedly, searching again and again, searching hard.

Li Xiaolai once mentioned a classic method for learning and growth: *search, search, search again; pay, pay, pay again; organize, organize, organize again.*

When researching a topic, find related questions, identify the difficulties, and then start the research. Make bold assumptions and carefully verify them.

**Title: How Ordinary People Can Use the Awareness of Difficulties to Conduct Research and Integrate It into Every Aspect of Life**

Research is no longer exclusive to scholars. With the arrival of the information age, it's becoming increasingly easy for ordinary people to research topics they care about. The awareness of difficulties has become an essential method for ordinary people to conduct research.

The awareness of difficulties can help ordinary people integrate research into every aspect of their lives and bring about significant progress in their personal and professional development.

The awareness of difficulties is a mindset that involves continuously paying attention to and thinking about problems in society, personal life, or work.

To discover a valuable research question, first determine its uniqueness and delve deeply into it. The awareness of difficulties helps us actively seek out problems and better understand the underlying reasons behind each one.

The core idea of the awareness of difficulties is problem-oriented. It needs to be strengthened through practice. We can start by addressing problems in daily life and create opportunities and topics for research.

For example, if we're interested in product design issues, we can deeply study the product development process, discover problems in practice, solve them, and continuously improve the product. Through this process, we'll gain professional knowledge and skills, enabling us to better master vocational abilities.

We can integrate the awareness of difficulties into all aspects of our lives. In natural science research, we can explore the relationship between humans and nature to find clues for research questions. In social science research, we can find research topics by participating in volunteer activities or visiting communities.

This approach of using the awareness of difficulties helps us better connect research with real-world problems, offering practical and feasible value.

The awareness of difficulties is a highly practical research method that can help ordinary people solve various life problems and improve their professional skills.

In our daily lives, we can fully integrate the awareness of difficulties into all areas and find problems and opportunities in various fields.

By cultivating the awareness of difficulties, we can become more creative, adaptable, and in control, gaining greater sense of achievement and satisfaction.

Furthermore, we need to learn how to ask questions and discover the real issues.

Solving problems is one of the core drivers of human progress and innovation. However, many times we get stuck in surface-level thinking and fail to deeply explore the root of the problem.

Learning to ask questions and uncover the essence and underlying causes of a problem can help us find practical solutions.

When facing a problem, we can dig deeper by asking "why." Don't be satisfied with surface answers; instead, try to explore the reasons behind the issue.

For example, if sales are declining, we can ask why sales are down? Is it due to product quality or changes in market demand? By continuously asking "why," we can gradually uncover the real issue and address it at its root.

Working backward is an excellent method for solving complex problems. We can start from the final outcome of a problem and work backward to find the causes and processes.

For instance, if a project is delayed, we can first work backward to identify the time point when the problem occurred and then find the reasons for the delay. This method helps us locate the root of the problem and provides the correct direction for solving it.

Another example is time, which is the most precious resource and also the most important production material. Whether an activity is worth doing depends on whether it requires a long time. From this perspective, starting with cutting down trees to make pencils is extremely inefficient and not cost-effective.

Speaking of this, I strongly recommend reading the article *The Story of a Pencil*. In modern production and manufacturing, few people truly understand the entire process of pencil production.

Multi-angle thinking is one of the key methods for uncovering the essence of a problem. We should avoid narrow-minded thinking and examine problems from different perspectives.

For example, if market sales are declining, we can consider it from the perspectives of product quality, competitors, and marketing. This approach helps us fully understand the problem and find more comprehensive and effective solutions.

Learning to ask questions and discovering the real issues is the key step in solving problems. By asking "why," using the backward approach, and thinking from multiple angles, we can deeply uncover the essence of a problem and find fundamental solutions.

In the process of solving problems, don't be satisfied with surface answers. Be brave in asking questions and exploring the truth. View problems from deeper and more comprehensive angles to provide more targeted solutions.

In the end, everything boils down to self-learning ability.

In this era of information overload, self-learning ability has become an essential skill. Knowing how to self-learn not only satisfies personal curiosity and thirst for knowledge but also opens up vast possibilities.

Self-learning is the ability to think independently and master knowledge. It allows us to explore, learn, and develop without external guidance.

In the field of learning, self-learning ability helps us enrich our knowledge base and improve learning efficiency. In life and work, it enables us to adapt to rapidly changing environments, boost confidence, and enhance our ability to handle challenges.

Ultimately, self-learning ability is a mindset and habit of lifelong learning. It allows us to continue growing and progressing at every stage of life.

When improving self-learning ability, the first step is to focus on developing good learning habits. Create a reasonable study plan, break down knowledge points, and master learning methods and techniques, such as setting a schedule, using memory techniques, and improving reading comprehension.

In addition, actively utilizing various learning resources is an important way to enhance self-learning ability. The internet offers a wealth of knowledge and learning resources, including online courses, teaching videos, e-books, and more. By making good use of these resources, we can choose our learning content and time freely, improving learning outcomes.

Pursuing self-driven learning and continuous learning is key to enhancing self-learning ability. Self-driven learning means we can actively set learning goals and maintain motivation and enthusiasm for learning.

Continuous learning means maintaining consistent engagement and exploration in learning, constantly updating our knowledge and skills. To achieve continuous learning, we can attend various training courses and workshops, find learning partners and mentors, and exchange ideas and experiences with other learners.

Cultivating self-driven learning requires developing curiosity, confidence, and self-discipline, and persistently pursuing knowledge and growth.

No matter in learning, life, or work, self-learning ability is an essential skill. Mastering self-learning ability allows us to freely navigate the ocean of knowledge and unlock vast possibilities.

By developing good learning habits, utilizing learning resources, pursuing self-driven learning, and committing to continuous learning, we can continuously improve our self-learning ability and expand our knowledge and skills.

Believe it or not, as long as you have self-learning ability, you will be able to face future challenges and achieve greater success.
