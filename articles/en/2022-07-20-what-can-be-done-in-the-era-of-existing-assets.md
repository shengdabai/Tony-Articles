Note 95

Existing Era Incremental Era #Plain Language#

01 As times develop, following trends is essential. Adhering to laws and respecting cycles, although these sound understandable, are hard to maintain in reality.

02 Suppose a streamer, after establishing their account, quickly expands, adds more live streaming equipment, expands the team, and increases the range of live-streaming products. After a series of operations, what happens?

03 It's likely that they'll find themselves unable to keep up with the fast pace of development, with insufficient personnel capabilities, bloated organization, increasing unnecessary expenses, and inadequate operational skills—these situations could all occur.

04 In the incremental era, there are many opportunities because the market is not mature and there are numerous commercial demands. However, in the era of existing assets, resources are abundant, and factors like quality and cost-effectiveness are considered more.

05 WeChat Channels were in an incremental phase early on, with a large number of creators entering, offering many opportunities and possibilities. Are we now in the era of existing assets? I think not; we're still in the incremental phase.

06 Although there are many opportunities in the incremental phase, it doesn't mean that entering the field will immediately lead to success. A period of calm is needed, consistent effort, and waiting for maturity. Also, one should be prepared for competition in the era of existing assets.

07 Be realistic, respect the development laws of things, and focus on content creation. Doing long-term valuable work and making choices that benefit the long term is always the way to go.
