Note 55

How important is continuous content creation? #Short Video #Plain Language

01 After stopping updates on WeChat Channels for over half a year, the likes and followers of my videos are still growing.

02 The long-tail effect, in my words, means that while you're sleeping, your content keeps spreading. Your work is like a fish in the ocean 🐠, it keeps swimming around, gradually growing bigger, and eventually leading more fish forward.

03 The process of video dissemination has randomness and uncertainty, but it also has certainty. As long as the content is good, someone will appreciate it.

04 What is good content? How can I keep creating? My content is pretty good, so why isn't my following growing? When you have these thoughts, congratulations—you're starting to think about the meaning of content distribution. What I want to tell you is that the content isn't enough yet; it's easy to get lost. Please be patient and let it mature.

05 So why, despite my inconsistent actions, haven't I been consistently creating? I need to admit my shortcomings. For the past six months, I've been fully focused on work and my postgraduate project, and I didn't take my content seriously. Now, after rethinking my direction and planning, I will get back to it.

06 For me, the core of creation comes from sincerity. Believe me, the content you put your heart into will definitely be liked by someone. In this vast world, you can influence and connect a group of people.

07 How can a drop of water never disappear? By falling into the sea.

I'm done writing. Thank you for being the one who makes things happen!
