What does the idiom *another way to think* really mean?

> 【Explanation】To open up a new path. It metaphorically means to create a new style or method.
>
>
>
>
> 【Origin】From Ye Shengtao's *Ni Huanzhi*: “For such an innovative educational philosophy and method, I indeed have a firm belief.”
>
> Han Dianhttps://www.zdic.net/hans/

One word in the definition is particularly appealing: *create*. Words like innovation, creation, and invention all sound positive and are enough to spark wild imaginations. But the question is: can we really create a new style or method?

When it comes to the idea of transformation, my first thought is investment, since it's seen by many as an opportunity for exponential income, even a way to change one's fate.

When I first entered the investment market, with so many investment options to choose from, what would you pick?

*(Note: "subject matter" is a legal term referring to the rights and obligations between contract parties. The object of these rights and obligations is called the "subject-matter asset," such as goods, property, or real estate. In the context of investment, a specific stock or asset is referred to as an investment "subject matter".)*

It's well known that high-quality, proven investment "targets" such as Coca-Cola, Apple, Moutai, and Tencent have delivered impressive historical performance and returns to investors and shareholders. But isn't there still a group of people who want to find the next "something"?

The next company that will surpass Apple, a better version of it. I think I found it... This company's wine is way better than Moutai, and its value will definitely surpass Mout, I believe.

Have you ever had such thoughts?

Why do we still have the illusion of improving something that has already been proven to be excellent? Why do we keep wanting to take another path, to discover something with even greater potential?

I think this might be the result of vanity. After all, if everyone buys Moutai and you make money, there's not much to brag about. But if you take a different path and find something better, that would be really impressive.

Isn't this just a fantasy?

Going deeper, could taking another path also be a reflection of our inner dissatisfaction with mediocrity? How can I be just an ordinary person? I have to take a path that no one else has taken. Look at that person, they changed their fate that way.

Wait a second. Having such thoughts shows that we have ambition, but are we missing something?

Are venture capitalists really taking risks? Are they just rich and bold, so they can afford to take risks and thus make money?

This is a fantasy. When we don’t understand a profession, we tend to label it and jump to conclusions. Obviously, venture capitalists are a profession that the general public doesn’t fully understand.

Venture Capital Investment, also known as VC, the word *venture* has the meaning of risk, but it can also mean the courage to do something. In reality, the core of venture capital is risk management, which is about reducing risk to the lowest level. Don’t worry, investors aren’t just blindly investing. In fact, they are more likely to be risk-averse.

If we now have the idea of taking another path, and decide to try venture investing, we’re probably just going to quietly exit.

In academic fields and our daily lives, we often apply a simple yet effective way of thinking: analogy.

For example, when we say a table is high, we are describing its physical height. But when we use "high" to describe a person's ability, we say they are "high-level." This is the application of analogy.

This is even more common in academic fields. For instance, we may have heard the term "entropy increase," which describes an increase in disorder. Originally, it was a physics concept, but it has since been applied to the business world.

Entropy is a physical quantity that reflects the uncertainty or disorder of a system. The concept of entropy was first introduced by Clausius in thermodynamics, used to measure the ratio of heat absorbed or released to temperature in a reversible process.

Later, in the field of information theory, entropy is a measure of uncertainty in a dataset. How do we calculate it?

Here we skip the formula and calculation examples. From its meaning, information gain helps us find the feature that best distinguishes a dataset, thus building a more effective decision tree. The greater the information gain, the more the feature can reduce the uncertainty of the dataset, meaning it can better separate samples of different classes. If the information gain is zero, it means the feature has no effect on dividing the dataset, meaning it cannot separate samples of different classes.

It still sounds a bit complicated. Let’s look at this sentence: the first person to use flowers to metaphorically describe a beauty is a genius, the second is a mediocre person, and the third is a fool.

For example, the first time you are scammed is because you're naive, the second time because you're ignorant, and the third time, it's just stupidity. My first time being scammed was because of online brushing, which others might see as foolish, but I thought it was a way to earn some extra money for living.

The information gain at the first time of being scammed is the highest, gradually decreasing.

Taking another path might provide some information gain, but whether there's evidence, whether it's logical, or whether it's influenced by others are all factors that matter.

Understanding information gain from different angles, being original might be an opportunity, but if it's incorrect and doesn’t provide value, then it’s not worth doing.

Writing is the same. We always want to be different, to make a big statement for the sake of information gain. In a lot of clickbait articles, you might have seen such content.

Bezos: “Once I asked Buffett, your investment philosophy is so simple, why don’t people just copy it?”

Buffett: “Because no one wants to get rich slowly.”
