In accordance with the 2023 "Clear and Bright" series of special actions, the Cyberspace Administration of China has launched a one-month special campaign titled "Clear and Bright - Rectifying Poor Information Orientation in Short Video Content" starting from December 5th. The rectification campaign has been ongoing for a week, and we have also seen the country's strong efforts to regulate short videos.

For example, our country was the first, and remains the only one, to impose restrictions on the duration and content of short videos and live streams for minors. It has strictly prohibited minors from making payments, especially in the gaming sector, where there are strict regulations. This area has indeed required a significant amount of effort and manpower.

On the policy and requirement level, efforts are being made to help us return to a healthier online environment. Meanwhile, netizens themselves also need to rid themselves of bad habits through learning, such as spending excessive time on short videos and live streams, which is, in fact, a very costly use of time.

Title: Cyberspace Administration of China Launches Special Action to Rectify Poor Information Orientation in Short Video Content, Contributing to a Clear and Bright Online Space

The "Clear and Bright" campaign mainly focuses on three major issues: the spread of false information in short videos, the display of inappropriate behavior in short videos, and the spread of incorrect concepts through short videos.

The first issue is the spread of false information in short videos. On short video platforms, some users create false scenarios through staged filming, generate fake videos using technology, or alter real content without regard for facts, misleading the audience or achieving some form of personal gain. This false information can confuse public perception and disrupt social order. This campaign will strengthen the review and control of false information, safeguarding the public's right to access accurate information.

The second issue is the display of inappropriate behavior in short videos. On short video platforms, some content creators attract attention by showcasing "pornographic edge" behavior, building lowbrow personas, or engaging in maliciousinfluencer marketing. However, this also lowers the overall quality and reputation of the platform. This campaign will rigorously combat such behaviors, promoting the development of short video platforms toward healthier, more noble content, allowing users to enjoy a clear and bright online space.

The third issue is the spread of incorrect concepts through short videos. Some short videos challenge public cognitive boundaries and spread incorrect value orientations, which can have negative impacts on specific groups such as young people. The Cyberspace Administration of China actively promotes correct values and moral perspectives, and through this campaign, it will strengthen the rectification of incorrect concepts, guiding short video content to be more positive and uplifting, spreading positive energy.

The "Clear and Bright - Rectifying Poor Information Orientation in Short Video Content" special campaign launched by the Cyberspace Administration of China holds significant importance for maintaining a clear and bright online environment. By addressing the three major issues of spreading false information, displaying inappropriate behavior, and spreading incorrect concepts, it aims to create a healthier and higher-quality short video content ecosystem. As netizens, we should also actively pay attention to the quality of content on short video platforms and work together to create a clear and bright online space.

With the rapid development of the short video industry, relevant authorities have also begun to intensify their efforts to rectify poor information orientation in short video content. In this context, short video creators not only need to pay attention to industry dynamics but also actively respond to the three major issues in the special rectification campaign.

The spread of false information in short videos is one of the hot topics of public concern. To address this issue, creators should focus on the authenticity and credibility of information. During the creative process, they should deeply understand relevant professional knowledge, collect reliable data and evidence, and avoid spreading unverified information blindly. At the same time, creators can enhance the credibility and appeal of their content by providing examples, citing expert opinions, and other methods.

The display of inappropriate behavior in short videos can easily lead to public skepticism and negative imitation. As creators, we should adhere to correct values and actively create positive short video content. For example, we can choose to showcase uplifting stories about people, share beneficial lifestyle practices for physical and mental health, or spread information about social justice and responsibility. At the same time, we should avoid lowbrow content and actions that infringe on others' privacy, establishing a good social image.

The spread of incorrect concepts through short videos can have negative impacts on specific groups such as young people. Creators should play an active guiding role and spread correct values and moral perspectives. This can be achieved by telling positive life stories, conveying a positive and upward attitude toward life, promoting social responsibility and awareness of public welfare, and guiding viewers to form correct perceptions and values.

When facing the three major issues in the short video special rectification campaign, short video creators should actively pay attention to social needs and industry trends, continuously improving their professional competence and content quality.

By adhering to a creative philosophy of authenticity, positivity, and optimism, creators can produce high-quality short video content that wins the favor and recognition of viewers. We believe that through joint efforts, the short video industry will usher in a healthier and more valuable development.

As users, how can we protect our enjoyment of visual and auditory content while avoiding negative impacts?

Although short video platforms have strengthened content review and management, false information is still difficult to completely eliminate. As users, you can protect your ability to judge and choose information.

When watching short videos, you should not only focus on the entertainment value of the videos themselves but also think rationally about the authenticity of the information. Try to choose videos from reliable sources with authoritative backgrounds, such as official-certified news accounts.

The display of inappropriate behavior in short videos can easily mislead viewers and lead to negative imitation. Users should cultivate their aesthetic sense and values, understand which behaviors are inappropriate, and avoid imitating them.

Also, actively participate in the short video community, expressing support for high-quality videos and creators through likes and comments, contributing to the spread of positive content and jointly creating a healthy short video ecosystem.

On short video platforms, it is easy to spread incorrect values and concepts. For users, it is very important to carefully choose which videos to watch. Always keep a clear mind, avoid being misled, and not follow blindly.

When enjoying short videos, you can pay attention to those that spread positive and upward values, such as videos that encourage hard work, share family and friendship, and promote social positive energy. At the same time, you should also actively participate in discussions, communicate with creators through comments or private messages, and jointly discover more valuable content.

As short video users, we are not only viewers but also co-creators of the platform. In the special rectification campaign, we should remain vigilant, screen high-quality content, and actively engage in community interactions.

While protecting our enjoyment of visual and auditory content, we should also contribute our own strength to the healthy development of the short video community.
