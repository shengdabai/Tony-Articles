# A Century of Dreaming

91st Original Article

The Communist Party of China was founded in 1921, marking its 100th anniversary. To be precise, the First National Congress of the CPC was held on July 23, 1921, and later, Chairman Mao Zedong designated July 1st as the Party's founding anniversary. Under the leadership of the CPC, over 90 million members have led the Chinese nation and its people onto a path of happiness, lifting 1.4 billion people out of poverty and ushering them into a moderately prosperous society. At this moment, I can only salute the Party flag and the national flag, pay tribute to the heroes who have dedicated themselves to the cause of the Party, the country, and the people, honor every revolutionary pioneer, and express my respect for every ordinary citizen!

I want to write this article to commemorate my journey of joining the Party. As of today, I have been a member for 2,586 days. I joined the Communist Party of China on June 2, 2014. You might already know that I joined at the age of 18. I would like to especially thank my high school for giving me the opportunity to participate in the university-level Party training program. As an affiliated high school, we could recommend students for Party membership, and I was fortunate enough to be one of only two students in my grade to join. Whenever someone asks me why I joined the Party, my answer is always two words—faith!

My original intention has never changed: to serve the people. I have also hesitated and felt lost. Some people think that being both red and specialized is just posturing, while others believe you're just a nice guy who always defends the Party. There are all sorts of opinions... Some people want to discourage my enthusiasm, others want to argue with me. They all have their own justifications. Sometimes I don't know how to respond, feeling powerless, unable to convince them. Now I realize my thinking is flawed. I want to persuade them, to change them—but that's futile. You don't need to say anything. Just do it. Let results speak. More importantly, let my personal actions respond to my original intention.

Not only do my friends challenge me, but my family members do too. They question our achievements, always thinking that poverty hasn't been eradicated, that some people are still poor! They can always come up with various examples at any time. But have they seriously collected data, conducted in-depth research, and thought about it? Of course not! Then what can I say to them? Let them say whatever they want. In fact, there are some people who just open their mouths and speak without any basis, without any reasoning. They just use a few examples. Ask them, do they know what the poverty alleviation standards are? Do they know how we achieved it? More importantly, do they really understand the significance of poverty alleviation? Say it, who doesn't know? It's just a matter of bringing the upper and lower lips together.

All these voices have helped me grow better and made me more resolute in my mission. I joined the Party in high school, became the student branch secretary of my college, and later served as the secretary of the Youth League committee. For me, these impressive titles are really not important. What matters is being able to do something for my classmates, helping them, and contributing a little to my college and university. Participating in community volunteer work, building long-term social support practices. Various public welfare activities on campus— we not only arm ourselves with theory, but also put it into practice!

After graduation, some friends thought I was suitable for a government position. My qualifications certainly fit, but I chose to become a teacher. I don't think one must hold a leadership position. I prefer to have leadership and influence more people! I am a student, pursuing a degree in educational leadership, and also participating in the International English Trainer Program. My master's degree, overseas experience, and international certification—none of these are so important. What matters is that I have been growing continuously. The excellent people around me have guided me. Thank you, it's because of you that I have grown step by step!

My original intention remains unchanged. I will dedicate my life to the cause of education. Under the leadership of the Party, I will do my job conscientiously and work hard to build dreams. We have completed the first century dream. The next one, we will continue!
