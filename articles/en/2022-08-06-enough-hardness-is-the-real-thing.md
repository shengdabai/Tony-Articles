Note 99

Content Must Be Hard Enough

001 WeChat Channels always emphasizes content. If the content is hard, WeChat Channels can make it bigger. But if the content isn't good, WeChat Channels won't be too harsh on you.

002 If the content isn't good, WeChat Channels has some tolerance, because at least your WeChat friends will support you. Maybe you'll get views, likes, comments, or even shares.

003 Most creators only see how many people are watching a streamer, how much income they make, and think that as long as they do good marketing, everything will be fine. But the truth isn't like that.

004 Being skilled is marketing, poor product needs marketing, and insufficient content needs marketing—these are all wrong understandings. Marketing is a means, and the core is content.

005 Some content can be emotional consumption, some gives practical skills, and some offers high-quality products. Just do the content well, and selling products or courses is fine. Keep doing it consistently.

006 There's one more point to mention. Content that reaches 80 points needs marketing to amplify it. Content that reaches 120 points doesn't need marketing or WeChat Channels amplification.

007 For example, people with great skills may not need to communicate; they can fully leverage their strengths. Most ordinary people need to do content well and also do marketing, because marketing makes content more influential and shareable, amplifying your value.

I've finished writing. Thank you for your likes and support if you're someone who can make things happen!
