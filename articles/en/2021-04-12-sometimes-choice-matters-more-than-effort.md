In the *Dedao* column *The Road to Financial Freedom* on July 31, 2016, there was an article titled *A Formal Invitation to Live Together for a Year*. The column author, Li Xiaolai, once labeled himself as a lifelong growth practitioner, and I also adopted this label for myself, to encourage each other. In the article, Li Xiaolai emphasized the importance of reading every word of the text. I joined the column in August 2020. Since most of the content on Dedao is audio courses, I spent most of my time listening. However, this column, which requires reading every word, was somewhat unfamiliar to me. At that time, I didn’t take it seriously and only listened to the later audio versions, but each time I was deeply moved. I knew it was time for me to change. In the article, Li Xiaolai mentioned:

> I said at the beginning that every article in this column needs to be read word for word, so this column will not have an audio version. The purpose is to "force" you to sit down and read the article thoroughly. This way, you can truly focus your attention and time on reading, thinking, decision-making, and action. At the beginning of the column, I also left a hint: when the right timing and format appear, I won’t rule out the possibility of making audio or other media versions.

The reason that attracted me to this column was initially freedom! As an independent individual, doesn’t everyone have a desire for freedom? After I left my job, I began to think more about wealth accumulation. I needed to think seriously, after all, I needed to learn how to both earn and save. This column might offer me some different changes! Luo Pang said this column helps you upgrade your cognitive operating system, and this summary made me even more determined to join the column. At first, I also used fragmented time to listen to the courses. Every time I heard Li Xiaolai share, I felt like I had been struck by lightning. The teacher emphasized that just listening was not enough—you had to think, record your insights, and put them into practice.

But I was still defeated by laziness, only listening, and sometimes trying things like “Zuo Xiang” (a method to enhance focus), which gave me a new experience and improved my concentration. What made me start writing a blog to record my learning insights? Honestly, there’s just too much knowledge, and there are so many paid knowledge communities out there. As Li Xiaolai said: paying is like picking up a bargain! I did pay, learning condensed knowledge from the industry, and had the chance to interact with knowledgeable teachers. But if learning and thinking only stay in my mind, I think it’s like memorizing vocabulary—looked at it once, remembered it, but when asked, it’s... you know, embarrassing! A lot of knowledge goes in one ear and out the other, only a little remains, and even that isn’t deep. That’s not deliberate practice, right? As Li Xiaolai told us, you need to internalize it, make it a natural behavior. I started recording my learning insights. Every time I typed a word on the keyboard, it went back into my mind, upgrading my understanding of the concepts. I wanted to practice each one, grow with time.

**Financial Freedom:**

> Financial freedom: Wikipedia says, “Financial freedom refers to the state where you don’t need to work for money to cover your living expenses.” This definition doesn’t seem to offer any guidance. After reading it repeatedly, I still didn’t know what to do next. That’s not good. What we truly want is not wealth itself, but freedom. Wealth is just a tool. What we truly want is the freedom to control our time. So I redefined “financial freedom” for myself: “Personal financial freedom” means that a person no longer needs to sell their time to meet basic living needs. I think this definition is concise, accurate, correct, and has practical guidance. “Knowing the clearest, most accurate, and correct definition of something” is what our column helps you achieve. How does our column help you achieve financial freedom? The answer is simply “brainwashing.” You can find the right direction early on through this “brainwashing” method. By actively cleansing your mind, you can become smarter.

> Above content is from the *Dedao* column *The Road to Financial Freedom* — Li Xiaolai

This definition of financial freedom made me think for a long time. Not having to sell your time to meet basic living needs! Basic living needs cover many aspects, and we each have our own understanding. The scope varies, but we can simply say it includes clothing, food, shelter, and transportation. But I know this is far from enough. No need to worry about these anymore, no need to sell your time for them. Li Xiaolai has often said that time essentially doesn’t belong to us, and we can’t manage it. The only thing we can do is manage ourselves, improve our focus, and fully commit to one thing. There’s a popular concept called “flow”—the state of being completely immersed in an activity. Only with such a clear definition can we get specific guidance. By not selling your time, you can make your income far exceed what it is now. Either increase your income per unit of time or sell one unit of time for multiple times, earning multiple returns from one unit of time. Only when we have enough earning power can we possibly start to achieve financial freedom.

> Asking questions is more important than finding answers. The “choice questions” I once encountered had three points:
>
> 1. There should be clear options.
> 2. The options should be sufficiently complete and specific.
> 3. Ideally, they should be details or small issues from a growing process.

After graduating with a degree in civil engineering, how should I choose:

> 1. Work in the profession—construction companies, real estate, etc.
> 2. Continue studying, pursue a master’s degree domestically, consider design institutes or further apply to universities.
> 3. Switch industries, such as training in civil engineering.
> 4. Switch to a different field, such as entering the education industry to teach English.
> 5. Study abroad, I had already received an offer for a master’s degree in civil engineering at a German university.

Perhaps there are even more possibilities.

After university, I had a conflict with my father regarding my future career plan. I chose to enter the education industry, stepping into a new field, which led to some disagreements between us. I insisted on choosing education, even though I had little experience. I had always enjoyed sharing with others during university, and when I taught others interesting things, I felt very happy. I gradually began to think about the impact of education on people, and I solidified my decision. My father wanted me to choose civil engineering, knowing many people who could help me find better jobs in the future. The country was also vigorously developing its infrastructure, and he believed this industry had a bright future. In a few years, I could become a chief engineer, and my life would improve. The core of our conflict was whether to choose an existing field or enter a new environment, which was a risk assessment and consideration of uncertainty. After graduation, I still chose education, continuously persuading my family. Although they didn’t clearly support me, I still let me go ahead. I moved to a new city and started my teaching career. I firmly believe I will continue in education. I want to complete something of my own, to help more people love language, and for everyone to become a language enthusiast.

> Comparative Analysis:
>
> Civil Engineering:
>
> Advantages: 1. Strong professional foundation 2. Broad career choices 3. Relatively stable work
> Disadvantages: 1. Poor working environment 2. Lots of socializing 3. Long project cycles
>
> Education:
>
> Advantages: 1. More development opportunities 2. Enjoy sharing and love for language 3. Great benefits for my child’s education in the future
> Disadvantages: 1. Insufficient foundation, need to strengthen theory 2. Lack of experience 3. High competition, uneven market

After analyzing, I think I will communicate again with my father, letting him know I don’t worry about starting from scratch. I love it, and I’m still young, with many opportunities to make mistakes. I will stick with it. Regarding industry development, I will also discuss with my father. Every industry has its own pros and cons. When I make a choice, I also accept the risks behind it. But my love will keep me going. I will let my father be at ease. I don’t know how much success I can achieve, but I believe I can help some people. I also hope to change some things in the future, and more people will love English and other languages, seeing a broader world!

From financial freedom to career choices, what we choose is more important. Choosing is the beginning of everything. When we choose financial freedom, we are already assuming we won’t sell our time for basic living needs. We need to find ways to get multiple values from one unit of time. Choosing a career also requires serious consideration. When passion, opportunity, and skill intersect, the best career choice appears. The education industry is where these three intersect for me, and I will stick with it firmly.

—Choice Matters More Than Effort
