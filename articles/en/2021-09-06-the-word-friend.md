There's joy in having friends from afar!

The word "friend" is just two characters, yet its weight is not light. Ask yourself, how many friends do I have?

When you ask this question, you might want to define the concept of "friend" or try to classify it, as if there are many kinds of friends...

To me, a friend is someone with talent and wealth 💰, and following them, I'm sure I can become better too.

Looking back on my student days and work period, I've met many friends. They've given me endless motivation on my path to growth, and meeting them has been a great honor!

Help your friends, help your friends, help your friends.

This has always been my practiced value. Being able to help my friends makes me feel honored. I used to not know why I was so happy, but now I understand. The moment I share, I gain something. Helping friends allows me to gain the value I want, bit by bit!

Starting from kindergarten, I've experienced making friends. This feeling seems different in every stage. Friends required within a set context, friends met by chance, friends introduced by friends—there are so many ways to meet friends. What comes next?

We need to spend time building connections with our friends. If we could mark something on our heads, we and our friends would have an emotional account on our heads. Some have 100, some have 10, some have 0, and some even have negative numbers...

When the number on our head is very small or negative, can we ask our friends to do something for us? I advise you not to. After all, the emotional account has no balance left, and it might even be a debt to the other person.

Instead of asking friends for something, we should consider what we can offer them. This also applies when meeting a mentor. Don't expect the mentor to help you; instead, ask yourself what you can offer the mentor, or what reason you have to ask the mentor for help!

The way we interact with friends varies, and so do our emotional accounts. Use the right way to interact with friends, and never mix up the accounts!

Some of these accounts are top-tier accounts for me, or VIP accounts. They would make me set aside everything, and I look forward to our conversations no matter how long it's been. Reuniting is always warm and joyful.

Treasure these few VIP users you have. Give them the highest weight, and make sure to value them!

Sometimes, you can also find your exclusive partner through different topics. If you don't know who to turn to, you should pay attention to your friends regularly and add some tags to their accounts. It can be quite fun to make friends this way!

We look forward to helping our friends, but when our own abilities are effective, don't give any advice, don't be overly enthusiastic, just nod politely. Respect your friends!

Thank you to all the friends I've met along the way. Having you all around is really great!

Goodnight, sweet dreams 😊
