01 If you have a lot of talent, will you hesitate because you can't fully express it? Are you thinking about how to design a product? It's completely normal to have these thoughts. The best choice you can make is to consult.

02 How can you grow better and faster? Output is a great method, and the forms of output can be summarized as writing and speaking.

03 Consulting is pretty powerful — not only do you have to speak, but you also have to write. Reading and listening are also essential. You'll come to understand your users better and refine a clearer business model.

04 For example, I've connected with high-quality clients and excellent partners every day through consulting. During the consulting process, we can continuously refine our own abilities.

05 Take a small point, listening. Do you think listening is important? If you do, are we really listening? If we can't listen better, how can we solve problems?

06 The consulting process is like the butcher who dissects an ox — breaking down every detail for analysis and organization. Each line of the ox becomes clearly visible. How to turn the beef into a delicious dish — is it our job to cook, or should we guide the user to do it? That's the business point after consulting.
