## Sharing One: The Power of Branding

Do you recognize these brand slogans?

New Oriental Middle and Primary School Education: New Oriental, Teacher Good

Lao Niang Jiu: Rice should bedone right, just eat Lao Niang Jiu

Dedao App: Knowledge is here at Dedao

Moxi Ice City: Real milk, real tea, real fruit particles, Moxi Ice City is sweet

Jiu Jiu Ya Duck Neck: Love me, then chew me

Ge Fu Ren: Ge Ge Ge, Ge Fu Ren

Xibei Yóu Mian Cun: I♥Yóu (Close your eyes and order, every bite is delicious)

Haidilao: Together, let's have fun, Haidilao

Zu Li Jian Elderly Shoes: Professional Elderly Shoes, Choose Zu Li Jian

Hanting Hotel: Love cleanliness, stay at Hanting

Tian Qi: Take photos and shout Tian Qi loudly

All these slogans come from one company, which is Hu & Hua Company. I first learned about this company through Dedao App, and then met Hu Shanshan, whose course *Learning Brand Marketing from Hu Shanshan* gave me a new understanding of the power of branding. Originally, brand marketing was so interesting.

Let me start with an object: traffic lights have three colors, red, yellow, and green. Their meanings are obvious to us. On a computer, which do you prefer: the red, yellow, and green buttons of Apple or the minimize, maximize, and close buttons of Microsoft? Which is easier to use?

We have a natural instinct for red, yellow, and green. We don’t need to be taught; we naturally know how to use them. This is what is called a super symbol.

What is a brand? A brand includes a "symbol system" and a "language text system."

The language text system is the logic of the product and its composition, consisting of business theory, value standards, choice logic, purchase reasons, naming rules, words, and definitions.

The symbol system is a sensory stimulus signal, and the five senses—sight, hearing, smell, taste, and touch—can all be used to build a brand's symbol system.

Let me give an example. Here is a quote from the original text about how Hu & Hua created the language/text system for Zu Li Jian Elderly Shoes.

Elderly shoes are not Zu Li Jian's brand "positioning," but rather its "naming." "Zu Li Jian Elderly Shoes" becomes a complete name, creating a new category. This is the language philosophy of Hu & Hua—words have more power than speech. Naming is cost, naming is investment, naming is a call. "Zu Li Jian Elder, Shoes" has a stronger call and more power than "Zu Li Jian is Elderly Shoes." It reduces the company's marketing and communication costs and becomes one of the most important strategies. The brand slogan for Zu Li Jian Elderly Shoes is: "Professional Elderly Shoes, Choose Zu Li Jian!" The speech system includes: "Elders should wear elderly shoes," which is a definition and judgment, taking on the responsibility of educating consumers. There's also "Mothers should wear mothers' shoes," "On New Year's, wear new shoes, give elders, Zu Li Jian," "First measure foot shape, then choose size," and so on. Thus, Zu Li Jian has formed the corporate mission of "Let every elder wear professional elderly shoes" and the corporate culture of "Caring for the elderly." Wholeheartedly serving the elderly has become Zu Li Jian's business theory.

In many cases, Hu & Hua's main approach to serving clients is to redesign menus, storefronts, shelves, and packaging, which has led to very significant results.

The essence of a brand is to reduce three costs for a company: social supervision costs, consumer choice costs, and corporate marketing costs.

Now, the key question is: How do we build a brand?

Many theories in marketing are rooted in psychology.

One line of thought comes from Freud to Jung, discussing collective unconsciousness, which is one line of art.

Another line is Pavlov, who discussed stimulus and reflex, which led to behaviorism, and then to big data.

Three approaches:

1) Parasitize the cultural mother body

Human life is a drama of continuous cycles. The cultural mother body is the part of human life that repeats itself, like eating mooncakes during the Mid-Autumn Festival, eating zongzi during the Dragon Boat Festival, and putting up couplets, setting off fireworks, and celebrating the Spring Festival. Every year, when these times come, people are automatically pulled in, irresistibly, inevitably, and repeatedly, which is collective unconsciousness.

Therefore, privatizing the cultural mother body's prototype role, modifying it, and sharing it with the public is a shortcut for building a brand. For example, we now know the white-bearded, red-hatted Santa Claus, which was created by Coca-Cola for marketing purposes, and it has become a cultural symbol that occupies consumers' thoughts and emotions without them realizing it.

2) Repeat annually

By parasitizing the cultural mother body, making the brand a part of the mother body, and then repeating it, the brand becomes a part of the mother body's culture. Why do Japanese people eat KFC on Christmas and Koreans eat fried chicken and beer on the first snow? It's all brand marketing. Only through repetition can people remember, and repetition can accumulate real brand assets offline, linking the brand with the festival.

Hu & Hua has helped many clients create festivals, such as the Valentine's Day wedding day of Snow King and Snow Girl, and the 99 Uncle Rice Festival of Lao Niang Jiu.

3) Accumulate brand assets

Having your own brand character means you have a brand asset that can be invested in long-term and accumulated. You should pursue a sense of scale and time span.

Some techniques in brand building:

1) Use rhetoric principles to write brand slogans or proverbs

The underlying logic of advertising slogans comes from rhetoric. What is rhetoric? Rhetoric is the art of persuading people to believe anything and to take action, which can also be understood as the art of persuading people to act, with the purpose of action.

Aristotle proposed four principles of rhetoric:

Principle 1: Common sense.

Principle 2: Simple words.

Principle 3: Use special sentence structures or rhyme.

Principle 4: Make people happy.

Let's analyze whether the brand slogan "Rice should bedone right, just eat Lao Niang Jiu" by Hu & Hua for Lao Niang Jiu fits these four principles.

2) Use proverbs or rhymes as brand slogans

Advertising is rhetoric, which is an action-oriented instruction.

People naturally trust oral expressions (such as proverbs or rhymes). Rhymes can bypass people's psychological defenses and directly reach their minds. It's an important concept to know what things people have psychological defenses against and what they don't.

3) Application of evolutionary psychology in advertising

In the case of Hu & Hua's Dream Lily Mattress, they chose blue as the brand color.

The popularity of blue is due to human evolution. When humans lived by hunting and gathering, the clear blue sky and water sources increased the chances of survival. Over time, this preference for blue may have evolved into a human innate preference.

In the world of symbols, everyone defines themselves through consumption of symbols and follows the commands of symbols. Among these symbols, those that refer most clearly, contain the most information, and have the strongest influence on human behavior are called super symbols.

Applying super symbols to branding can reduce a company's marketing costs and consumers' choice costs. If a company wants to turn its brand into a super symbol, it should start from the five senses—sight, hearing, smell, taste, and touch—and understand and meet consumers' needs at different stages.

Of course, around the concept of super symbols, many questions can arise, such as how to develop a strategy or design a product. The book *Super Symbol Is Super Creativity* provides detailed analysis and introduction.

In Hu Shanshan's view, if you can fully understand the concept of super symbols, you have read the book. Because in the world of symbols, everyone is a symbol, and the question we face is how to turn ourselves into a super symbol.

Can you design your own brand and slogan?

## Sharing Two: Strategies for Clear Thinking

Turn off the "speakers" in your mind that cause frustration

There is a saying that many people find heart-wrenching: "The adult world has no easy words."

When people reach a certain age, pressures from all sides come in waves, and life becomes like a compressed spring that has been living for a long time.

Long-term anxiety leads to chronic stress, which can cost us years of life.

This is not just scaremongering. Canadian researchers conducted an impressive experiment.

Researchers used nets to isolate the sparrows in a safe environment.

On one side, they played predator sounds through a speaker, while on the other, they played natural sounds.

The result was that the sparrows hearing predator sounds laid 40% fewer eggs, and the hatching rate was also low.

Adult sparrows, out of fear, did not forage, so their chicks starved to death.

The entire experiment proved that fear alone, without actual threats, can affect a person's life.

In many cases, these anxieties are often elements imagined by humans.

To be honest, daydreaming is quite popular in today's society: to avoid dealing with trouble, people often focus on abstract future imaginations.

In simple terms, bad thoughts in the mind always sound like a speaker shouting loudly.

Mark Twain once said in his later years: "I am old, have experienced many troubles, but most of the troubles I worried about did not happen."

In fact, most of the worries people imagine will not happen.

What are some practical ways to reduce anxiety?

First, set aside a fixed time to focus on your anxiety. Write down the things that worry you.

Second, focus on work, letting engaging and content-rich work shift your attention.

Perhaps, focusing on the present is the key to unlocking a happy life.

The father of Japanese management, Masaru Ibuka, was born into a not-so-wealthy family. He failed in his entrance exams for middle school, high school, and university, as well as his job application.

Later, after deep reflection, he came up with a "Life Result Equation":
Life's result = Thinking style × Passion × Ability

The quality of the thinking style accounts for one-third of the result.

The kind of life one has depends on how one thinks.

If you feel you are still far from happiness, it might be that you are walking on a path where your mind is lazy.

Charlie Munger believes that each discipline offers a unique perspective to understand the world, like blind men touching an elephant. To truly understand the world, one must master the core thinking models of multiple disciplines.

We each need a large number of different thinking models to develop extraordinary thinking abilities. Today, let's learn Charlie Munger's path to developing thinking.

The content of the book integrates contemporary psychology, the Stoic school, and the thinking models of investors, helping us observe the world objectively and maintain rational behavior over the long term.

As Nobel Prize winner Richard Feynman said: "If you know how to say a bird in all the languages of the world, you will know nothing about the bird itself. So, let's carefully observe this bird and see what it is doing. That's the most important thing. I understood the difference between knowing the name of something and understanding it when I was very young."

## Sharing Three: The Story of Reading

Let me tell you a story about a young man named Xiaochun. When he was a senior in college, he faced the choice of his graduation project. Among many topics, he chose a brand-new project this year. Although he had only studied structural design, he still wanted to complete the full design of an 18-story residential building.

The project was composed of four people, but it was difficult to find time to discuss and communicate with each other. Their professional knowledge was also lacking, and the hardest part was that others seemed more focused on finding jobs and preparing for postgraduate studies, while Xiaochun was fully immersed in the project.

Xiaochun started working in the lab at 7:00 AM and returned home at 11:00 PM. After a half-hour lunch break, he continued working on the design. To better communicate with his teammates, Xiaochun self-studied other professional knowledge, solved problems on his own, and sought help from his mentor and software developers when he couldn't. When his teammates encountered obstacles in their designs, Xiaochun actively shared and coordinated time to facilitate group discussions. He also encouraged his teammates and sought common goals.

After nearly three months, the project won first place in the National BIM Design Competition for Prefabricated Construction and was invited to share in front of all participating teams from universities. Xiaochun also successfully completed an excellent graduation project.

During this time, there was not only pressure from the design, but life also seemed to be not so smooth. Xiaochun had broken up with his girlfriend, who had been with him for over two years, and he felt down. After leaving the lab, he ran wildly on the playground, as if sweat was his remedy.

His grandfather's cancer had been treated with chemotherapy for a year, but his body gradually couldn't support it. After the New Year, during the last few months of his senior year, his grandfather passed away, which deeply affected Xiaochun. His emotions were hard to express, and sadness had become the norm. His overseas application was also hindered by language scores.

Under the pressure of these three events, Xiaochun still had to complete his graduation project. Thanks to the school's psychological counseling, he found a place to get a mental massage, and gradually he could share his experiences and problems.

His family and friends didn't know much about what he was going through, and Xiaochun experienced these alone. Psychological counseling and exercise gave him some relief, and the busyness of the graduation project also allowed him to immerse himself and temporarily forget other things.

After graduation, Xiaochun completed the design with excellent results. During this time, he deeply realized the importance of self-restraint and striving to be the best, giving himself the maximum growth.

Charlie Munger once said that he could have today by not chasing ordinary opportunities. Xiaochun believed in the power of this statement. During his graduation period, he didn't consider too many opportunities and focused his main energy on completing the graduation project, doing it to the best of his ability.

In the book, Maosu mentioned that we must restrain ourselves. Some opportunities that seem good are actually bad ones. We often say that we need depth in our field and breadth across disciplines, but most people lack the ability to achieve both, as we are not geniuses. So, should we choose depth or breadth? It's more likely that depth is more popular and has more opportunities.

Focus on your own field and strive to be the best, because only by being the best can you experience this feeling!

## Sharing Four: Be Willing to Be a Ladder for Others

Don't know how these eight characters feel to you? I felt an endless power, each word is powerful, and each word carries endless energy.

In *One Year is Worth Ten Years*, Maosu gave the ultimate secret to upgrading wealth and personal influence, which is these eight characters: "Be willing to be a ladder, those close to me will be rich."

"Be willing to be a ladder" means to treat yourself as a ladder, always helping others go up, and voluntarily making sacrifices to help others progress or achieve a certain cause. "Those close to me will be rich" refers to continuous sharing. If there are users, build successful cases, making those close to you better.

I want to talk about the story of "Be willing to be a ladder." A book published in 1950, *The Style of the Pine Tree*, written by Tao Yung, aimed to guide young people in the socialist era to care about politics and master scientific and technological knowledge, to have a broad chest like the sun and a firm character like the pine tree. The phrase "Be willing to be a ladder" comes from this book. I respect the pine tree for its self-sacrificing spirit more than its style.

In my childhood, I learned about a special plant in the northwest called the poplar tree, which we compared to the soldiers defending our borders. Their straight figures stood on both sides of the road, silently guarding us. At that time, I knew this was dedication, this was sacrifice.

My family elders said that suffering is a blessing, and we shouldn't worry about gains and losses. Since elementary school, I seemed to have a smooth path, though I need to put quotes around the term "official luck." Why have I always been a student leader? Was it because I was outstanding in both character and academics or had leadership qualities? I understood it as being the one left behind. But don't worry, I think being left behind is a good thing.

I have never been a smart child, but I just do things honestly. When the class needed someone to do something, I always participated. From opening the classroom door to being a life committee member, discipline committee member, and class representative, I eventually became the class president. I did everything I saw in the class, and I thought it was quite simple. The class is a big family, and helping the family members is nothing big.

In high school, I became the student union president, and in university, I held five positions in the party committee, Youth League Committee, and student organizations, making a little effort for all the students, and I enjoyed it. For students, positions are not symbols of power; I think they are trust in me, and I naturally try my best.

Student life passed quickly, and when I entered the workforce, I wanted to become the best teacher. In my first year, I achieved the best project performance, and a bigger idea came to me: I wanted to help more teachers, and I wanted to become a trainer. I started by wanting to teach everything to new teachers, gradually learning to systematically assist them. The number of students I could influence was limited, but every teacher I helped would benefit more students.

After leaving my job, I chose to pursue a master's degree and started creating content on the internet. In the community, I met many friends. When I was needed, I was always happy to share my experience. Sometimes, I would talk with my partners for one or two hours without realizing it. Honestly, helping others achieve something is more joyful than achieving it myself.

Maybe I can only help people around me become spiritually rich now, but I will continue to practice, carrying the thinking methods taught by Maosu, being down-to-earth, and taking one step at a time. Let those close to me not only multiply their wealth but also grow better!

Teaching is my life's luck. I am willing to be a ladder for others, rooted in the education industry, doing the career I take pride in for my whole life.

Thank you for your patience!
