# The Experience of a Stiff Neck

> Published: 2021-09-25 · [中文](../zh/2021-09-25-%E8%90%BD%E6%9E%95%E7%9A%84%E4%BD%93%E9%AA%8C.md) | [English](../en/2021-09-25-the-experience-of-a-stiff-neck.md)

---

Have you ever had a stiff neck? If you have, then I want to first congratulate you! It's said that young and middle-aged people are more prone to this.

When I woke up in the morning, my neck felt as if it were locked in place, like I was wearing a cervical collar.

It wasn't until later that I realized I had a stiff neck. The whole day was basically spent resting, because I comforted myself by telling myself that if you're injured, you should rest well.

Having a stiff neck became a source of joy for the whole family. After all, when a familiar person turns into this, I myself couldn't even look in the mirror for fear of laughing too hard. The result was that my neck hurt even more.

My mom tried various methods to help me relieve the pain. After a series of screams, I felt a little better and was about to brag about it, only to return to my original state.

In reality, a stiff neck needs to be eased gradually, with some methods helping the recovery. The most important thing, perhaps, is the mindset.

The feeling of a stiff neck is like a restrictive shackle. You move a little, and it restricts you again. I always wanted to recover as soon as possible, but I just couldn't. What's even more infuriating is that it's always there, making me feel restless and irritable.

Such a small issue can already cause emotional fluctuations. What if it were a real shackle? You might know who it is.

Today, the whole internet is talking about one event: Ms. Meng Wanzhou's return to China. Perhaps before 2018, I didn't know her well. But in December of that year, she was wrongfully detained in Canada, and the news spread quickly across the country.

Today, I made a short video, recording a conversation between me and my younger brother, telling him the story of Ms. Meng. As I was speaking, tears came down my face. How did she endure such hardships?

Ms. Meng had limited time with her family. When she made the firm decision to go to the front lines, she began living in the grassroots. She believed that the starting point of life wasn't important; what mattered was where you were heading.

Maintaining an extremely open mindset and actively seeking change, in the face of this political drama, Ms. Meng showed remarkable resilience. Her confidence, dignity, and backbone have been shining through these three years of restrictions.

An electronic ankle bracelet was on her left ankle, and from the photos over the years, you could see it clearly. When it was removed today, the deep marks left on it left me deeply moved.

Today, we watched the live broadcast together, witnessing the moment when Ms. Meng stepped off the plane and returned home. She's really back home now!

On September 25th, Ms. Meng Wanzhou returned to China. Welcome home!
