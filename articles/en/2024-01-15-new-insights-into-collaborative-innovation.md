**Paper Title:**
Remote collaboration fuses fewer breakthrough ideas

**Paper URL:**
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06767-1

**Authors:** Lin Yiling, Carl Benedikt Frey & Wu Lingfei

The number of scientists and inventors, as well as the output of innovation, has been increasing, yet the challenges they face are also growing.

On December 29, 2023, a paper published in *Nature* revealed new insights into collaborative innovation. A study conducted an in-depth analysis of research papers and patent applications over the past half-century, finding differences and connections in knowledge production between remote and on-site teams.

Although remote collaboration has led to more scientific output, it is harder for remote teams to produce breakthrough discoveries compared to scientists who can communicate face-to-face.

The popularity of remote collaboration brings more scientific output

With technological development, remote collaboration has become widespread across various fields. Scientists use the internet and video conferencing tools to transcend time and space constraints, collaborating on research and innovation.

This global collaboration model has greatly expanded the possibilities of cooperation, enabling scientists to share resources and knowledge, and learn from each other's experiences.

The study shows that the number of remote teams and scientific output has been rising sharply. This undoubtedly brings more opportunities and development space to the scientific community.

Remote collaboration faces challenges in producing breakthrough discoveries

However, the study also uncovered limitations of the remote collaboration model. Compared to face-to-face collaboration among scientists, remote teams find it harder to produce breakthrough discoveries. This is due to certain limitations in information transmission and sharing.

Although scientists can communicate via email and online meetings, these methods often cannot replace in-person interaction.

Face-to-face communication allows for better transmission of ideas and perspectives, fostering in-depth discussions and inspirational thinking. This is crucial for innovation and breakthrough discoveries.

Emphasizing Effective Funding and the Choice of Collaboration Models

This study not only highlights the differences between remote and on-site teams but also provides important insights into how to conduct scientific collaboration and how to effectively fund research projects. Science funders and research institutions should focus on providing good communication and collaboration platforms, offering scientists opportunities for face-to-face interaction and collaboration.

At the same time, scientists should also weigh the pros and cons when choosing a collaboration model, striving to select more effective methods. Only by combining remote collaboration with face-to-face interaction can innovation and breakthrough discoveries be truly promoted.

There are differences and connections between remote and on-site teams in scientific innovation. Although remote collaboration brings more scientific output, it still faces challenges in producing breakthrough discoveries.

The scientific community and funding institutions should pay attention to creating a good collaborative environment. Scientists should also choose collaboration models that suit their projects. Only by fully leveraging the strengths of remote collaboration and face-to-face interaction can the scientific community continuously innovate and advance knowledge.

As technology advances rapidly, the remote work model is becoming increasingly popular. However, from an innovation perspective, this model is not always the best choice, especially for teams like the one at Zhujiazhujin, which rely heavily on creativity and flexibility.

From the perspective of Zhujiazhujin, team cohesion is a crucial factor. A high-efficiency, seamless-collaboration team is the prerequisite and guarantee for innovation. In a remote work setting, it is difficult to deepen understanding and trust among team members through face-to-face interaction, team activities, or a shared living environment, making the establishment of team cohesion even more challenging.

In innovative teams, information sharing and the stimulation of creativity are very important. Poor communication and information asymmetry can affect trust and understanding among team members, slowing down the innovation process. The limitations of the remote work model restrict face-to-face interaction and engagement, potentially leading to a lack of creativity and communication issues, which can affect the efficiency and quality of innovation.

To ensure smooth remote collaboration and maintain the smooth operation of the lab, the Zhujiazhujin team needs relatively dense personnel and smooth collaboration. The remote work model imposes higher requirements on the management of team members in terms of time and space, including coordinating work schedules, reasonably allocating workloads, and monitoring team members' progress. This places higher demands on the capabilities and energy of team managers.

From the case of the Zhujiazhujin team, the remote work model is not always the best choice in all situations.

Issues such as team cohesion, the stimulation of creativity, and communication, as well as time and space management, need to be carefully considered. Although remote work has its unique advantages and applicable scenarios, for teams focused on innovation, face-to-face interaction and collaboration remain indispensable.

The application of remote work should be based on the characteristics and actual needs of the team, with attention to details and reasonable planning and organization.
