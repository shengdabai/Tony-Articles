Tony Sheng's Owned Audience Notes

Note 15

01 You look at that someone, who's live-streaming and selling products every day, how impressive! That someone has so many followers, all are gold V accounts, and many people are following them...

02 Are these voices necessarily connected to your decision to start a video channel? Have you clearly thought through why you want to do a video channel?

03 A friend said, due to the impact of the pandemic and economic development, not transitioning online makes it hard to do business. Sometimes, you might have zero income in a day, and it's really stressful!

04 Reality is harsh, and so is illusion. Just because everyone else is doing it doesn't mean you can do it too. Think clearly about why you're doing it, and what the value and meaning behind it are. Only then can you do it more sustainably.

05 If you haven't thought it through clearly, I recommend you watch at least 100 videos.

06 After watching and analyzing a lot, you'll have some insights and gains. Because you'll know what's good and what's better.

07 If you're willing, feel free to consult with me. It might be faster and more valuable than you doing it yourself.
