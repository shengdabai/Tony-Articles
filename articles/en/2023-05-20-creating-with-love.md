Ignoring the simple, seeking secrets, and looking for shortcuts — these are some of the reasons we are getting further away from a better version of ourselves.

Many people are confused about whether to do vertical content or build a personal brand on video accounts. In fact, thinking deeper, it's all about building a personal brand, letting users fall in love with you.

A big influencer on Douyin may not have an advantage on video accounts, and a Bilibili content creator may have very low views on video accounts. Different scenarios have different user groups, so finding the right platform to build your personal brand is crucial.

It shines here, it shines there — try more, build your brand in the right environment, and you'll be able to do it longer and better.

Likes and dislikes come from the same cause. Someone loves you, and someone else will hate you. The sooner you understand this, the better you can connect with the users who love you.

It's hard to please everyone, even though we all know this. In practice, we still expect everyone to pay attention. Create niche products, find your precise audience, and then try to gain traction in the mass market.

Stereotypes — the biggest burden for female hosts is their beauty. If she wants to attract high-end white-collar women to buy products, do you think they will be attracted?

Discuss in specific contexts, in the right platform, be yourself genuinely.

Live well on your own, love yourself well.

The best punishment for a bad person is to ignore them, let them keep doing what they do, and the biggest damage will be to themselves.

Why did I suddenly talk about this point? It's inspired by Teacher Li Xiaolai. Facing various criticisms and smear campaigns, he told me to live well on my own. Exactly, who is responsible for my life, who will bear the consequences?

As we grow and develop, we will become better, but have you ever thought about the troubles and problems that come with it? After all, we are not money, and people don't love money — obviously, there's no need for such things.

Since we are destined to meet bad people and bad things, why should we be so angry, so passionate, so united in anger… using so many words, yet not clearly conveying the various emotions that appear at that moment. Is it worth it? To argue, to debate, to seek recognition — just let them do as they please.

On the path of self-media, I've also occasionally heard these voices, some insider information, some dissatisfaction, some smear campaigns. When I encounter unpleasant things, I feel sympathy. But what can we do?

Never let yourself be consumed by them. Let them do what they want as they think, and eventually, they will get the feedback they deserve. As for us, just do what we need to do, because time is precious, and you are more important than them.

I've seen it, heard it, and experienced it. All along, we should keep it simple, keep it humble, do what we want, and keep going.

Li Xiaoyi's content creation heart method: find out what your users love, fear, and idealize.

In short, it's about understanding people, paying attention to people, not user personas, not a bunch of traffic numbers, and certainly not the money they can bring.

What is love? Everyone has their own understanding. I think it's a great driving force on our journey. When we have love in our hearts, we live with effort, and gradually, we will meet people who love us.
