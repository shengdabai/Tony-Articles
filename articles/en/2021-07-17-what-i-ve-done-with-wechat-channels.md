I see the WeChat Channels as a dynamic social business card, breaking free from the constraints of traditional text-based business cards. It allows for a multi-dimensional presentation and display of oneself. If we draw a comparison, it's more like Weibo, as it supports text, images, short videos, and even medium-length videos. Its flexibility is stronger, and it connects users more conveniently!

My WeChat Channels is positioned around language education. I don't plan to focus on specific English subjects like teaching vocabulary, grammar, or exam techniques. Instead, I hope to share my understanding of language education to help more people appreciate language, fall in love with English, and be willing to use it in daily life. Using it is the most efficient method—reading an English original book can significantly improve one's English. In most cases, people don't have a real need to use English, so I hope to use the WeChat Channels to help more people discover the charm of language and turn English into a second language rather than just a foreign language.

So far, I haven't made any money through the WeChat Channels. My plan is to collect users' language learning questions, build trust through video content, and share my insights on language education. In the future, I will create my own community for companion-style growth, making the use of English a tangible outcome. Currently, I have set up two free small classes to improve my teaching skills and conduct a small-scale experiment.

My WeChat Channels name may not be very focused, but I hope to build a warm and approachable image through the name "Tony Sheng," serving as a learning companion. The name comes from my English name Tony and the classic cartoon character Baymax, combining into TonyMax. However, I found that someone else already has this name, so I added my surname, resulting in "Tony Sheng." I hope this name will leave a memorable impression on users. My WeChat Channels name is: TonyMax Shengdabai!

Why should you follow me? I want to share my ideas through my favorite English teaching platform, and connect with a fun, eager-to-learn English companion. I am TonyMax Shengdabai!

A little thought: WeChat Channels operation is a long-term endeavor. We need to keep trying, keep listening, and find methods that suit us. However, we don't need to put too much pressure on ourselves. First, create content, refine it, and combine it with operation. If the content isn't good, the operation won't last. Also, I and some partners have discussed that it's hard to find accounts that match our content. This actually shows that we have a lot of potential. Sometimes, it's not necessary to stick strictly to a vertical field. We can learn from top streamers in other areas. People need resonance and fun, and content should be both informative and engaging. A mix of soft and hard approaches, learning together, and growing continuously.

The material library I've compiled is all in English. My current plan is to increase users' interest in language learning and encourage them to use English more frequently. For most people, there's no real need for English, or they haven't found enough motivation to use it. I hope to create an environment where people can start using English on their own, rather than just studying it or taking classes. I'm trying to learn English without formal classes, focusing more on reading English books, writing in English, and speaking English. Through my understanding, I want to help more people use English.

I have more plans and thoughts for my WeChat Channels in the future. Maybe I don't like the content of the top English streamers, but if a video spreads quickly, there must be something good about it. I need to carefully analyze and reflect on the videos, find better ways to help me keep expanding. The key to this door is definitely not in your hands or on the door itself. Perhaps we need to look elsewhere, gain more diverse perspectives, and learn from content from different fields. This is something I'm most excited about now. Every day's creation makes me very happy. Keep going, let's start this WeChat Channels journey!
