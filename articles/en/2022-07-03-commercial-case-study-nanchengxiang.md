Nanchengxiang: Playing by the Rules

001 Leverage the Masses

Avoiding bustling shopping malls, Nanchengxiang enters residential communities to serve neighbors. Word of mouth spreads, and if neighbors often visit, we tend to go too.

002 Predict User Preferences

Combining wet and dry dishes (dumplings, rice bowls), with grilled skewers and main courses, it caters to any time of day. New products keep coming, and you might feel like eating just to try something new.

003 Active Promises Build Motivation

To customers, we promise quality, so you can eat with confidence. To employees, we offer growth incentives—hard work leads to opportunity. Making active promises helps occupy the mind.

004 Skilfully Use Loss Aversion

When persuading users to buy, offering multiple options makes the purchase feel natural and stress-free.

005 Keep Users Loyal

24-hour community dining means you can always find a warm, home-like meal. A community shop is a great place to drop by and chat casually.

006 Extreme Focus

Using scissors to cut vegetables, strictly controlling the number of refrigerators, and using induction cookers to cook dumplings—every detail is carefully managed. Leftovers are minimal, costs are limited, and this achieves five times the average revenue of the catering industry.

007 Accurate Pricing

Through in-depth market research, pricing is precise, so users don’t hesitate. Eating well doesn’t mean paying a lot. Occasional discounts and affordable prices have made it popular among the people.

0.08 Managing Mistakes

The founder has operated in the catering industry for years, understanding product evolution and having made many mistakes. From these, they’ve identified enduring consumer needs, such as freshly grilled lamb skewers.

009 Strong Risk Resistance

During the pandemic crisis, they controlled risks and reduced losses. United as one, they even expanded their stores during the pandemic, going against the current.

010 Focus on Details

From street-side stalls to franchised chains and then to self-operated stores, they established a food distribution center, building one store at a time. They focus only on the Beijing market, offering full-time community dining. Small things reveal big potential.
