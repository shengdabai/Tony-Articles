I used this image again, hoping it might offer you some inspiration.

The phrase "The Cost of Ignorance" was mentioned by a Harvard president during a speech, and the audience was deeply moved by it. The more I think about it, the more I agree.

I used to think that the cost of ignorance was simply not knowing a few things. There are so many people who don’t know things, and they still manage to live fine, right? Plus, it’s impossible for anyone to know everything.

But do you know what’s really scary? When I was ignorant, I stopped thinking for myself and just listened to all the voices around me. Guess what happened?

I became anxious!

The cost of ignorance is not realizing that I can’t think independently. Without thinking, I become a mindless follower, and anxiety is just around the corner!

Don’t think about it anymore—just go read!

This is for myself, and for you too. Read, read, read well!

The key to solving problems is to make yourself better!
