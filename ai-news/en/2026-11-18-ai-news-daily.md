# AI News Digest | November 18, 2026

> 发布日期:2026-11-18 · 类型:AI 热点日报

---

## 1. OpenAI releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol

OpenAI has introduced GPT-6.2 Astra for complex professional work and GPT-6.2 Sol for everyday tasks. The company says both models can produce better results using fewer output tokens. The release gives users two models with different intended roles, from demanding projects to routine work.

**Why it matters:** Model selection can now start with the difficulty and value of a task. Fewer output tokens may also affect costs, but the full cost of a job depends on the work required and the quality of the result. Teams can compare both models on representative tasks before choosing a default. [Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/)

## 2. Gemini tasks become free in six countries

Google says it is rolling out Gemini tasks, powered by Gemini 4 Hydrogen, to free users in the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia and India. The feature can help with actions such as calling to make appointments, checking bills, shopping and managing schedules.

**Why it matters:** The feature extends an AI assistant into activities that can have consequences outside a conversation. For users, the useful question is how well a task is completed within the permission they intended to give. Shopping, for example, makes the scope of an instruction and the resulting purchase especially relevant to check. [Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/tasks-in-gemini-free-new-features/)

## 3. Microsoft introduces a speech-to-speech model

Microsoft AI has announced MAI Realtime 1, its first speech-to-speech model, with pricing starting at $0.01 per minute of audio. Microsoft claims that the model exceeds GPT Realtime 2.1 on quality, speed and price in its Big Bench Audio and τ-Voice comparisons.

**Why it matters:** The published starting price gives developers a point of reference when estimating the cost of a voice application. The benchmark comparison is a Microsoft claim, so a buyer should test the model in the languages and situations that matter to the application. A low starting price alone does not establish the cost or quality of a complete service. [Source](https://microsoft.ai/news/introducing-mai-realtime-1/)

## 4. Replit Missions assigns work to groups of agents

Replit has introduced Missions, a product in which users specify a goal, a budget and boundaries for a task. Groups of agents then plan, build, test and iterate in parallel. Replit says a separate AI judge checks the outcome against agreed criteria.

**Why it matters:** Missions puts the budget and acceptance criteria alongside the goal when defining an automated task. That could help users express what a useful result should look like before work starts. The result still depends on the clarity of those criteria: an automated review can only assess the work against the standards it has been given. [Source](https://replit.com/blog/introducing-replit-missions)

## 5. Security firm reports malicious Python package versions

Security company Wiz alleges that versions 1.26.1 and 1.26.2 of Microsoft's agent-framework package on PyPI contained a credential stealer. According to its report, an inserted file ran the stealer when Python started and collected material including cloud and continuous-integration credentials. Wiz says the versions were removed.

**Why it matters:** If confirmed, the incident illustrates how a software dependency can expose credentials in a development environment. Teams using the package can check which versions were installed and review relevant credential activity. The description here is based on Wiz's report; the allegations have not been independently confirmed in this digest. [Source](https://www.wiz.io/blog/malicious-version-of-microsofts-agent-framework-published-to-pypi)

## 6. Kling Image 4.0 adds an API

Kling AI has released Kling Image 4.0 and opened its API to enterprise customers and developers. The product announcement says the model supports native 8K image generation, more than 110 preset styles and more than 70 commercial fonts. It also describes generation of coordinated image sets and long-format images.

**Why it matters:** The release offers another option for teams producing product displays and other visual material that needs multiple images. The announced features describe what the tool is intended to support; users still need to assess text accuracy, visual consistency and cost against the needs of a particular project. [Source](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/p3_DIBRUUgmxShjD5Wp27w)

## 7. Suno Visuals generates imagery for songs

Suno has launched Visuals, a tool that creates images and videos of up to 15 seconds from songs in a user's library. Its formats include lyric videos, album covers and video clips derived from photos. Users can edit or extend results and revise them with text prompts.

**Why it matters:** Musicians have a new way to develop short visual material around music they already have. The editing and prompting features allow further iteration after an initial result. Creators still need to decide whether the generated material fits the song and the audience they want to reach. [Source](https://suno.com/blog/introducing-visuals)
