# AI News Digest: Search Revenue, Infrastructure Spending, and Product Changes
> 发布日期:2026-10-30 · 类型:AI 热点日报

---

1. **AI search raises a revenue question.** Google's third-quarter search revenue grew by roughly 12%, while a report says AI Mode queries earn less than conventional search queries. The company also described strong growth in search use. Those two developments make the business question sharper: increased activity does not automatically produce the same revenue per query. The reported gap matters to publishers and advertisers as well as Google, because a shift in how people find information can change where visits and advertising value go. The figures and management comments describe current performance; they do not establish how quickly AI search revenue might improve. [source](https://the-decoder.com/googles-ai-search-problem-finally-shows-up-in-the-earnings-report-some-investors-believe/)

2. **Reddit plans to test an experience centered on logged-in users.** Reddit said search referrals have fallen and announced that it would first test the change outside the United States. It has given no timetable for a global rollout. The move matters because platforms that host public discussions may reassess access when search traffic changes and AI systems use their content. Requiring sign-in could affect how easily occasional visitors find discussions, while giving Reddit more control over access and advertising. The test, rather than a completed worldwide change, is the relevant development for now. [source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/as-search-traffic-dries-up-reddit-announces-its-shifting-to-a-logged-in-experience/)

3. **Amazon sets out a large AWS investment plan.** Amazon expects about $220 billion in cash capital expenditure in 2026, with most of it directed toward AWS. It also described ambitious expectations for AWS revenue over the coming years. Those expectations are company forecasts, not realized results. The spending plan matters because demand for AI computing can require major commitments before the corresponding revenue arrives. Customers and competitors can watch whether additional capacity brings faster access and useful services, while investors can compare future returns with the scale of the spending. [source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/aws-will-be-a-1t-business-jassy-says-but-first-it-will-spend-big/)

4. **Figma explains how it trained a model for canvas editing.** Figma says it began with more than 250,000 synthetic training examples and later used reinforcement learning alongside rule-based checks and visual evaluation. The work targets editing inside Figma, where a useful result must change the intended elements while preserving the design's other parts. This matters as software companies build AI features around specific workflows: success depends on whether the model can make reliable edits in the product, not just produce plausible text about a design. The training account and performance comparisons come from Figma and should be treated as vendor claims. [source](https://www.figma.com/blog/training-a-figma-fluent-editing-model/)

5. **OpenAI reorganizes its public Model Spec.** The updated guidance groups expected model behavior around five values: following human intent, reducing harm, honesty and transparency, supporting human autonomy, and care. It also adds guidance on understanding context, oversight, and autonomy, while changing some defaults. The document matters to developers who need to know what behavior the provider intends when instructions or goals conflict. A published specification describes that intent; it does not, by itself, prove that every response in every product will follow it. Teams using the models can compare the guidance with behavior in their own tasks. [source](https://openai.com/index/updated-model-spec)

6. **Gemini adds direct work with common files.** Google says users can generate, preview, and edit documents, PDFs, presentations, and spreadsheets within Gemini. Its announcement also describes selecting part of a file for a further change. The practical value lies in the editing step: a user can inspect an output and request a specific adjustment without starting the whole task again. This could make routine preparation of study materials or presentations easier, although the announcement alone cannot show how well the tools handle complex files. Availability and results should be checked in the product. [source](https://x.com/GeminiApp/status/2115845164824711247)

7. **Wiz examines the use of AI agents in attacks.** Drawing on its incident response work and publicly disclosed cases, Wiz describes patterns it associates with agents taking part in intrusions. The report matters to security teams because it offers leads for investigating how automated tools may be used during an attack. Its descriptions of third-party incidents and any associated allegations remain the investigator's account; the feed does not independently establish every claim. Readers can use the patterns as questions for their own monitoring and incident review without treating the reported cases as universal evidence of how all attackers operate. [source](https://www.wiz.io/blog/the-rise-of-agentic-attacks-what-they-look-like-and-where-theyre-headed)
